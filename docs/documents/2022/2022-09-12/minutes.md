##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $- Downtown

DDA and Economic Development Director Mack stated at the last DDA meeting they discussed having a winter Farmers Market. The market master currently does the same for Holly and it is successful. He stated it would be every other weekend, possibly at the VFW Hall. He then stated Lefty's is having a ribbon cutting this week, corporate has taken over that franchise. He stated they are continuing to discuss the Holiday Spectacular, and they have 8 sponsors so far. He further stated he has started the RRC self-assessment and after that is completed, the MEDC will create a base line report to show where we are in relation to the best practices of the program. Mr. Mack stated they are continuing to work on the RCA building, and they have some of their final inspections shortly. He then stated the deadline for the building to be completed is September 30th. Councilmember Dilg stated our market manager is great and she likes the idea of having a winter market. Mr. Mack stated we have 20 vendors that are interested as of now. Councilmember Hansen asked about the theater. Mr. Mack stated he has spoken with the person that is assisting the owners look for someone to lease or buy the building. They would like someone to run it as a theatre. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked about the extension cords hanging downtown. There is a reason why they are there, but we need to start one block at a time, and get some outlets, and move things around. It should be an easy fix, add an outlet at the bottom of the tree, and wrap the cords around the trees. Mr. Mack stated the DPW have gone out and moved some of them. Councilmember Kennedy asked if he met the new owners of Lefty's, and if he met with them about reminding them to follow our ordinances. Mr. Mack stated he had not met them yet, but will be attending the ribbon cutting and will speak with them. Councilmember Kennedy then discussed the new business in town in front of 12 Kitchen Elves. Mr. Mack stated the owner of 12 Kitchen Elves is also running a bridal salon. Councilmember Kennedy then asked how Mr. Mack will remind businesses of Pumpkinfest and what events will be in front of their businesses. Mr. Mack stated he plans on emailing the businesses with a reminder. Councilmember Kennedy asked if he will be reminding the businesses and residents of Motorfest. Mr. Mack stated he will send out an email to the business owners. Discussion was held in regards to informing residents/businesses of the annual events. Councilmember Kivell stated to start a project this late in the year to get new lights up and outlets downtown would be difficult, there will have to be engineering, trenching will need to be done, that should have been brought up while we were working on the budget. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated the issue is to resolve the problem. She further stated it isn't that complicated, and it seems to be another excuse why South Lyon still has this problem when other communities have figured it out. She further stated she just wants to make sure someone is working on it because the cords are unsightly. She then thanked Mr. Mack and said he is doing a great job. Councilmember Dilg stated she doesn't feel it is the DDA Director's responsibility to knock on residents' doors, and she is sure the Police Department will handle that as they have in the past.

## MERS-Presentation

Sue Feinberg of MERS gave a presentation regarding the employee's retirement programs. She discussed the annual evaluation which projects the employer's contribution. She further stated the current funded status is 70%. She explained the primary reason is we had a good market and the return was over 14%. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if the gains made in a year is 14%, why can't the portion we receive be used to reduce the unfunded liability. Ms. Feinberg stated the gains are still being used in the city's fund, but by using a more conservative rate of return assumption it doesn't impact it as much. Discussion was held regarding the assumptions of the actuaries use such as inflation which is 2.5 or 3%. Further discussion was held regarding unfunded liability. The city is expected to be fully funded within 16 years if only paying the minimum amount. They always encourage people to fund more if possible. Ms. Feinberg stated the city is currently contributing $786,588.00 and that will be going down, and next year it will be $50,000.00 less. For projecting for budget purposes, we are looking at the assumption of 7.00% rate of return. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if she had heard any discussion about the government assisting cities for unfunded liability, because they are doing that now with other communities that are over 60% unfunded liability. Ms. Feinberg stated they have not heard any discussions. Ms. Feinberg explained a graph showing the history of the valuations from 2011-2021. She stated the city made over 2.2 million dollars that went into the plan. She stated the city's contribution was $674,370 while the employee contributions were $49,336. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated it is important to realize the city employees' contributions are much smaller than the city's contribution. She hopes the employees of the city understand the sacrifices being made by the residents of this city to fund this retirement program and she is sure they are. Ms. Feinberg stated the employee contributions are very low compared to other communities.

He then asked about replacing the tree by the post office. City Manager Zelenak stated there are several to be replaced. He and the DDA Director have been discussing different types of trees for the downtown. We will also be gaining a couple of trees at the expense of the RCA building for trees that were removed for the work done at the building. There are several we will be replacing. She then stated there are many window companies such as Pella, any local window companies could get them done immediately. She then stated the landscaping on the outside is just irresponsible ownership, you can have a landscaper come over and pull weeds and it is an embarrassment to the property owner.
