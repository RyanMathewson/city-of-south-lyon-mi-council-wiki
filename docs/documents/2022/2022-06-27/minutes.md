## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Lieutenant Noechel stated as of today they have had 527 calls for service, which is up from last year. He stated recently they gave mutual aid to a residential structure fire with Green Oak Township, and the wind storm caused multiple power lines down and trees across the roadways. He further stated they did some hose management training for staff and they also did some fire truck evolutions.
