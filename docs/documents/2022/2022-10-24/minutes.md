2. First Reading of Ordinance to amend the South Lyon Code of Ordinances, Chapter 34, Article II Downtown Development Authority Ordinance

City Manager Zelenak explained the T1F public act 57 of 2018 provides that the Chief Executive Officer shall make appointments to fill vacancies on the DDA. MCL 125-4304(1)(f). The DDA bylaws provide that the Chief Executive Officer of the city shall make appointments to fill vacancies on the DDA. Section 4.2 of the City Charter provides that the mayor is the Chief Executive Officer. The DDA ordinance Chapter 32, Article II, defines the City Manager to make appointments to fill vacancies on the DDA. This amendment reconciles the DDA Ordinance with the (act)and updates references to the Act. CM 10-5-22 MOTION TO APPROVE FIRST READING

### 3. Discussion of potential uses for ARPA funds

City Manager Zelenak explained that the city will be receiving funds as part of the American Rescue Act. He stated he has met with department heads to see if there are potential uses for these funds within each department. The city still hasn't received definitive uses of the funds, but a discussion is necessary. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated she has been able to find some information on what other communities have used the funds for. Infrastructure, safe drinking water and additional pay for first responders have been approved. Councilmember Dilg stated she spoke with Elissa Slotkin and she promised we would receive a phone call with additional information on what the funds can be used for. Councilmember Kivell asked about the mandated reflective street signs. City Manager Zelenak stated we currently have those, but he will check into it. Councilmember Kivell stated there are a lot of places where you can't really see the signs and they aren't as noticeable. Councilmember Kennedy asked about fixing the bridge by Oak Creek as well as 2 other walking bridges throughout the trails. Ms. Tiernan stated we are classified as non-entitlement and we know infrastructure is allowed. Councilmember

Hansen asked about the city getting Wi-Fi in our parks for the public. Ms. Tiernan stated you would have to have the funds to continue the program after the funds are spent. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked the city manager what his opinion is on the rapid transit tax and how it will affect our residents. City Manager Zelenak stated they are proposing a .95 mill on all Oakland County taxpayers. It will generate 65 million dollars the first year. We utilize Peoples Express for our residents. He stated our County Commissioner voted no on the ballot proposal language because he doesn't think it benefits the communities he represents. They have not given anyone much information and they don't have a back-up plan if it doesn't pass and the original smart mill will not be renewed. This mill is for everyone in Oakland County. He stated if passed, they will receive $450,000 from South Lyon. There is not enough detail to even show how the money will be used. Mass transit is a good thing but he doesn't see any benefit to the city or its residents. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated our seniors are very worried about it. That will be a very large tax increase for 10 years and they don't allow communities to opt out. The service run will be out of Pontiac. She stated this will be devastating. She further stated she heard some of the money will be going to Detroit. Councilmember Kivell stated the historical obstacle had always been Farmington because they wouldn't allow SMART to end up working in their jurisdiction, so everyone on the west side thought they were safe from having not having people from other communities getting access to be able to come into their community on public transportation. Obviously, the people at Oakland County think that's a step in the right direction. He would like the city to entertain the idea of entertaining what we could end up getting from it by exhaustively trying to expand that rather than saying we're going to end up pitching in that money and not get anything. City Manager Zelenak stated they have talked about expanding that, but they have not defined this area as an area of expansion. They've been looking at the other area of the county. It is very hard for a voter to show them the benefit is to our community when we don't know what it is going to be yet. It is very disappointing that they are telling people to vote yes, when they can't show what the benefit will be. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated it is her understanding there isn't a board in place to oversee it. So, they'll be collecting money for 3 years and just let it sit in their bank. There is no plan. She further stated this is tough. She feels bad for Lyon Township, they are looking at several tax increases for the library, Fire and now possibly the transit tax. City Manager Zelenak stated that $450,000 could really enhance our city instead of giving it to the county.
