### 3. Consider CDBG application projects for the City of South Lyon

City Manager Zelenak stated the 2023 CDBG application deadline is December 1st. On November 13th at the regularly scheduled Council meeting the city held a public hearing relating to the CDBG funds allocated to the City of South Lyon. The city's tentative funding allocation for 2024 is $28,356. The allocation for public service projects is limited to $4,253 (15%) of the total allocation. Even though we received requests for more than one public service entity, we can only fund one of them based upon the minimum and maximum limitations set forth in the CDBG requirements.
