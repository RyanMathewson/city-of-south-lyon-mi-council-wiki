## Rules of Conduct for Public Comment at Council Meetings $ ^{*} $

Members of the public may speak at a Council meeting upon recognition by the Mayor. Public comment may only occur during periods designated on the agenda for public comment or a public hearing. A person may speak for up to two (2) minutes during each of the two public comment periods on agenda items or non-agenda items. Walyers of the time requirement may only be granted in the discretion of the Mayor, and Walters to speak at a time other than a designated comment period may only be granted by the Council. Any person wishing to make a presentation longer than two minutes or requiring audio-visual equipment is asked to contact the City Clerk requesting to appear on a future agenda.

A person may only address Council from the podium. Only one person may occupy the podium at a time. All remarks are to be directed to the Mayor and Council. Speakers are not to engage in direct dialog with other meeting attendees.

Any person who violates the Rules of Conduct, disturbs the peace at the meeting, and/or interferes with the meeting may be warned, ordered to be seated, removed, and/or ticketed.

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. 2023-2024 City Budget

City Manager Zelenak stated Council was presented with the draft 2023-2024 budget this evening and this budget was prepared and based upon City Council recommendations, staff input and review of needs of departments and the community. The preliminary numbers are reflective of expected revenues based upon taxable values provided by Oakland County Equalization and proposed millages. He then reminded Council the budget work session will be Wednesday April 5th at 6pm. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if the bonds have gone to sale yet. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan explained it was completed in February and the funding is sitting in the bank account we set up specifically for that. She stated we have utilized some expenditures for the bond sale, the finance consultant, our bond lawyers, and some fees we had to pay to process the paperwork. She further stated Huntington Securities purchased the bonds, and she just received the schedule showing the payment and interest, which will be provided at the budget meeting on the 5th. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked the total revenue and what the interest rate was. Ms. Tiernan stated we received 8.8 million and she doesn't recall the interest rate, but will give Council that information at the budget meeting. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she was looking at the City Charter in regards to the banking issue that occurred a week ago. She was wondering if the language would allow us to use a more financially backed bank, and not just the local banks. She stated we may need to look at the Charter to be able to move money if there is trouble. She stated if you follow the trends in banking, and you wonder if some of the local banks will be in business in 5-8 years. Some are going to virtual banking only. It isn't an emergency right now, but we have to watch the trend. Ms. Tiernan stated she and Clerk Deaton have been working on looking at all of our banking options even before the banking crisis hit the news. We are looking at more diversification with our accounts. Council did approve a new investment policy a few years ago, and we are looking at Oakland County's Investment Program. She further stated we are trying to stay ahead of the game.

### 2. Opioid Settlement Resolution

After several years of negotiation, nationwide settlements relating to the distribution and sale of opioids, have been reached against two national pharmacies, CVS and Walmart, and against two pharmaceutical companies, Teva and AbbVie/Allergan. The two settlements are in addition to the settlements previously approved in 2021 with McKesson, Cardinal Health and AmerisourceBergen and Jansen Pharmaceuticals, INC and its parent company Johnson and Johnson. The settlements require companies to pay up to $16.5 billion dollars over 13 years. The city is eligible to participate with the settlements. Councilmember Dilg asked if there is any reason to not join. City Manager Zelenak stated we have discussed this and all the municipalities have not found a reason to not join. We just have to wait to see how it must be spent. CM 3-4-23 MOTION TO APPROVE RESOLUTION
