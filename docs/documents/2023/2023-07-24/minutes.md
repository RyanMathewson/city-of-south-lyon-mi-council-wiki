### 3. Bid award Sidewalk Replacement project

DPW Superintendent Archey stated this project will be replacing any sidewalk that has been heaved or misaligned due to the root system of our city trees. He stated the contractor will be directed in which areas to address, instead of bouncing around from here and there. They will be removing and replacing 300 flags that are creating trip hazards. They will also be removing and disposing of the tree roots that are causing the problem. This is a budgeted job and will be coming out of GL 101-441-974.

Councilmember Dilg asked how they decide which sidewalks to replace. Mr. Archey stated a lot of the information came from phone calls, and he has also looked throughout the city. He then stated they have a crew that will tear out the sidewalk, then another crew will tear out the roots, another crew will form and prep, then another crew to pour. He further stated he looked at the work they are doing in Brighton and he talked with the Director in Brighton and he is very happy with the contractor. Councilmember Kivell asked if there is a way to carve back some roots so we don't have to keep doing this. Mr. Archey stated he can look into it. He thinks with the mild winter we had; it appears the roots didn't stop growing like it normally does. Councilmember Kennedy stated he wants to applaud him for his creativity and the approach. By contracting this out you increase the capacity of the work that meets their skill set, and also with them having the different crews to come in in sequence, it is dealing with those problems concurrently rather than sequentially and shortens the time to do that job overall.

# THE BUD MARTIN FAMILY

It is my pleasure to speak about the Martin family.

#### (Pic. of Bud Martin early years.)

Charles (Bud) Martin was born in South Lyon in 1935 at the Havershaw Hospital at Detroit & Wells Streets. His family has roots in this area dating back to 1830.

Bud started work at Ed Nugents' Hardware at 17 year old. Madonna Trinowsky started work there at 18 years old soon after.

You no doubt have heard of high school sweethearts ---well these two were hardware sweethearts.

#### (Pic. of their wedding.)

They had three children: Bob, Bill and Marianne.

In 1968 they bought the hardware store at 105 N. Lafayette from Max Gates. Donna was the bookkeeper and worked the sales floor with Bud.

#### (Pic. Of Donna climbing.)

Bob first worked in the store in 1972 at 12 years old. Bill joined in 1982 at 16 years old. He was busy with High School sports: baseball and basketball. Marianne had a career with the South Lyon Schools.

#### (Pic. of family in front of the Hardware.)

Bud & Donna laid the foundation for Bill and Bob to succeed in this business. They were kind and friendly to the community. The family has supported many local organizations and causes over 55 years.

#### (Pic. of Bud in store.)

--From sports teams to VFW, Operation Injured Soldier, camps for blind children, Active Faith, local churches, S. Lyon School programs, The Historical Village, American Legion, and Boy Scout Eagle Projects such as the downtown American flags.

In addition — they have given first jobs to many South Lyon youth, trained them to be helpful and friendly to all.

#### (Pic. of Bud in store.)

Norm Somers told this story to me: He and his wife Myla went to Canada and they saw an antique pitcher pump that he wanted to use. Well, it was okay except for the leather seal. So, he went to see Bud and ask him, "Do you have any leather that I could make work." Bud reached into a bin and pulled out the exact replacement seal --and Bud says, "Like this one?"

Ron Weber called Bud at home after hours: "My pump failed and I don't have water."

Bud picked up a pump at the store and delivered it & helped install the pump to get him going again.

Sometime in the 70's there was a big snowstorm and Bud & Donna snowmobiled from their home at 8 Mile & Ruston to be there for customers. This was the commitment they always made to their community.

It has been my pleasure to work with Suzann Martin who is the wife of Bob. Eighteen years ago she joined the City of S. Lyon water management programs, of which there are two: Wellhead Protection, and Storm Water Management. Her recent work was an Art Contest for the new water dept. logo, now in use. She has assisted me with the Creek Cleanup for 18 years. Suzann has had a water booth at Depot Day for 16 years, as well as at other annual South Lyon events.

## V

Suzann enjoys her work because \textit{t} involves science, and working with students and other water organizations. Suzann has worked studiously for the programs --stays up to date with the science of water, and does many tasks including education and outreach activities.

Suzann also volunteers at the S. Lyon Historical Society and Historical Commission as a Director and a Commissioner. She is a committee member of The Watershed & Storm Water Committee of Mich. Water Environmental Association and a member of Water Environment Federation.

When I moved to town in 1976 I was fortunate to experience the special treatment of the Martin family – from Bud, Donna, Bob, and Bill and also their employees who carry on with the family atmosphere of Martin's Hardware.
