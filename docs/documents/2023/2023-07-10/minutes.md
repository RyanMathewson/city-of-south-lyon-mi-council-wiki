##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $- Downtown

DDA Director Mack stated they submitted the grant for the MEDC was submitted on June 29th, and the total request was over $600,000 to build a park downtown. We already have some funding and we will find out in September if we were successful. He stated our application was competitive and hopes for the best. He then stated the weather wasn't the best for the Farmers Market, but it still went well. He also stated the economic vitality committee met last week and they worked on an activity called asset mapping, which highlights things that are in our downtown to focus on to progress the downtown and flushing that out and turning it into an economic strategy for the downtown. Mr. Mack stated he is still working on the Social District implementation and the application has been submitted to the State of Michigan.
