### 8. FIRST READING OF FIREWORK ORDINANCE

City Attorney Wilhelm stated the State legislature adopted public act 65 of 2013, and it entails a number of revisions to the Firework Safety Act. The most permanent to the City is the fact that it authorizes the City to adopt local ordinances to regulate the times the consumer fireworks can be discharged on holidays. He further stated the proposed amendment would prohibit any consumer fireworks between 12:00 a.m. and 8:00 a.m. including the day before, the day of and the day after any holiday excluding New Years Eve.

Chief Kennedy stated there has been a lot of confusion about this. He further stated the consumer fireworks are the fireworks that leave the ground. People can still use sparklers, and fountains between those hours, this law does not give the City the leverage to prevent all fireworks between those hours. Councilman Kivell stated he is surprised this wasn't seen as a problem at the beginning.
