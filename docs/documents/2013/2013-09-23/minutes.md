## OLD BUSINESS

### 1. Knolls of South Lyon-First Reading

City Attorney Wilhelm stated this is before Council again due a motion to reconsider the motion made at the September 9 $ ^{th} $ Council meeting. He further stated the motion from the previous meeting is still pending, and it is presented to Council again for discussion.

Mayor Wallace stated at the last meeting there were some misunderstandings. He further stated Council has some concerns regarding this, such as not having access from the north and northeast, as well as the footprint of the homes on the property.

Councilman Kivell stated he has done more research and there were only 5 properties that had to ask for a variance to install a deck in Charleston Park, not as many as we thought. He stated if all homes fall under the criteria in the example, we won't have many requests for variances regarding decks. He further stated there is a great deal of wetlands and wondered if it is regulated by the State. He stated he is also concerned about the grade variations, and there will need to be a water retention area somewhere. He stated HRC will have some input in regards to those issues. He further stated Council wouldn't be doing their jobs if we didn't discuss all concerns regarding this property and especially the safety of our residents. He further stated he would like to research the viability of another access point.

Councilmember Kopkowski stated she would like to know the square footage of the largest home the developer plans on building. Mr. Elkowe stated the largest home is the Wellington and it is 2236 square feet. Councilman Kivell stated that would be too large for the lot size. Mr. Elkowe stated this is a colonial; therefore it would fit on the building envelope.

Councilman Kramer stated it is his understanding that if Council approves the first reading, Council can set some conditions that need to be addressed by the Planning Commission before the second reading. City Attorney Wilhelm stated that it is correct, the second reading will require two motions. One for the zoning and one for the stage one site plan of the Planned Development. He further stated Council will be able to put conditions on the site plan before it 9/23/13

goes back to the Planning Commission for the final site plan review. He stated you can keep this process moving and let the Planning Commission deal with the conditions Council would like to see addressed. Councilman Kramer stated if there are conditions Council would like to see placed, it can be discussed after the first reading. City Attorney Wilhelm stated he will be providing Council with the proposed language to address the factors that are identified in the planned development zoning. He further stated whether to approve or deny, you need to relate to factors in our Zoning Ordinance. In addition in the site plan approval process, you can deny, but need to state reasons why, and if approving you can place conditions in the motion. Councilman Wedell stated the first and second reading is just for the rezoning, not approving the current plans. City Attorney Wilhelm stated if you rezone the property, you cannot deviate from the plans that are approved. Councilman Kivell stated it is important for Council to discuss all concerns whether they are large concerns or incidentals. Councilman Kramer stated he wants to be clear that you do not have to impose conditions on the first reading. City Attorney Wilhelm stated no, you can impose conditions in the second reading and site plan review.

Discussion was held regarding the concerns of Council, such as drainage, setbacks, size of homes, and the need for more than one entrance. Councilman Ryzyi stated his concern is for the safety of the residents, and he asked Chief Collins if he could clarify his concerns for only one entrance. Chief Collins stated he is very much in favor of having two access points open to the public. He stated if one entrance is blocked, the residents have no way to leave, the morning peak traffic, school buses and patrol patterns are all reasons he is in favor of a second entrance. He further stated according to our zoning ordinance, the second access point is a requirement. Councilman Ryzyi stated the plan only has one access point which is contradictory to what Chief Collins is suggesting. He further stated for the safety of our residents he has to agree with the Chief that there should be two access points.

Councilmember Kopkowski stated that her concerns are the entrances, drainage, the tree survey, wetlands and she would like to see the designs of the homes and she doesn't want 89 Wellington's in this development.

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. 2012-2013 Audit Presentation

Brian Camillar of Plante Moran stated the City of South Lyon is a longtime and very important client of Plante Moran. He stated the financial statements are the property of South Lyon. He further stated the opinion letter belongs to the firm. He stated the opinion letter states that the financial statements are complete, accurate, it can be relied upon and it follows the rules. He stated this is the highest level of assurance an auditor can give, it is like getting an A on a report card.

Manju Patnaik of Plante Moran presented and discussed a set of graphs. She highlighted the revenues of the City's general fund. She stated the revenues have remained consistent since 2012. She stated the City has been cautious with expenditures which is a good thing. She discussed the taxable values, net assets as well as the revenue sharing. She stated the City has done a great job.

Brian Camillar of Plante Moran discussed the audit and stated the management of the City has done a great job. Councilman Kramer asked if the DWRF was included in the graph for the water and sewer. Mr. Camillar stated no, that graph just shows the ins and outs. Mr. Camillar discussed the Federal Awards Special Audit. The Federal Government sometimes gives money in form of grants but has special rules on how it has to be spent. There are many rules involved. He stated the auditors test the related rules with the Federal money. He stated 2.3 million expenditures occurred, and 2.2 million is all tied to the DWRF. He further stated it has been administered effectively and in compliance with all federal rules.

Mr. Camillar discussed the Management letter. He stated the City's finance department did a great job, and they found no major deficiencies. He stated the EVIP documents are due September 30th. He stated they only have a few minor suggestions, such as a credit card use ordinance, and passwords should be changed every month and using more complex passwords.

Mayor Wallace stated Plante and Moran always do a great job, and Lori Mosier our bookkeeper has always done a great job as well.
