Nate Mack, DDA Director stated through the Oakland County Restaurant Relief Program, the City was awarded a few weatherization items from the County and he has been in contact with all the restaurants. He then stated there will be an interlocal agreement that will come before Council at the next meeting. ## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Vogel thanked Kelly Breen for her kind words. He then stated we brought in some critical incident teams after the shooting and the plane crash. We had staff at both. He and Chief Sovik are making sure their employees have what they need for their mental health after both incidents. We didn't realize there was a glitch with our employee assistance program, but he and Patricia have been able to solve it, so his staff will have access to the program. He then stated if anyone is interested in getting the vaccine, you can text oak gov to 28748 and you will receive updates on your phone. He stated they have been bombarded with calls about the vaccine. He wants everyone to know the City of South Lyon and Lyon Township will not be a vaccine site. It will be Novi, station 4 near Wixom Road. He then stated Inspector Mathews was hospitalized from complications of COVID, but he is now home resting. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if when you get the first shot of the vaccine, does it take 8-10 days for the body to build some immunity? She doesn't think it is immediate. Chief Vogel stated it is about 50% effectiveness until you get the second shot.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Sovik stated there was a 17-year-old that was tragically killed, and his 43-year-old father was also shot. We have one individual that is in custody and charged with that murder, as well as 3 others that are housed in the Oakland County jail in connection with the incident. They should be arraigned this week. Unfortunately, these things do happen. He stated we were very fortunate to have the resources of the Oakland County Sheriff's office. We had detectives from the Special Investigation Unit that were not dealing with any other incident in the County and they were able to assist with locating some of the individuals. Chief Sovik thanked them for all their assistance. Chief Sovik then stated the Michigan Law Enforcement Accreditation Program represents a significant professional achievement. Accreditation acknowledges the implementation of written directives, policies, and procedures that are conceptually sound and operationally effective. He further stated it can be a daunting task and right now there are only 26 agencies that are accredited and there will be 5 more in February and 70-80 more is in the process. We hope to be on the edge of the technology that we need in the compliance standards and as far as receiving federal funds, this will help. We will begin the process as soon as the new hire is on his own, hopefully in February. Chief Sovik stated he will be coming to Council with information from CLEMIS for equipment that we need to update within the next 18 months, the fingerprinting and mug shot hardware will need to be updated. He further stated we have funds from the DEA asset forfeiture seizure that we would like to use to purchase these items. He stated it is all part of the criminal case building system and it is very important. He then stated we now have the contracts signed and they should be dropping the lines this week. In the meantime, we have all the phones ordered and hopefully will be installed in the next couple weeks. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated his handling of all the situations in the City reflects the true leader that you are. For you to be working with McCabe and the Oakland County Sheriff's office demonstrates that you are developing the professional network and the professional connections to lead the city in any issues that involve law enforcement issues.

Councilmember Kennedy stated the Salem-South Lyon District Library was one of fifty organizations across the country selected for a $1,200 Lift Every Voice programming grant. The Library is using the grant to host a traveling exhibit from the Michigan State University Museum titled "Welcome to Idlewild: The Black Eden of Michigan". The exhibit provides a glimpse beyond the often told stories of Idlewild's entertainment scene during its resort heyday period to learn the full story of a community that has survived the challenges of historical change. The exhibit has been created in collaboration with current residents and scholars of Idlewild. The exhibit consists of photographic banners and a reproduction of an Idlewild history quilt by Michigan quilter Deonna Todd Green. The exhibit is currently on display at the Salem-South Lyon District Library through February.
