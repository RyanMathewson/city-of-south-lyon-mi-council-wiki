### 1. Consider approval of revised resolution establishing policies and guidelines for granting poverty exemption from payment of property taxes

City Manager Zelenak stated due to recent changes to the General Property Tax Act, specifically MCL 211.7u, the provision commonly referred to as the Poverty Exemption, the Resolution Establishing Policies and Guidelines for Granting Poverty Exemption from Payment of Property Taxes needs revision. The attached revised Resolution Establishing Policies and Guidelines for Granting Poverty Exemption from Payment of Property Taxes removes the City application requirement and specifies the exemption amounts of 25%, 50% or 100%. The Resolution will remain in effect without having to approve a revised resolution each year after the annual income level is updated. Councilmember Kivell asked if the City provides the information to the applicant. Clerk/Treasurer Deaton stated they have to apply every year. Clerk/Treasurer Deaton stated the March Board of Review meets 3-4 times to hear poverty exemptions. ## Memorandum

TO: Honorable Mayor & City Council

DATE: March 8, 2021

The purpose of this memo is to provide my analysis of the request for a City of South Lyon Class C Liquor License for 135 E. Lake Street (also known as The RCA Building).

##  $ \underline{\text{Background}} $

1. The City of South Lyon has one (1) Class C Quota Liquor License available, based upon the Michigan Liquor Control Commission (MLCC) rules for municipalities based on population, known as the quota system.

2. This is new information as it was previously thought the City of South Lyon had no Class C Quota liquor licenses available.

3. The approximate cost of a Class C quota license is $1,000.

4. Compliance with the City's liquor license ordinance.

5. Police department review as part of the MLCC process to approve.

6. The City's (DDA and City Council) desire to attract an upscale restaurant to the downtown as outlined in the master plan.

7. Availability of adequate space for an upscale restaurant.

8. Availability of Redevelopment (DDA) Liquor Licenses. For the applicant to be eligible for the Redevelopment License, there must no City of South Lyon quota licenses currently available.

9. The initial enhanced license fee for a Redevelopment Liquor License is $20,000, which is set by the State of Michigan.

10. The cost of a non-quota Class C Liquor License purchased by a private owner and transferable from anywhere in Oakland County is approximately $60,000 - $120,000.

11. Does the proposed upscale Italian restaurant improve the business mix in downtown South Lyon?

12. MLCC requirements.

13. City of South Lyon building requirements.

14. The substantial investment the developer is currently making into a property that has been unoccupied and unused for years.

15. The likelihood of the liquor license remaining in South Lyon via the stability and longevity of the business operation.

16. State of Michigan and United States Federal Government Barrier Free Requirements (Americans with Disabilities Act).

## Recommendation

After my review of background information, and after numerous conversations with the applicant, it is my recommendation to the City Council approve the request from Tom Palushaj for the City's final Quota Class C Liquor License for the following reasons:

1. The request meets the requirements of the City's liquor license ordinance.

2. The information provided by the applicant reveals to me that the proposed business operation will be high quality.

3. The applicant has considerable experience in the restaurant industry and has demonstrated success in previous restaurant endeavors.

4. The interior design of the space will provide an intimate environment unlike any restaurant currently in the City of South Lyon.

5. This upscale Italian restaurant provides an important component of an optimal business mix in the downtown.

6. The likelihood of the liquor license remaining in South Lyon is greater because the applicant has been a part of the community for more many years and is well respected with numerous years of restaurant industry experience.

7. The applicant has, most recently, developed the Parkside apartment building on S. Warren St. and has shown a willingness to upgrade building materials based on the recommendation of the Planning Commission and has been very responsive and timely to City of South Lyon requests.

8. This development will provide a massive boost to the vibrancy of downtown and the overall community, which will greatly improve the downtown by making it more attractive to other potential restaurants and retail establishments.

9. The feedback received from the planning, police, fire, and public works departments has been positive.

10. The applicant's site has a municipal parking lot in the rear and is within the City's Parking Exempt Zone as noted in the City's zoning ordinance.

11. The restaurant's hours will extend to later in the evening, which may encourage other establishments to extend their hours beyond 6 or 7pm.

12. The liquor license is essential to the success of this business model. The upper story bar is dependent on the ability to sell alcohol and will provide a new entertainment experience to South Lyon.

13. The building where the restaurant will be located is a significant historical structure in the city and is well-known by its residents. Its restoration and use will be important for the City of South Lyon.

14. Approval of Mr. Palushaj's request will not hinder our ability to recruit and accommodate another experienced, upscale restaurant with the availability of Redevelopment (DDA) Liquor Licenses.

#### RESOLUTION APPROVING THE APPLICATION FOR A CLASS C QUOTA ON-PREMISES LIQUOR LICENSE FOR DUA VINO LLC, 135 E. LAKE ST., SOUTH LYON, MICHIGAN

WHEREAS, the City adopted and has a Liquor License Ordinance, City of South Lyon Code of Ordinances, Chapter 8 – Alcoholic Liquor, which provides for the City's regulation and licensing of new on-premises liquor establishments within the City;

WHEREAS, the City's Liquor License Ordinance is intended to ensure that the individuals and entities seeking liquor licenses or who propose to operate licensed on-premises liquor establishments within the City meet certain minimum requirements as to background, experience, financial resources, business operations, and management experience and that the proposed liquor establishment meets the needs of the community;

WHEREAS, the City Liquor License Ordinance follows the City Council to review application materials and other information in light of certain criteria to identify the applicants and proposed liquor establishments that best qualify for a liquor license and best meet the needs of the City and its residents;

WHEREAS, an applicant who receives an approval or recommendation for approval from the City must also obtain applicable licenses from the Michigan Liquor Control Commission and comply with the Michigan Liquor Control Code and applicable MLCC regulations and other agency requirements (e.g. Health Department permits);

WHEREAS, in January 2021, Applicant, Dua Vino LLC, which is owned and operated by Tom Pulashaj, submitted to the City a Liquor License Application for a Class C quota on-premises liquor license to operate an Italian restaurant at 135 E. Lake St., South Lyon as part of the rehabilitation of the historic structure;

WHEREAS, the City Administration and Departments have reviewed the Applicant's application and other materials in light of the review criteria in Section 8-36(f) and other applicable requirements;

WHEREAS, the City Administration has met with the Applicant and its representatives regarding Plan of Operations, proposed interior renovations and building improvements, and other applicable requirements, including the proposed schedule for completion of the proposed renovations;

WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the Applicant's Liquor License Application and other information in light of the review criteria in the City's Liquor License Ordinance, and finds:

This article shall be known and may be cited as "The City of South Lyon Liquor License Ordinance."

Sec. 8-32. - Statement of purpose.

The purpose of this article is to establish the city's policies and procedures for regulating liquor licenses including application and review procedures for the issuance of new on-premises licenses, transfers of on-premises licenses into and within the city and among owners and/or applicants, and for the renewal and revocation of liquor licenses. The process is intended to ensure that the individuals and entities seeking licenses or who propose to operate licensed on-premises liquor establishments within the city, meet certain minimum requirements as to background, experience, financial resources, business operations and management and that the proposed establishment meets the needs of the community. It requires the city council to review application information in light of certain criteria to identify the kinds of applicants and establishments that best qualify for a license and best meet the needs of the city and its residents. It reserves to the city any and all discretion afforded to it under applicable laws relating to the issuance of on-premises licenses.

(Ord. No. 03-18, Pt. I, 5-14-18)

## I-96 Flex Route Maintaining Traffic Concept Pros

■ Maintaining 2 lanes in each direction allows traffic to be shifted to one bound, giving contractor full access to the opposite bound, improving contractor safety and shortening overall construction duration

• Maintaining 3 lanes in each direction adds approximately 15 months to overall construction duration

■ Maintaining 2 lanes in each direction on one bound minimizes the temporary widening required to maintain traffic, which minimizes potential environmental impacts and reduces construction cost
