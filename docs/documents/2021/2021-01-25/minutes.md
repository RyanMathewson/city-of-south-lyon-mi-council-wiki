## 2020 Firefighter of the Year

SOUTH LYON, Monday January 17, 2021 Congratulations to Sergeant Cindy Conrad who has been selected as the South Lyon Fire Department 2020 Firefighter of the Year!

Sergeant Conrad has 7 years of dedicated service with the South Lyon Fire Department. Her passion and commitment as a Company Officer is second to none and may only be superseded by her investment in the EMS Program. She spends countless hours both on and off-duty working towards the improvement, innovation and ownership of this Program. She always goes above and beyond as an EMS Coordinator by maintaining personnel needs, equipment and supplies. She is a key component in the Department's successful relationships with outside agencies and provides Continuing Education for the Emergency Medical Services Program.

Sergeant Conrad achieves all of the above while still preparing her crew for the all-risk challenges that a fire company may face. She is constantly learning new information and skills of the job while holding herself to the highest standards without compromise.

Sergeant Conrad compassion for the public and his crew is unmatched. She constantly ensures the happiness and wellbeing of others before herself. Her integrity is commendable as he sets the bar for excellence in the performance of her duties. Her outstanding leadership is evident as he inspires personnel through his strong work ethic and positive attitude. She exemplifies the Department's shared values of integrity, ownership, compassion, innovation and excellence.

Please join us in congratulating Sergeant Conrad for this honorable recognition as the South Lyon Fire Department 2020 Firefighter of the Year.

Councilmember Kurtzweil congratulated Ms. Conrad and stated she is a role model for every young girl in the City that wants to grow up to be a firefighter. She then thanked her for everything. Ms. Cindy Conrad thanked everyone for the speech and the recognition. All of Council congratulated her and thanked her. Councilmember Kivell stated she is the standard for every firefighter, male or female. ## NEW BUSINESS

1. Bartlett Robo-Bear Presentation

City Manager Zelenak stated a local elementary school robotics team, the Bartlett RoboBears have approached the City to initiate a challenge called RePlay to find ways to help people become more active. The plan is to encourage people to get out to community places and be active. The team will place small signs with QR codes that will link to a challenge at various locations in McHattie Park, and future potential locations along the trails or at other locations throughout the City.

Andrea O'Keefe introduced herself as the assistant Coach to the robotics team and then she stated there are also two members joining the meeting, Clark O'Keefe and Justin Zischke. Ms. O'Keefe stated the presentation is pre-recorded. *Anyone interested in watching the presentation along with the Council meeting, may watch on YouTube, or the City's website* The Robo Bears have 6 team members and are the first logo robotics team, and in 2020 they came in 3rd place out of all teams in the state. The presentation explained they understand that people aren't active enough. Partly because of the pandemic and being on technology more. They decided to design a web app game using fun activities in safe spaces, such as parks, libraries and walking trails. It is a friendly competition where players can set their own goals to achieve, be active and have fun. The motivation is to earn points and badges and the game will show a leaderboard. The game encourages healthful, safe activity by being outdoors in all seasons. Players get exercise by going to the different locations in the community and we offer a fun activity at every location where they can earn points and badges. If interested, go to robotbearsrumble.com and scan a QR code and login, then follow the instructions. Councilmember Dilg thanked them for coming and stated that was a great presentation and asked if there will be things in Lyon Township, and will it be one game, or will the City and the Township be separate. Clark O'Keefe stated it will all be part of one game. Councilmember Dilg asked how they will know if the game is successful. Mr. O'Keefe stated they will know by how many people join the game. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated they are already a success just by doing something for the community and giving up your time for your community. She then stated you placed 3rd place out of 64 teams, that is a great job. Mayor Pelchat said he is very proud and if there is anything he can do to help, he will be happy to.
