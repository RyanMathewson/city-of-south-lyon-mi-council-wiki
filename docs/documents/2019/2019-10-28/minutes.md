## FIRE CHIEF REPORT-

Chief Vogel stated he reviewed the survey and he is proud of the 4.5 and he hopes to make some improvements. He stated he thinks one of the issues is the Inspector Mathews is part time. He is flexible and he tries to work with the business owners, but the problem is he is bombarded with phone calls and emails. He stated he made the change that he now receives his emails as well so if the Inspector isn't available, he can respond to it. He further stated another issue is the growth and public safety. He is going to make the website more user friendly by adding the tent code as well as the fire code.

Chief Vogel stated the overhead door at the Fire Department is still broken, so the fire truck is still at the Water Department. He stated he had a conversation with Councilmember Kurtzweil who gave him contact information for Jim Runestad. He stated he tried contacting him by phone, but he emailed him regarding the PA291 and the requirements for training in the future and he is happy to be heard. Councilmember Kivell stated we are in the process of updating the websites. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she always gets questions on the firework policy. Chief Vogel stated it is much more restrictive and it has always been on their website. Councilmember Parisien asked if the change with the email will go into effect. Chief Vogel stated it is in the process of being changed. Councilmember Parisien suggested possibly amend the budget for a full-time position or maybe hiring an assistant for him. Chief Vogel stated the Inspector does a great job and he is seen in 100% of the businesses throughout the year. He stated the problem is when someone calls when he isn't available and we are bombarded with plan reviews and things like that. He further stated one problem is Safebuilt will get plans, and not pass the information along in a timely matter to the Fire Department for review. He further stated he would rather not hire that position full time and save the money for staff. Councilmember Walton questioned the problem with the fire truck. Chief Vogel stated there are two ways the bucket can move up and down. We can raise it from the turntable but it is safer for the people in the bucket to control it and the communication wires are broken, they are worn out. He has contacted the manufacturer twice to figure out what wires need to be replaced and they are now ordered and he is hoping they will be here this week.
