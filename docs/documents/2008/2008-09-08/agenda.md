**PUBLIC COMMENTS**

Nancy Kern of 450 W. Liberty stated she is here to make a complaint about the increase in noise
level at the Seamless Tube Company. She then read a letter that she emailed to each Council
Member. It stated she is very concerned that the City does not enforce the noise ordinance, and
since there has been a change in ownership, they are making much more noise, and they want to do
an expansion. Mayor Doyle asked her what she was looking for Council to do tonight. She stated
she would like Council to comment on why the noise ordinance is not enforced for the tube mill.
Mayor Doyle stated the tube mill has been there for many years, and we do go through and
investigate the noise complaints. He further stated this is the first complaint they have heard of in a
long time. Councilman Kivell stated she is looking at the worse case scenario, just because they are
talking about an expansion, does not mean they will do one. If they do want to do an expansion,
they will have to go through regular site plan procedures.
City Manager Murphy stated he has spoken with Larry Ledbetter, and he stated there have been no
changes in their process. He did state they would shut the door when the lathe is on and he would
change the time of day they run that. He further stated there are bins that are being dumped more
than one or two times a day and he stated they would change that to one time during the day.

the yard, or put some kind of barrier around it, it would help a lot.

drop them into a tub and it is very loud. She further stated it has gotten louder. They have semi
trucks that come around the back and they have moved a large high-low next to her yard where the
fence is. There are definitely a few things that are different.  More discussion was held on the
noises that come from the tube mill.

**NEW BUSINESS**


1. Homecoming Parade

Chief Collins stated the route is the same as the past. He further stated we have had some issues
because they insist on creating an unsafe situation by allowing the people on the floats to throw out
candy to the children, which could cause a child to run into the road to retrieve it. They have
promised they will identify the people they had that particular problem with last year and they will
not have those issues again this year.
Chief Collins stated he would recommend Council approve the Homecoming Parade, but there is a
good chance, we will be barring a number of vehicles from the parade for safety purposes.

CM 9-4-08-HOMECOMING PARADE

Motion by Maida, supported by Wedell

Motion to approve the closure of Pontiac Trail, University, Oxford, Cambridge, Mcmunn, W
Liberty, Warren and Lake Street on October 10, 2008 between 4:00 p.m. and 5:00 p.m. for the South
Lyon High School Homecoming Parade.

VOTE: MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY


2. Indemnification agreement with Lyon Township

Attorney Hill stated all the paperwork has gone out to the State Boundary Commission. In order for
Lyon Township to continue installing their sanitary sewer system we were asking them to provide an
indemnification agreement in case something happens to anything or person they would indemnify
the City of South Lyon. Councilman Kivell questioned if there is any concern regarding if they put
hardware in there and the transaction does not go through, we are stuck with a chink in the armor of
our system. Attorney Hill stated there is no contingency clause in this agreement. Councilman
Kivell stated this is a good step, but there is nothing to protect our system and us if for some reason
it does not end up happening. Discussion was held regarding the consequences we would have to
deal with if they left this half done. Attorney Hill stated she could add a paragraph to the
indemnification agreement for a contingency.

Maggie Kurtzweil of 939 West Hills Drive stated she would like to suggest that the City require the
engineers to post a performance bond and the City could file a claim against it if there were any
issues. You always want to think of the worse case scenario. Councilman Kivell stated whether
they get it done or not, really is not our issue, our concern is if the sanctity of our community is
breached by their system.

It was the consensus of council to table this until the next meeting.

**MANAGERS REPORT**

City Manager Murphy stated that Reynold Sweet Parkway was supposed to be finished already.
Some discussion was held regarding why it wasn’t completed.

Mayor Doyle questioned City Manager Murphy about a light being placed at Martindale. City
Manager Murphy stated there would be a light there. He further stated he requested to be notified
ahead of time the next time they decide to put in a new light.

Jennifer Hill of Booth Patterson stated she has good news on the Demaria case. It is over and the
judge granted Trotters Pointe’s motion for summary disposition without a trial, What that means is
the open space parcel will remain a part of Trotters Pointe. It was in favor of the City and Trotters
Pointe. They do have until the 19 [th] of September for an appeal.
Councilman Morelli stated he would not be at the next meeting.

Council Member Maida stated she would like Department Head Martin to explain the monthly water
report that is in the packet. Department Head Martin stated this is an overlook of what happened in
the month of July relating to the Water Department.

Some discussion was held on the report.

Councilman Kivell stated he would like Department Head Martin to keep an eye on the untreated
water that is used at the tube mill to see if there is an increase.

Martin Gaut of 459 W Liberty questioned why the water is so hard. Department Head Martin stated
there are minerals deep in the ground that make up hardness. He further stated we remove the iron,
but it would be very expensive to remove the hardness level. It is not a health issue.  Iron is much
easier to remove than hardness.

Council Member Maida stated Depot Days was this past weekend and it was wonderful. She further
stated the weather was great and thanked the DPW for all their work.

Councilman Wedell stated he also enjoyed the Depot Days.

Council Member Kopkowski questioned when the lights are scheduled to go on for the streetscape
project. City Manager Murphy stated they are waiting for the bulbs. Council Member Kopkowski
questioned when the brick is going to be finished. City Manager Murphy stated they want to do the
brick when they do the trees. He further stated he is pushing it along the best he can.
