CITY OF SOUTH LYON
SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL MEETING

MAY 3, 2008


Mayor Pro-tem Kivell called the meeting to order at 8:03 a.m.
Mayor Pro-tem Kivell led those present in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag

PRESENT: Mayor Doyle
Council Members: Kivell, Kopkowski, Maida, Morelli, Selden and Wedell
Attorney Lee
City Clerk/Treasurer Zemke

Mayor Doyle arrived at 8:08 a.m.

Interviews with candidates for the City Manager position as follows:

David Murphy - 8:10 a.m. to 9:20 a.m.

Dennis Sparks – 9:32 a.m. to 11:07 a.m.

Gary Rawlings – 11:18 a.m. to 12:22 p.m.

Council adjourned for lunch at 12:25 p.m. and reconvened at 1:00 p.m.

Eric Wiederhold – 1:00 p.m. to 2:52 p.m.

A brief break was taken from 2:52 p.m. until 3:05 p.m.

Discussion was held on the candidates. The consensus was that Council was pleased that they
interviewed additional candidates.

Discussion was held on the matrix used to determine the qualified applicants based on the
advertisement for the position.

Discussion was held on any liability issues that may be a result of interviewing candidates not following
the matrix.

Discussion was held on interviewing additional candidates. Discussion was held on Council reviewing
the resumes of the remaining qualified applicants. Consensus of Council was that if at least three
council members wished to interview any of the candidates then they would proceed with that interview.
The City Clerk will email the additional resumes to Council for their review and Council would provide
any additional names to her no later than Wednesday, May 7, 2008.

Discussion was held on changing the date of the budget workshop session scheduled for May 7 [th] .

CM 5-1-08 – SUSPENSION OF COUNCIL RULES

Motion by Wedell, supported by Morelli

To suspend Council rules to allow discussion of changing the date of the budget workshop

VOTE: MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY

Mayor Doyle stated that he would not be able to attend on Wednesday and asked if Council could
move the workshop to Thursday.
