7:30 p.m. Call to Order

Roll Call

Pledge of Allegiance

Minutes: Special Meetings- April 19th, May 3rd, May 8th, May 12th, and May 15th, 2008

Monthly Bills- None

Approval of Agenda

Citizen's Suggestions

4. Establishment of Rewards for Vandalism

2. Resolution approving Bank Depositories for the various City funds.

III. Council Comments

IV. Adjournment
