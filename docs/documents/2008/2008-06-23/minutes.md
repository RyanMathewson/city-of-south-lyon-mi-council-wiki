**CITIZENS SUGGESTIONS** :

Mr. Tom Duncan of 60440 11 Mile and Randy Duncan of 1009 Carriage Trace were present representing
Duncan Disposal. Mr. Tom Duncan stated that the new contract for garbage pick-up commences July 1 [st],
therefore the last pick up with the current hauler will be June 30 [th] . He further stated that what they are
proposing to do is to split the City into two pick-up days. This will allow them to complete pick-up much
earlier and allow for more prompt service. He stated that this will involve informing 1300 residents.
Residents north of the centerline of Lake Street will continue to be picked up on Monday and residents
south of the centerline of Lake Street will be picked-up on Tuesday. He stated that they will be delivering
notice to the front door of each of the affected homes.

Discussion was held on the need for the split. Discussion was held on the problems experienced with the
current hauler including very late pick up times, missed residents, etc.

Discussion was held on delays due to holidays. Mr. Duncan explained that if there is a holiday that falls
on or before the pick-up day during the work week then the pick-up day is delayed one day.

Discussion was held on some residents being disappointed in the Tuesday pick up as many people do
weekend yard work, etc. and they will now have to let it sit for an additional day.

**OLD BUSINESS:**


1. Approval of Park and Recreation Master Plan

Retired City Manager Cook stated that Council did see this plan some time ago. This plan needs to be on
file with the Michigan Department of Natural Resources in order to qualify for any funds that might become
available. The plan has been updated and adopted by the Parks and Recreation Commission and is a five
year plan.

Discussion was held on some of their proposed projects and goals. Discussion was held on a proposed
recreation facility in Volunteer Park. Retired City Manager Cook stated that is something they would like
to ultimately see. However, that type of project would require a larger body. That is not something that is
moving forward at this time. This is a list of things they would like to see done, but it is a fairly expensive
list.

Discussion was held on grant funds available. Retired City Manager Cook stated that there is an
application process every year. The problem is that they have limited funds in comparison to the number
of proposals. He stated that we did receive a grant for the first phase of Volunteer Park.

Councilman Selden stated that he would be opposed to spending any funds to match a grant for a nonnecessity.

Councilman Kivell stated that he was hoping to see some bracketed perspective costs rather than leaving
it open-ended. He further asked if the DNR looks at the lack of completed projects unfavorably. Retired
City Manager Cook stated that is generally not the case. Priorities do change and that is not an issue.
Further discussion was held on the small amounts of money available.

2. Collective Bargaining Agreement- Police Officers Association of Michigan for the period 7/1/07 to
6/30/2010

Retired City Manager Cook stated that before Council is the negotiated contract with the Police Officers
Association of Michigan which has been ratified. The contract is for a three year period retroactive to
7/1/07. It is his recommendation to approve the contract and move forward. It is basically the same as
the command unit with the exception of the holidays which would be retroactive to January 1, 2008
anything prior to that would be lost.

Discussion was held on the elimination of 0 days accident. Retired City Manager Cook stated that we
have had a number of short term disabilities due to participation in athletic activities. We are trying to
control that and if that happens they have to use 7 days sick time before short-term disability begins.

Council Member Kopkowski stated that she would have rather had the contract to review instead of a
memo. Retired City Manager Cook stated that because they changed unions, they need to totally
reconstruct the contract whereas the other unions already have them on their computer. Council Member
Kopkowski stated that she is being asked to approve a contract that she does not have in front of her, and
that disappoints her. She further stated that she is disappointed with the notion that whatever the
Command Unit gets the Patrol Unit gets. We have less income coming into the City and we are not even
thinking of cutting back. At some point we will be looking at laying people off.

Discussion was held on what happens next if the contract is not approved.

Mayor Doyle stated that he feels that this is a fair and reasonable proposal.

Councilman Kivell stated that in the past we were concerned about turnover because we were lower than
comparable communities, however we do not have that turnover anymore. He further stated that
arbitration is always a gamble.

3. Resolution to Annex and Detach properties with Lyon Twp.
a. Resolution Annexing certain property- 10.0275 acres
b. Resolution Detaching certain property- 22000 Pontiac Trail
c. Approval of negotiated Annexation Agreement- Exhibit B with attached Township
Resolution

Attorney Lee stated that this began when the City Manger discovered that the Township was attempting to
cross through the City with their sewer line. Rather than negotiate a franchise agreement, we elected to
negotiate with the Township to allow the church, which is the only parcel east of Pontiac Trail and south of
Nine Mile in the City, to detach and go back into the Township in the event that they would allow the
northern 10 acre parcel of Carriage Trace to be annexed into the City. Their only concern was that a new
site plan could allow a street to empty onto Trebor Lane which is a gravel Road. We would never wish to
or consent to have a paved City Street empty onto a gravel road. Therefore, we had no problem getting
into an annexation agreement which would limit us in our site plan approval. We would provide for a berm
along Trebor Lane so that no outlet would go into that street. Other than that restriction the City Planning
Commission is under no constraint as to how to approve and what to require in the site planning of that 10
acres. There are three items that need to be approved in order to accomplish this. The Township will be
holding a special meeting on June 25 [th] to approve the agreement and required resolution.

Discussion was held on the current site plan with the Township. Retired City Manager Cook stated that
the site plan is no longer valid. They would have to come through our Planning Commission for approval.
He further stated that the road that goes out to Pontiac Trail is built to City standards. It was stated that it
will come into the City as R1-A as a blank slate.

Discussion was held on water and sewer to the church. Attorney Lee stated that the reason we can
detach it is that we cannot provide water and sewer to the church. Once detached they will have to look to
the Township for water and sewer. It should be to their benefit because they can connect to the sewer
that is currently being installed. Further discussion was held on the cost to the church to bring water and
sewer across Pontiac Trail if it were to remain in the City.

Council Member Kopkowski asked if we have to have an agreement for the detachment. Attorney Lee
stated that under State Law, municipalities can mutually agree as long as the detachment is back to the
community the property was taken, and there must not be water and sewer provided. Both those
qualifications have been met. This was the simplest way to handle this otherwise we would be in
negotiations over a franchise agreement for the Township to run their sewer lines, which would be very
difficult. Discussion was held on contact with the church and their best interests.

Councilman Kivell stated that he does understand their desire to protect the Trebor Lane residents, but we
have a capable Planning Commission that would be mindful of that. The Township is the one that needs
their water and sewer lines to go through, and he does not see why we are being restricted. Pam Weipert,
Planning Commission Chairperson stated that she did understand that it could come in with restrictions
and she is happy that there are relatively few restrictions. It will not burden the planning process. Further
discussion was held on the site planning process
