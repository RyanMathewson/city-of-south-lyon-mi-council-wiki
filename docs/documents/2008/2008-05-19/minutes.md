CITY OF SOUTH LYON
SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL MEETING

APRIL 19, 2008

Mayor Doyle called the meeting to order at 8:15 a.m.

PRESENT: Mayor Doyle
Council Members: Kivell, Kopkowski, Maida, Morelli, Selden and Wedell
Attorney Lee
Assistant Clerk/Treasurer Lynch

Interviews with candidates for the City Manager position as follows:

Joseph Merucci – 8:35 a.m. to 10:20 a.m.

Don Crawford – 10:25 a.m. to 11:35 a.m.

Reid Charles – 11:45 a.m. to 12:45 a.m.

Council adjourned for lunch at 12:50 p.m. and reconvened at 1:35 p.m.

Lynn Markland – 1:40 p.m. to 3:15 p.m.

A brief break was taken from 3:20 p.m. to 3:30 p.m.

Kevin O’Donnell 3:30 p.m. to 4:45 p.m.

Another break was taken between 4:45 p.m. until 4:55 p.m.

Discussion was held regarding the candidates.

ADJOURNMENT AT 6:15 P.M.
