1. Ordinance change; screening of roof top equipment 2 [nd] reading

Mayor Doyle asked Attorney Lee if there were any changes made. Attorney Lee stated there

2. E-Commerce Agreement with County

Mayor Doyle asked Attorney Lee if Oakland County agreed to the changes in the contract we
requested. Attorney Lee stated no, they will not change the contract. They do not interpret the
wording the way he does. They will not make changes to it and they state that other
communities sign the same agreement and they have no problems with it. Some discussion was
held regarding the language in the contract.

CM 8-4-08- CITY OF SOUTH LYON - APPROVAL OF E-COMMERCE AGREEMENT

To adopt the E-Commerce agreement with Oakland county

VOTE: MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY

2. Equipment Bids

Department Head Martin stated we had a used equipment sale. It is something he and
Department Head Renwick do every 2 years, we get a list of stuff not working and we run an ad
in the paper for two weeks. We had 7-8 people come in and check out the equipment and place
bids. We had a total of 13 items. We ended up with a total of $6242.77. Council Member
Kopkowski questioned if we put a list of the vehicles in the paper. Department Head Martin
stated no, we place an ad telling them what is up for bid. They are welcome to come out to the
plant and look at the vehicles. He further stated it would cost much more if we listed all the
information for each piece of equipment up for bid. Department Head Martin stated item #4 did
not sell, but he now believes he has an interested party so with council’s approval he is going to
run the ad again for another bid on that item.

CM-8-6-08- ACCEPTANCE OF USED EQUIPMENT BIDS

Motion by Maida, supported by Wedell

To approve the sale of used equipment and vehicles to the highest bidder as presented.

VOTE: MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY

the voting delegate.

5. Consent Agendas

City Manger Murphy stated that he put some information together for council to entertain to
move the meetings along a little quicker, some routine items can be put on a consent agenda.
Councilman Morelli stated he went through the information, but he is confused as to what kind
of information could be put on there. City Manager Murphy stated for example, you could put
the minutes, the approval of agenda, approval of bills and for a while we were getting a lot of
requests for block party road closures, things that don’t need a lot of discussion.  Council
Member Maida stated she would be willing to try it. Councilman Wedell stated he thought it is
an effective issue, but he doesn’t think the minutes and the bills should be included because we
always discuss those things, other than that, road closures and parades, some of those routine
things would be ok.  Council Member Kopkowski stated she is neither for nor against it. If
everyone wants to try it, she is ok with it. She further stated she doesn’t feel it takes that long to
go through the individual items, and sometimes the only way people know there is a block party
planned is by us discussing it at the meeting.  Councilman Kivell stated he does see how this

MANAGERS REPORT:

COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilman Morelli asked if the City still owns the old library. Mayor Doyle stated we do.
Council Member Maida stated she wanted to thank Department Head Renwick for all his help
today with her questions about the cemetery. She further stated Operation Injured soldier, we
made $10,000.00 for that event. She also stated that Depot Days is coming on September 6 [th] .
Councilman Kivell stated he wanted to thank the DPW for removing the hanging branches along
the easements. He further stated the street improvements that have taken place look great and he
is looking forward to seeing Reynolds Sweet improvement and the alley being squared away.
Councilman Kivell stated it would be valuable to have a discussion of what went right and what
went wrong, so the next project may go smoother.
