*[Page 2 of the original](https://www.southlyonmi.org/document_center/City%20Council/Agenda%20packet%20and%20Minutes/2008/minutes/minutes_120308.pdf?t=202108310733010#page=2)* (read by OCR; check the original)

City Manager Murphy stated this information should have been given out earlier, but the dates were off. He further stated that of the three options he would suggest that Council accept the Chester Street project. The drainage problem seems to be a reoccurring problem and needs to be done as soon as possible. He further stated the Whipple street project looks to be very costly. The third possibility is the high intensity signage. He stated those are currently being placed as needed, but all will have to be changed by 2012. City Manager Murphy further stated we could use the rest of the money for sidewalks. Some discussion was held regarding each of the projects. Department Head Renwick stated the drainage problem has been an ongoing issue for many years. He further stated the storm drain was installed back in the 50's or 60's, and it is clay tile. It has been repaired a number of times. Councilman Wedell stated the Chester Street project sounds more critical. Councilman Maida questioned the portion of Chester that crosses Woodland, and if that is located in Lyon Township. Renwick stated that portion is deeded to the City of South Lyon. Councilman Kivel questioned if the repairs have been close to the sides of the road or the middle. Renwick stated the storm sewer is only a few feet below the road and the problems have been in the middle of the road.

### CM 12-3-08- RESOLUTION APPROVING 2009 CDBG APPLICATION FOR THE CHESTER STORM SEWER IMPROVEMENTS FOR $53,365.00
