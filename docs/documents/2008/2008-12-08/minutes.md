## CITIZENS SUGGESTIONS:

## OLD BUSINESS: None

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Approval to close City Offices

Mayor Doyle stated that we have a request to close City Hall the day after Christmas and the day after New Years. This happened five years ago due to the timing.

Councilman Selden asked if someone was called in would they be paid overtime. City Manager Murphy stated that they would not be paid overtime, but rather given an additional day at a later date. Police Department personnel will be handled as it had been in the past. Those officers who worked their scheduled work would be given an additional eight hours at a time it would not hurt the department.

It was stated that this will be for this year only due to how the days fall.

Council Member Maida stated that she did not know about the household battery drop off at City Hall. City Manager Murphy has asked that it be put in the paper. We have filled up a bucket already and RRRASOC will come in and take them away. There is no cost to us.

## MANAGER'S REPORT

City Manager Murphy discussed the President-elect's Saturday speech and the biggest infrastructure economic stimulus package. We are keeping an eye on that as far as our water, sewer, bridges, roads, etc. He discussed some future projects. If the money comes through we should be ready to get some things done to our infrastructure.

Discussion was held on some of the design engineering completed as well as the water and sewer studies being completed.

## COUNCIL COMMENTS:

Council Member Maida thanked the DPW for the great job they have been doing with the snow removal and keeping the bike trail clean. They also did a great job with the holiday decorations. She reminded everyone of the Holiday Spectacular this Saturday and reviewed the schedule of events.

Councilman Wedell wished everyone a happy holiday.

Councilman Selden stated that it is good to be back after his recent illness. He stated that he was pleased with the things happening around town.

Council Member Kopkowski questioned the issue with the non-profit for the Injured Soldiers. City Manager Murphy stated that he did receive 5013C document from Jack Renwick. He will be meeting with Mr. Renwick who will be dropping off information that connects the two organizations.

Council Member Kopkowski stated that there has been a lot of information in the news regarding what communities are doing with the on-set of winter so early. She asked what the situation is with our salt. Superintendent Renwick stated that we will be monitoring it, and if it gets to a point as we did last year, we will be cutting down on the salting and concentrating on the intersections. At this point in time we are okay and we have been assured that there is plenty of salt for us.

Councilman Kivell congratulated Maryann Mihalic, Diane Roest and Gary Childs for being elected to the Chamber Board.

Councilman Kivell stated that last week's craft show was very successful and very well attended. The downtown looked very nice and everyone was having a good time.

Councilman Kivell stated that in relation to the Operation Injured Soldiers issue, it would be nice in the years to come that instead of waiting until days before the event that the issues are resolved up front.

Councilman Kivell wished everyone a great holiday, our neighbors, employees and residents of the City.
