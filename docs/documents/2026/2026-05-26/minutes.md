### 2. Discussion only-city credit card limit

Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated she and the City Clerk have been working on this for the last few weeks. We have had a relationship with PNC for many years and we currently use their credit card which has a $10,000 limit for the City Manager and Department Heads to use. We have had issues with the fact they don't offer an online option so we can't go online and we have to wait for a paper statement to reconcile that account. She further stated we have had a problem with remitting payments because we have to pay by check, and mail it to a lockbox, and sometimes the payment will sit in the lockbox for weeks, which makes it difficult for the city. We have been doing some research, and Huntington offers a corporate credit card with an online function so we can remit payment for the credit card statement by a transfer. She further stated that Huntington's lowest limit for their credit card is $25,000. She further stated they can offer us the ability to control a limit on each of the four cards. The city overall limit would be the $25,000. Councilmember Hansen stated he appreciates this being looked into, and he is happy to have it more technologically forward as to set different limits for different cardholders. He further stated it also gives her the ability as an emergency increase. He thanked her for her efforts. She further stated when someone leaves that is a card owner, we can cancel their card online. Further discussion was held regarding the credit card limit. It was the consensus of Council to approve the higher credit card limit and to add the Water and Sewer Superintendent and DPW Superintendent as card holders.

### 4. Patching project

DPW Superintendent Archey stated the DPW is requesting the services of a qualified company to perform flex seal spray injection patch work. The spray injection project will address multiple areas throughout the city that are in need of patch repairs. This process will provide a more durable and effective alternative to the continual cold patch currently being performed in targeted locations. He stated the project will focus primarily on higher-traffic areas in order to improve roadway conditions and extend the life of the pavement until full paving and reconstruction projects can be completed. We have available funds between local and major streets of $30,000 to complete this project. This will equate to about 2 tons of material. DPW Superintendent Archey stated through his research he has found one company that performs the flex seal spray injection method and is capable of completing a project of this scale. He
