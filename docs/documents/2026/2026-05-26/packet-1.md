# Rules of Conduct for Public Comment at Council Meetings $ ^{*} $

Members of the public may speak at a Council meeting upon recognition by the Mayor. Public comment may only occur during periods designated on the agenda for public comment or a public hearing. A person may speak for up to two (2) minutes during each of the two public comment periods on agenda items or non-agenda items. Waivers of the time requirement may only be granted in the discretion of the Mayor, and waivers to speak at a time other than a designated comment period may only be granted by the Council. Any person wishing to make a presentation longer than two minutes or requiring audio-visual equipment is asked to contact the City Clerk requesting to appear on a future agenda.

A person may only address Council from the podium. Only one person may occupy the podium at a time. All remarks are to be directed to the Mayor and Council. Speakers are not to engage in direct dialog with other meeting attendees.

Any person who violates the Rules of Conduct, disturbs the peace at a meeting, and/or interferes with the meeting may be warned, ordered to be seated, removed, and/or ticketed.

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Video Franchise Agreement

City Manager Baaki explained that this is a renewal uniform video services local franchise agreement that the city received from Direct TV. The city attorney has reviewed it. It is a boilerplate agreement issued by the State of Michigan, and local government franchising entities have no authority to modify them. Under state law, if the city fails to take action within 30 days to approve the franchise it is automatically deemed approved. Under state law, the city cannot charge Direct TV anymore than it charges any other cable operator with franchises in the city. The city's current franchise fee with Direct TV (previously AT&T) and other cable providers with franchises in the city is 5%. The city's current PEG fee with other cable providers is zero. Councilmember Dilg stated the way it reads is we have to approve it, or it's going to be approved anyway. City Manager Baaki confirmed that is true.

# PROCLAMATION HONORING

## Tim McGillen

WHEREAS, Tim McGillen, a veteran firefighter with more than 25 years of service to the City of South Lyon; and

WHEREAS, the South Lyon City Council along with the entire community, would like to honor the admirable contributions that Tim McGillen has made during his firefighting career; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Stephen B. Kennedy, Mayor of the City of South Lyon, and on behalf of the South Lyon City Council, hereby commend the outstanding service performed by Tim McGillen during his firefighting career. We wish him all the very best in his retirement.

The Department of Public Works is requesting the services of a qualified company to perform flex seal spray injection patch work. The spray injection project will address multiple areas throughout the City that are in need of patch repairs. This process will provide a more durable and effective alternative to the continual cold patch currently being performed in targeted locations.

The project will focus primarily on higher-traffic areas in order to improve roadway conditions and extend the life of the pavement until full paving and reconstruction projects can be completed.
