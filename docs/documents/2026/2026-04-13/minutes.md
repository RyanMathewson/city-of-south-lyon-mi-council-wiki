### 1. Resolution opposing Michigan House Bills

City Manager Baaki stated the Michigan Legislature has introduced Michigan House Bills 5529, 5530, 5331 and 5532, along with anticipated companion legislation require significant changes to local zoning and land use regulations that would preempt local zoning authority by imposing statewide requirements. He further stated this is a resolution to oppose the Michigan House Bills. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated there are many communities and the MML opposing these house bills. In her opinion it is a very large step towards central planning instead of allowing municipal and local planning by the decision makers that were elected to represent the communities they serve. The package of bills would take away the vote of everyone in the City of South Lyon, every planning commission member, every planner and every Councilmember. Everyone would lose their vote on how they want their city to look. She further stated that is central planning, the State would tell us what kind of housing we will have in our city. They want to tell us where to put the housing, and what the setbacks will be. They would allow manufactured homes and duplexes in subdivisions that are already developed. They would restrict parking. She finds it interesting that people that don't live in our community, been to our community, and don't have an interest in our community, are now telling the good voters that are paying property taxes now want to tell us how they want to solve the affordable housing issue. The uniqueness of our community and thousands of other communities throughout the state will be destroyed by central planning and central zoning. We need to understand the State wants to come in and get rid of our zoning laws pertaining to various parts of housing. So, the individual of communities will be controlled by Lansing. It will be destroyed by central planning and will erase the community character and the diversity that we have. She further stated she can only imagine what Frankenmuth would look like 15 years from now if the package of bills goes through. Communities have a right to decide through their elected individuals, and those here tonight engaging in dialogue. We want to hear how the residents want the community to be. There is no one here tonight listening to us from Lansing. They are not listening. She then stated she is deeply opposed to this process because she is deeply opposed to government intervention in her life and she doesn't like central planning. The housing affordability is complex and should be solved at the local level. South Lyon is the best example of housing that effects all demographics and incomes. ### 3. East and West secondary clarifier replacement

Water and Wastewater Superintendent Beason stated they are requesting approval for the replacements of the east and west secondary clarifiers. The clarifiers are essential in the settling process as part of the biological treatment at the wastewater plant. The two existing 60-foot clarifiers were installed in 2003 as part of a capital improvement plan and are showing signs of significant wear and deterioration. This project will include the replacement of the entire sludge collector mechanism, structural steel components, rehabilitation of the bridge, railings and repairs to any damaged concrete. This is a scheduled purchase for the 2026-2027 fiscal year budget as a capital improvement from line item 592-557-972.
