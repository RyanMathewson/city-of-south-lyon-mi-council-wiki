## NEW BUSINESS

1. Local Road Project- Jean Road

DPW Superintendent Archey stated as part of the 2026 Road Improvement Program and South Lyon's ongoing commitment to improving local roadway infrastructure, the city solicited competitive bids for Eagle Heights. A total of 11 bids were received, ranging from $1.7 million to $2.3 million. Upon review and evaluation of all submissions, Federal Paving Inc. was determined to be the lowest responsible bidder. In addition, they have agreed to hold their unit prices for the upcoming 2026 Jean Road project. In recognition of this agreement, city staff is recommending that City Council waive the formal bid process and authorize contracting with Federal Paving, under the same unit pricing structure established for the 2025 Eagle Heights project. This approach will provide cost stability, ensure continuity of work, and streamline project coordination for the city's ongoing road improvement program.
