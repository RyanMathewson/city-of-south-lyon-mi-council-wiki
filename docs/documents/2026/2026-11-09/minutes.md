##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION-}} $ Downtown

DDA Director Abeid stated over the past two weeks she has been getting up to speed on the DDA's existing operations and building relationships and trying to move forward with some immediate priorities related to downtown events and partnerships. She then stated she was able to introduce herself to all the board members and committee members. She has met with the design committee and the economic vitality members to discuss their priorities for the upcoming months. She further stated she's been going through the DDA's strategic plans and reading past minutes for meetings. She then stated she's been going through the master plan and the zoning ordinance to understand current initiatives and to understand what those opportunities are moving forward. She then stated she has been working with the Promotions Committee and city staff preparing for the last 2 events in December just tying up some loose ends. It includes coordinating with committee leadership and trying to learn how these events have been run up to date so she can jump in early next year with the events and setting the event list for 2027. She then stated she has been working on a shared content calendar across all of our communications channels, so that our social media, website, newsletters and event postings. We are trying to make sure we are being as transparent and inviting as possible. When it comes to downtown and making sure all the business owners are aware of all the resources we offer and all the ways we're trying to lay solid groundwork for them. She then stated above all, over the last 2 weeks has been about building those relationships. DDA Director Abeid stated she has met with the Department Heads, the Planning and Zoning staff and the building staff. She has started connecting with all the business owners, including local and regional partners, including the Chamber, Main Street Oakland County, SEMCOG and she attended a Cultural Arts meeting. All of the conversations are helping her see where we're at and to understand what our short- and long-term priorities are going into the new year. She stated she has more meetings in the upcoming weeks, and she'll keep Council updated. She further stated she is up to speed on the active grants and future funding opportunities for Lafayette Park, including Main Street Oakland County and Oakland County Parks programs. ## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Oakland County Parks Community Park and Trail Capital Grant Program Agreement Amendment

City Manager Baaki stated that on May 4th 2022, the city entered into an agreement with the Oakland County Parks and Recreation Commission (OCPRC) who has appropriated monies in the amount of $100,000 to the City of South Lyon in exchange for work to be performed to develop Lafayette Park, A.K.A Downtown South Lyon Park, OCPRC project #22-13. The agreement was extended February 28th 2025 by the previous City Manager, for an additional one year, at which time an amendment was signed. OCPRC is requiring a second amendment to the agreement be signed by the current City Manager in order to meet the grant program requirements. This current amendment would extend the term of the agreement to May 3rd, 2027.

# CITY OF SOUTH LYON

## PROCLAMATION HONORING

## JEANETTE BENSON - VOLUNTEER OF THE YEAR AWARD 2026

WHEREAS, Jeanette Benson has served the Friends of the Salem-South Lyon District Library for nearly 30 years, serving as a member of the Friends of the Library Board, as Secretary, as Vice President and as President, and

WHEREAS, Jeanette played a crucial role in establishing the beautiful gardens at the library, developing a team of 30 volunteers from the community including herself, and making it a focal point and place of beauty and peace for the community to enjoy, and

WHEREAS, Jeanette also serves as the Friends of the Library Book Room Coordinator, leading a dedicated team of 17 volunteers as well as being responsible for the budgeting and financial reporting to the Friends Board, and

WHEREAS, Jeanette ensures the book room provides a place for community members to donate books for sale, with the funds providing significant financial support for library programming, resources, marketing, and communications, and

WHEREAS, Jeanette developed innovative ways to sell donated materials outside the library through online selling services such as Thrift-books, eBay and Amazon Seller, allowing the library to offer even more to users beyond the walls of the Salem-South Lyon District Library, and

WHEREAS, Jeanette has served our community in other capacities over the years as a volunteer at Active Faith, delivering food to those in need and at the St Joseph Catholic Parish bringing communion to the homebound, and

WHEREAS, Jeanette has proven to be a true Servant Leader whose traits include: strong communication, authenticity, integrity, compassion, empowerment, continuous improvement, and always putting others first, and

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Stephen B. Kennedy, Mayor of the City of South Lyon, do hereby declare that in recognition of her exemplary volunteer service to our community, Jeanette Benson shall be the recipient of this year's Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year Award.

Stephen B. Kennedy, Mayor
