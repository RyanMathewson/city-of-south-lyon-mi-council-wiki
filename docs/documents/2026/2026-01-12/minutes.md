Interim City Manager Baaki stated in September 2025, the City of South Lyon released a request for proposals for waste hauling services, and responses were due on November 3rd. The current contract with Priority Waste will expire June 2024. The city requested bids for a 5-year service period beginning July 1, 2026. Mike Csapo of RRASOC gave a presentation of the bids that were received. He stated when we began, we wanted to ensure we met the needs of the community as of July 2026, because the current contract expires June 2026. He then stated he worked with the City Attorney and City Manager to issue the RFP and was advertised on bid net. He stated we did receive some good proposals, and the low bidder is Priority Waste, the current provider. He stated we looked at different alternatives for service and we determined that continue service as is it is will be the best for the community. He stated there will be a market adjustment which is happening in all of Michigan. Markets in SE Michigan have been very repressed for several years and now there is a normal market adjustment happening. He further stated we spoke with city administration and the attorney with Priority Waste to ask questions about the company and their future. They answered the questions to our satisfaction.
