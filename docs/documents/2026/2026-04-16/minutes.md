## NEW BUSINESS

Mayor Kennedy opened the meeting giving background for the last year. Last September, city council gave a performance review for the previous City Manager. It was a positive review, with a substantial pay raise. The next morning, he announced he was retiring effective November 4th. In the few weeks that followed, our City Clerk Lisa Deaton stepped forward and identified some potential recruiters to locate a new City Manager. We selected a recruiter from the list and Council worked to find the skills and expertise we were looking for in the next City Manager, and the search began. He then stated the City Manager left on Election Day, truly one of the busiest and critical days of the city. As the days continued, the Department Heads managed their departments and coordinated with other department heads to address any issues for the benefit of our residents. The election took place and was certified, and the streets were plowed, water was supplied to the residents, wastewater was treated, ordinances were enforced, the streets were patrolled and the residents were protected and assisted by the Police and Fire Department. He further stated, it was made clear, we wouldn't find a new City Manager within 90 days, as required by the Charter. He then stated assistance was requested and an Interim City Manager was identified. Working with Lisa and Patricia, a contract was developed and approved by Council and offered and accepted by Doug Baaki. The recruiter finally brought their candidates to City Council at the end of January. Council interviewed the candidates, and decided to offer the position to Doug Baaki instead of any of the other candidates. He further stated again, working with Patricia and Lisa a contract was developed for the permanent City Manager and negotiations took place, and the offer was made and accepted by Doug at the end of February. In the months following the City Manager's departure, the department heads rose to meet the challenges and rose to meet the challenges seamlessly during that time. As we go through the budget this evening, there is a section in the budget for wages and salaries. Union positions in those departments are getting 4.5% raise for the upcoming year, except for PD which is getting 5.5%. As negotiated by the former City Manager. He then stated the budget calls for 4% raise for department heads. He stated he is recommending a 5% raise, minimally, for City Clerk Lisa Deaton, Finance and Benefit Administrator Patricia Tiernan, Water Superintendent Ron Beason, DPW Jeff Archey, Mike Carlson, Kim Rivera, Bonnie Unruh, and Cindy Conrad. He stated he hopes and agrees and sees what these individuals have done, not just this year, but last year, but every year they have held their position. He stated the increase from 4%-5% for these 8 individuals is $5,561 in wages, $978 in fringe benefits from the general fund. He stated the total difference between the 4% and the 5% raise is a total of $7,787. Councilmember Kurtzweil thanked the mayor for being extremely insightful and caring.
