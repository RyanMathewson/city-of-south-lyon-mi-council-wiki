## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Conrad stated since the last council meeting, they have had 56 calls. 37 EMS, 3 false alarms, 10 public assist, 4 hazardous conditions, 1 assist to law enforcement and 1 vehicle fire. She then stated she was able to meet Tom Barrett last Thursday night, and he was late arriving, but Councilmember Kurtzweil made sure she was able to meet and speak with him for his support for the grant, and he said they already cut some out, so we are still being considered. Chief Conrad then reviewed her annual report. She then pointed out that there are new national reporting system and incidents are recategorized, so she will be moving some things around. She then stated page 5 shows the incidents by month and incidents by year going back to 2013. She further stated they had a 14% increase in calls from 2024 to 2025. Partly due to the large number of smoke alarms they received from the grant. She stated lift assists had a 52% increase as well, and overall, the department has had 215% increase since 2013. She then pointed out the organizational chart and she said it reflects the largest number of staff our FD has ever had in at least 20 years. She stated she is proud to say that and she has been turning away applicants which is really never heard of. She wants to stay cautiously optimistic because it is a paid on and part time call department for firefighters, and that number could change at any time. She then pointed out pages 11-13 reflect honors and recognitions and she is very proud of the individuals because they have gone above and beyond for the community and the department. Chief Conrad then pointed out the different divisions that are part of the Fire Department and they all work together to make sure the department works together seamlessly. She then stated they have attended a lot of community events which are also reflected in the report. She further stated their first goal is to continue to provide the highest level of emergency response and customer service to the community with a focus on professionalism, efficiency and compassion. She further stated training and readiness will always play front and center in our operations and leadership and professional growth are her favorites and she will continue to support her staff to continue to learn and grow. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she doesn't get to see a lot of department heads in action, but she had a great opportunity to see the professionalism when you were addressing a United States Congressman. She and City Manager Baaki were great and they were very impressed. She stated other people thought the same and she is very proud of both of them. Councilmember Squires stated he could see the training and readiness and he wants to give her kudos for always making sure our firemen are trained and he was very impressed.

She then stated recently our Officers found a couple of youths riding their e-bikes on the sidewalks, and one had been warned before. The 14-year-old was referred to Youth Assistance and for further action. The mother said they knew better not to ride on the sidewalk.

## NEW BUSINESS

1. Renew contract with Novi to provide dispatch services

Chief Unruh stated the current contract with the City of Novi to provide dispatch services to the City of South Lyon will expire on June 30, 2026. The City of Novi would like to enter a new five-year agreement, for the period of July 1, 2026-June 30, 2031 for the City of Novi to provide dispatch services to the City of South Lyon at the following rates which reflect a 4% increase each year. Councilmember Squires asked how long the city has been using Novi dispatch. City Manager Baaki stated probably since the mid 80's. At one time in the 70's we had our own dispatch, and found it to be very cost prohibitable. Novi does a great job for us, and we were expecting possibly a 5% increase, so we are very happy with the 4% increase each year. Location wise, it works great because we can lodge prisoners in their jail instead of driving them to Pontiac. They will also arraign them for us as well. CM 3-3-26 MOTION TO APPROVE AGREEMENT
