### 2. Purchase of 5 Motorola radios

Chief Conrad stated their department has 15 operational Motorola radios available for 20 non-officer staff members. This creates a significant communication and safety gap during emergency responses, as not all personnel are equipped with a radio. The lack of adequate communication tools can hinder coordination, delay response efforts, and increase risk on scene. Acquiring 5 additional Motorola radios will bring the department a 1:1 radio-to-person ratio, enhancing operational effectiveness, accountability and safety for all members during incidents. Chief Conrad stated funding was approved in the FY 2026/2027 budget for the purchase of 5 portable radios in the amount of $53,000. We are asking for approval to lock in the price as it reflects $750.00 off per radio and the discount ends June 20th. Purchase will not be made until after the new fiscal budget year begins. Mayor Kennedy thanked Chief Conrad for bringing this to Council and getting the $750.00 off per radio. Councilmember Hansen agreed, and thanked Chief Conrad for getting this to Council and saving tax payer dollars.

### 4. CLEMIS participation agreement

Chief Unruh stated the Police and Fire Departments utilize the Oakland County CLEMIS (courts and law enforcement management system). She explained the Police Department uses CLEMIS for report management, crash reporting, citations, impounds, activity logs, evidence and property management, Talon and LEIN. She further stated the Fire Department uses CLEMIS for fire report management and to calculate payroll hours. For many years CLEMIS operated as a group of law enforcement agencies collaborating with Oakland County to share data and information technology services. The CLEMIS Authority was created to enable Oakland County to transfer to a separate legal entity most of the operations and services the county IT Department provided to CLEMIS agencies. Councilmember Hansen asked what are the benefits of being part of CLEMIS for the public's knowledge. Chief Unruh stated it is a partnership benefit. It's also important for report management, and we are connected with other communities in Oakland County. She stated when she runs background checks for solicitors for a criminal history and sometimes people aren't convicted of something. But when she runs it through CLEMIS she can see if they have been suspects or had contacts. She further stated if a Detective is looking at someone for an investigation, and they don't have a criminal history, but they're doing similar acts in other communities they can connect the dots. Chief Conrad stated the majority of Fire Departments in Oakland County use CLEMIS. She stated we use it for fire reporting and payroll collecting. We don't have any sensitive information like social security numbers or anything. When they get dispatched to a call, it automatically goes into CLEMIS and it generates the dollar amount for the person that was on that call. It is more complicated than what she can explain right now, but it's simple to use and one of the cheapest they can use. Councilmember Dilg asked if the Fire Department can use and not see the criminal history. Chief Unruh stated that is correct. She further stated when you sign in to CLEMIS, it is on record that you signed on and what you searched for. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked about the databank. She stated a lot of the solicitors are not from the State of Michigan. So, if there is someone that is soliciting and nothing comes back from the background check in Michigan, how can you check further. Chief Unruh stated they will do an ICHAT search. She further stated that is a free service to us. She further stated she does everything she can to make sure we don't have people soliciting with criminal backgrounds, but if someone is from another state, she doesn't have the ability to check if they have been suspects, but through ICHAT she can see if they've been convicted.
