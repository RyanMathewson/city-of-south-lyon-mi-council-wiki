Ryan Mathewson of 884 N. Mill stated he wanted to bring up the discussion from a previous meeting about updating the security cameras. He stated it is a positive step to upgrade infrastructure. ### 3. East and West secondary clarifier replacement

Water and Wastewater Superintendent Beason stated they are requesting approval for the replacements of the east and west secondary clarifiers. The clarifiers are essential in the settling process as part of the biological treatment at the wastewater plant. The two existing 60-foot clarifiers were installed in 2003 as part of a capital improvement plan and are showing signs of significant wear and deterioration. This project will include the replacement of the entire sludge collector mechanism, structural steel components, rehabilitation of the bridge, railings and repairs to any damaged concrete. This is a scheduled purchase for the 2026-2027 fiscal year budget as a capital improvement from line item 592-557-972. The equipment will need to be ordered prior to July 1st 2026, due to manufacturing and deliver time restraints. We received four competitive bids and recommend awarding the project to Artisan Contracting of MI, LLC in the amount of $1,125,347.00 Councilmember Squires asked what the clarifiers do. Water and Wastewater Superintendent Beason stated it is a settling process for biological treatment. He further stated the wasted is transferred to another tank, then it gets thickened and used for fertilizer. He stated is a 24/7 days process and never turns off. It is crucial to our wastewater treatment. Councilmember Hansen asked about the difference in bid prices. Water and Wastewater Superintendent Beason stated Artisan is new to the game, they aren't a big company, and he recently had them come out and do some work for us. # AGENDA NOTE New Business #1

MEETING DATE: April 27, 2026

PERSON PLACING ITEM ON AGENDA: Patricia Tiernan, Finance and Benefit Administrator

AGENDA TOPIC: Cost Participation Agreement for the Construction of Pontiac Trail at 9 Mile Road to Lottie Street

EXPLANATION OF TOPIC: The City of South Lyon and the Board of County Road Commissioners of Oakland County in cooperation with the Michigan Department of Transportation are looking at entering a Cost Participation Agreement for the construction of Pontiac Trail at 9 Mile Road to Lottie Street. The City of South Lyon shall contribute a total of $1,484,740 towards the project. Funding will include Tri-Party Program Funds (City of South Lyon, Road Commission of Oakland County, Oakland County). The Tri-Party Program funds portion will be $83,686 and the City will directly fund $1,401,054.

MATERIALS ATTACHED AS SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS: Cost Participation Agreement and Exhibit A Tri-Party Program Cost Participation Breakdown.

POSSIBLE COURSES OF ACTION: Approve or not approve the Cost Participation Agreement between the City of South Lyon and Board of Road Commissioners of the County of Oakland for the construction of Pontiac Trail at 9 Mile Road to Lottie Street for total funding by the City of South Lyon in the amount of $1,484,740.

SUGGESTED MOTION: Motion by ___, supported by

___ to approve the Cost Participation Agreement between the City of South Lyon and Board of Road Commissioners of the County of Oakland for the construction of Pontiac Trail at 9 Mile Road to Lottie Street for total funding by the City of South Lyon in the amount of $1,484,740.

# COST PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT CONSTRUCTION Pontiac Trail 9 Mile Road to Lottie Street City of South Lyon Board Project No. 56331

This Agreement, made and entered into this ___ day of _____, 2026, by and between the Board of County Road Commissioners of the County of Oakland, Michigan, hereinafter referred to as the BOARD, and the City of South Lyon, hereinafter referred to as the COMMUNITY, provides as follows:

WHEREAS, the BOARD and the COMMUNITY, in cooperation with the Michigan Department of Transportation, hereinafter referred to as MDOT, have programmed the road reconstruction, drainage improvements, box culvert rehabilitation, traffic signal modernization, street lighting, rail road crossing protection, concrete curb and gutter, sidewalk, curb ramps, watermain, sanitary sewer, guardrail, and permanent signing/markings along Pontiac Trail from 9 Mile to Lottie Street, as described in Exhibit "A", attached hereto, and made a part hereof, which improvements involve roads under the jurisdiction of the BOARD and within the COMMUNITY, which improvements are hereinafter referred to as the PROJECT; and

WHEREAS, the estimated total cost of the PROJECT is $13,226,205; and

WHEREAS, the BOARD has entered into an agreement with MDOT in Contract No. 26-5023 for partial funding of the PROJECT with federal program dollars in the amount of $10,241,438; and

WHEREAS, the BOARD, as the requesting party therein, will be the party financially responsible to MDOT to bear all costs of the PROJECT in excess of federal funds, hereinafter referred to as the LOCAL SHARE; and

WHEREAS, the COMMUNITY'S share of said LOCAL SHARE involves certain designated and approved Tri-Party Program funding in the amount of $251,056, which shall be paid through

<a id="p48"></a>
*[Page 48 of the original](https://www.southlyonmi.org/April%2023%202026%20Online%20Copy_REVISED%20Redacted.pdf?t=202604241359370#page=48)* (read by OCR; check the original)

equal contributions by the BOARD, the COMMUNITY, and the Oakland County Board of Commissioners, hereinafter referred to as the COUNTY; and

WHEREAS, all the parties hereto have reached a mutual understanding regarding the cost sharing for the LOCAL SHARE and wish to commit that understanding to writing in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and in conformity with applicable law, it is hereby agreed between the COMMUNITY and the BOARD that:

1. The COMMUNITY approves of the PROJECT, declares its public necessity, and authorizes the BOARD and MDOT to perform or cause to be performed, and complete the PROJECT along with all administration in reference thereto.

2. The PROJECT shall include construction engineering and total payments to the contractor. Any costs incurred by the BOARD prior to this agreement date shall be allowable.

3. The estimated total LOCAL SHARE cost of $2,984,767 shall be applied in the following order:

a. Tri-Party Program funding shall be applied first, simultaneously and proportionately.

b. The COMMUNITY shall contribute $1,484,740 which includes Tri-Party funds in the amount of $83,686 and a CONTRIBUTION of $1,401,054.

- The Tri-Party Program funds of $251,056 includes $101,056 from fiscal year 2026, $50,000 from fiscal year 2027, $50,000 from fiscal year 2028, and $50,000 from fiscal year 2029 which has not yet been announced. If these funds are not available, the project costs will be funded 100% by the COMMUNITY.

<a id="p49"></a>
*[Page 49 of the original](https://www.southlyonmi.org/April%2023%202026%20Online%20Copy_REVISED%20Redacted.pdf?t=202604241359370#page=49)* (read by OCR; check the original)

- The COMMUNITY shall use $412,000 of their CONTRIBUTION towards the Right turn lane, Sanitary sewer lining, and Utility work.

- The COMMUNITY shall use $250,000 of their CONTRIBUTION toward the advance construction of the PROJECT and is subject to refund after October 1, 2029, of the beginning of fiscal year 2030.

c. The BOARD shall contribute $1,416,341 which includes the CONTRIBUTION of $1,332,657 and Tri-Party funds in the amount of $83,684.

d. Any LOCAL SHARE costs above $2,984,767 will be funded 100% by the BOARD.

4. Upon execution of this agreement, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COMMUNITY in the amount of $33,685 (being 100% of the COMMUNITY'S FY26 Tri-Party contribution) as well as an invoice for $1,401,054 (being 100% of their contribution).

- After February 1, 2027, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COMMUNITY in the amount of $16,667 (being 100% of the COMMUNITY'S FY27 Tri-Party contribution).

- After February 1, 2028, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COMMUNITY in the amount of $16,667 (being 100% of the COMMUNITY'S FY28 Tri-Party Program contribution).

- After February 1, 2029, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COMMUNITY in the amount of $16,667 (being 100% of the COMMUNITY'S FY29 Tri-Party Program contribution).

5. Upon execution of this agreement and approval by the COUNTY, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COUNTY in the amount of $33,685 (being 100% of the COUNTY'S FY26 Tri-Party contribution).

- After February 1, 2027, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COUNTY in the amount of $16,667 (being 100% of the COUNTY'S FY27 Tri-Party contribution).

- After February 1, 2028, the BOARD shall submit an invoice to the COUNTY in the amount of $16,667 (being 100% of the COUNTY'S FY28 Tri-Party Program contribution).
