### 2. Plante Moran engagement letter

Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated the City of South Lyon will engage Plante & Moran, PLLC to perform an audit of the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026 financial statements, assist in drafting the financial statements and assist related notes. The approximate cost of the engagement will be 77,500. The proposed service agreement includes a 3% increase from the previous year's contract. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she has a concern about paragraph 17 in regards to recording conversations. She asked if there has been a problem. Finance and Benefit Administrator stated she spoke to them about that, but they said their legal department decided to add this to all of their agreements, and she doesn't think it has anything to do with the city. She said it is going into all their engagement letters. Councilmember Kurtzweil speculated that it sounds like they had a private conversation with someone and it came back and they didn't like it. She found it unusual. At least they understand that anything in a public meeting can be recorded. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked what  $ 3^{rd} $ parties would they be using outside of the US. Each poppy is carefully assembled by disabled veterans and features the red petals, the green stem center, along with a label.
