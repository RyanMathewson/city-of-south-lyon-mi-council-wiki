##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $- Downtown

DDA Director Rivera stated the new DDA website is www.downtownsouthlyon.com and it is currently live for the merchants and the DDA board to review. We will be making a big marketing splash for that this week. She stated it is going well; she has only had a few comments so far. She then stated she has many food trucks and vendors signing up for the Farmers Market. She said they are getting many sponsors as well. She then reminded everyone of Carnivale, which is this Saturday the 14th from 2pm - 5pm and it will coincide with the St. Patrick's Day Celebration. She stated there will be an 18-piece big band there and they are hoping for good weather. She then stated they can always use volunteers to help decorate. She further stated she and the DDA chairperson attended the Downtown Michigan Association Conference in Lansing to advocate for our city and the downtown. She further stated they had successful meetings with Jason Morgan, Senate Runestad staff member, while advocating for downtown South Lyon, the tiff, and the Pontiac Trail Road project and CSX, and hoping it can work as a nice flow so we don't have to shut the road down twice. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated the videos on Facebook regarding the various businesses are great, they are upbeat and the music is great. She has heard a lot of good comments from people as well. She then asked about the breakfast/brunch store that is supposed to be opening. DDA Director Rivera said she will stop in and check with them. Councilmember Dils stated the website looks great, she then asked how to get people to get people on the website. DDA Director Rivera said they will do a PR splash this week, a push for volunteers to get people involved with committees, and there is a link from the City's website to take people to the new site.

##  $ \underline{\text{FIRE CHIEF REPORT}} $

Unier Conrad stated since the last council meeting, they had 58 incidents, and 253 since the beginning of the year. She further stated of the 58 incidents, 46 EMS, 3 false alarms, 4 public service, 4 hazardous and 1 fire. She further stated she was able to get the grant submitted last week, along with 4 letters of support. She stated Tom Barrett will be in Lyon Township next week, and she plans on stopping in to speak with him on getting support to get the grant. Mayor Kennedy stated that is encouraging and he appreciates her getting the grant done on such short notice.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Unruh stated the SLPD assisted the Oakland County Sheriff's with an armed robbery at Tobacco Bills on Friday night. No one was hurt. She then stated the Citizens Police Academy started on March 4th and 14 people attended and 8 were from South Lyon. On March 11th they will be at the Wixom Police Department for crime scene investigation and there will be someone there from the Michigan Police Lab. She then stated the lights are still being installed, and they were able to sell the metal from the ceiling, but they are having trouble getting rid of the tube lighting because there is mercury in it. Mayor Kennedy stated there are hazardous waste collection events, and the next event is in Wixom next month. Chief Unruh stated after 29 years of service Officer Stroupe will be retiring on April 9th, as well as Officer Barbour will be retiring after 25 years on June 1st. Lieutenant Sederlund is retiring March 31st.
