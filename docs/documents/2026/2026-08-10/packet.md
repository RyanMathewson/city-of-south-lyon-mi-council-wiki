## WATER SUPERINTENDENT REPORT

Water Superintendent Beason stated that on Monday July 20th an operator that was performing their daily outside rounds found a leak on a ground storage tank at the water treatment plant. He stated he responded and confirmed a major leak on the million-gallon water holding tank, GST1. The leak is about 9 feet and there is a crack that extends vertically to the top of the tank. He stated they then isolated the tank and manually closed the valves and diverted the treated water to the 600,000-gallon holding tank. He further stated the million-gallon tank is off line and the water is manually being pumped out to waste. He then stated after the tank is empty, Preload will come out and perform an interior inspection. HRC will be assessing the exterior. The tank was designed and built by Preload in the 1980s. It is a 5" thick pre-stressed tank with a metal diaphragm. He stated he has contacted the engineering team and waiting for a response from Preload. He then explained that in 2022 the tank went through an interior and exterior rehab. That included lining the interior of the tank with approved water proof epoxy and repairing any exterior gunnite coating. What that means is with the one-million-gallon tank off line, we have lost 62% of our tank storage capacity. Since we found the leak, we have been monitoring the water usage levels and the tank levels via the SCADA system. He stated there is a significant increase in usage between 3am and 7am, which is normal for the dry summer months. But when you consider the 60% reduction in our treated water storage it becomes a concern. Superintendent Beason then stated the ground storage tank is full at 28.5 feet. He then read the attached bulletin regarding the watering on odd and even days. He then stated this is not a water ban or boil water alert. Just a restriction on irrigation to relieve the stress on the water system. We are continuing to monitor the water levels and the demand has dropped. He thanked
