### 4. FIRE STATION CEMENT REPLACEMENT

Fire Chief Conrad states that they are looking to replace the cement approach, the ambulance parking area, curbing and sidewalk. This area is severely deteriorated. Funding was approved in the FY 2026-2027 budget in the amount of $100,000.00. MAC Concrete Demolition was chosen to provide this service because the contractor submitted a comprehensive written contract that clearly defines the scope of work, including the exact dimensions of the concrete to be removed and replaced, the construction methods, project phasing, and warranty of work. This level of detail provides a clear understanding of the contractor's responsibilities and minimizes the potential for misunderstandings, change orders, or disputes regarding the work to be completed. The recommended contractor also processes completing the project in two phases with a five-day concrete cure period. The longer cure time is expected to produce a more durable finished product. Chief Conrad attached 3 quotes for Council to review. MAC Concrete Demolition, $46,377.00, Copeland Paving, Inc., $46,884.00, and Boley Construction, $44,113.00. Councilmember Popa thanked all of the information Chief Conrad turned in. Councilmember Hansen stated the information was very detailed. The Little Free Library

WHEREAS Stories and adventures from books have provided enjoyment and wonder for readers of all ages from generation to generation... and

WHEREAS The Little Free Library of the Salem-South Lyon District Library promotes literacy, community and free access to children's books... and

WHEREAS The Little Free Library of the Salem-South Lyon District Library and its band of volunteer stewards have enthusiastically maintained the restocking of books and the Library's custom-built structure... and

NOW, THEREFORE I, Stephen B. Kennedy, Mayor of South Lyon, do hereby congratulate The Little Free Library for providing 10 years of enjoyment and education to the South Lyon Community and wish them many more to come.
