Presentation: Judge David Law- Update on the 52-1 $ ^{st} $ District Court

Judge David Law, Judge Travis Reed, and Judge Robert Bond were all present. Judge Law gave a presentation of the different processes of the  $ 52^{nd} $ 1st District Court. Judge Bondy stated they handle civil cases that involve money; they also handle landlord/tenant issues, land contract forfeitures, zoning cases, and criminal cases as well. Judge Bondy stated they also hear misdemeanor cases which are retail frauds, domestic violence, embezzlement, as well as narcotic possessions. He further stated a lot of the crimes deal with drugs and alcohol. He further stated the City of South Lyon had 73 traffic misdemeanors, 819 traffic tickets, which are non-criminal. He stated there were 110 total non-traffic misdemeanors, and 30 drinking and driving tickets of different levels, and 28 felony matters. He stated the total filings for the City of South Lyon was 1,062. Judge Reeds stated even though 1,062 total filings sound like a lot, the bottom line is they handle approximately 40,000 filings a year. Therefore it is really a testament to how safe the City of South Lyon really is. Judge Reeds stated anyone is welcome to come and watch the inner workings of the court system. He further stated the court has some specialty court programs. He stated a specialty court program is something they are very proud of. The specialty court programs are a very constructive, intensive, specialized probationary period involving a community partnership between prosecutors, judges, local law enforcement, probation officers to address repeat offenders. We don't want people to come back, most people do not, but there are a small percentage of people that offend over and over again. This program gives them the tools and intense supervision some need to ensure they do not re-offend, and it is highly successful. Judge Law stated they are also proud to have a veteran's specialty court. Many veterans come back from oversees with mental health issues, or substance abuse issues, and this is a team approach to try to help the veteran get through whatever their issues are.

## Presentation: Bret Resagan- Oakland County Main Street program

Bret Resagan of the Oakland County Main Street Program gave a brief power point presentation regarding the Oakland County Main Street Program. He stated the Main Street program is basically a program which is historic preservation based, community involved and economic development. He stated at one time this was an all or nothing program, now it is a three tier program under the County Executive and with the support of our county commissioners. He further stated we offer services for historic downtowns such as South Lyon. He stated this program tracks and reports progress throughout the year. Mr. Resagan stated the Main Street Program is a proven program and is used all over the United States. He further stated the main street approach is a management methodology. Mr. Resagan

## 2 ) Resolution Establishing Policies and Guidelines for Granting Exemption from Payment of Property Taxes

Clerk Deaton stated in 2008, Council adopted a resolution Establishing Policies and Guidelines for Granting Exemption from Payment of Property Taxes (Poverty Exemption) which incorporated and adopted by reference the Federal Poverty Income Standards as set annually by the United States Office of Management and Budget. This Resolution is approved by Council each year. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated the requirements currently state the applicant must submit prior year tax returns, but it may need to be specified to read that a signed and filed tax return must be filed with the application. Councilmember Kivell stated there was a complication with the December Board of Review because the paperwork from the County included the HUD guidelines as well as the Federal guidelines. He further stated he would like to look into that in the future. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated the resolution doesn't state the income level. Clerk Deaton stated the print out from the Federal Government is included with the application. Attorney Wilhelm stated we could revise the language to state a signed and filed tax return forms are required with the application. Councilmember Kivell stated he was surprised some of the required information was not asked for on the application. Clerk Deaton stated we have revised the application and it is much more detailed and requires much more information involving anyone else living in the home.

### VI. Manager's Report

City Manager Ladner stated she received a thank you letter from a teacher thanking Bob Martin for the tour he gave the students at the wastewater treatment plant.

Department Head Martin stated after they fixed the sink hole problem by Waters Edge and we introduced water flow, we found the water wasn't going anywhere. He further stated they found a break in the line. He stated on Saturday they did an emergency repair and it took approximately 8 hours. This line is the only line that carries sewage across the tracks to the wastewater treatment plant. He further stated we are in good shape right now. Councilmember Wedell asked if we will have another emergency any time soon. Department Head Martin stated that line has been in for a long time, sometime in the near future; we may need to look into another bore we can put in place for future use.

City Manager Ladner stated the strategic planning session is on the  $ 19^{th} $ of March, and she will let everyone know the location as soon as the contract is signed. City Manager Ladner stated we will need to schedule budget work sessions in March or April, because it needs to be approved by the second meeting in May. The consensus of Council is to schedule the Budget workshop meetings for April 9th and April 23rd.
