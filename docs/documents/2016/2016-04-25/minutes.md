## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Consider application for City of South Lyon quota Class C liquor license

Mark Jerant stated he recently moved to the area. He stated he has been in the restaurant business for 14 years and it appears the City is lacking a nicer date night type restaurant. Mr. Jerant stated he is proposing the corner coffee café location and he would like to open a bistro style restaurant. He further stated he has spoken with the current tenant regarding buying out his lease. Mr. Jerant stated there are a couple other locations in the City, but that would be his preference. Discussion was held regarding the floor space available for seating. Mr. Jerant stated he wants a smaller space for a more interactive experience. He further stated the outside seating area would be great as well. Councilmember Kivell stated he likes the smaller environments, but with this being our last quota license, he would like to see something that holds a lot more potential for being a draw for additional economic development that would be sprung around that entity. He further stated there are 11 escrow licenses that are available and he understands there is a cost difference between the quota license, but that quota license is one of the few lures we have to attract something more of a grand scale. Mr. Jerant stated the difference in cost of the quota license basically means whatever extra the escrow license costs, which is money not getting put into the business. He further stated you can put a contingency with the quota licenses stating the license has to stay in the City for a certain number of years, and that is an option. Councilmember Ryzyi thanked Mr. Jerant for coming out, and he agrees we need to have a nicer restaurant in the downtown to draw people out and people want to keep their money here, but that hasn't happened as much in the past.

### 2. Consider approval of Michigan Mutual Aid Box Alarm System (MI-MABAS) updated agreement

Chief Kennedy stated this is an agreement for Michigan Mutual Aid Box Alarm System. (MI-MABAS) He stated we fall under the Oakland County emergency management system. He further stated we would get the first 8 hours of free service. Chief Kennedy stated most counties are having a hard time funding the specialized teams, which would assist with technical rescue, and hazmat. He stated we have very few of such incidents in the City, but they have happened county-wide. Oakland County does have their own assets, but if the City had an incident, we could call in assets from the members of the MI-MABAS.
