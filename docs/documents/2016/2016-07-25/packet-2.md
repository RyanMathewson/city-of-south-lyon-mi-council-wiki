## MEMO

DATE: May 23, 2016

TO: City of South Lyon City Council

FROM: Pumpkinfest of the South Lyon Area Committee

RE: Wells Street closure request for September 23, 2016 from 9am - 12pm

Based on suggestions of SLPD and SLFD the Pumpkinfest of the South Lyon Area Committee is respectively requesting to close Well Street, from Lake Street to Detroit Street, from 9am to 12pm on September 23, 2016.
