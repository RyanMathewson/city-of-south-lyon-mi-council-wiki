the last meeting. He further stated they did away with the just cause from his original version, and
he will remain an at will employee. Attorney Wilhelm stated the severance will not be given if he
resigns or if he is terminated due to cause. He further stated this could set a precedent for future
Police Chiefs as well as other Department Heads. Attorney Wilhelm stated we cannot take the
termination right away from the City Manager because that would go against the Charter. Attorney

terminated without severance, again, this is similar to the City Manager’s contract.
