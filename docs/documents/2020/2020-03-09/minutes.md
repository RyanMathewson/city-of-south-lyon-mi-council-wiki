### 5. Establish the Commercial rehabilitation district

City Manager Zelenak stated City Council recently adopted a tax abatement policy for the community. This policy outlines the requirements for establishing a commercial rehabilitation district, and for approving a proposed tax abatement as outlined in Public Act 210 of 2005. At our City Council meeting on February 24th, we set a date and time for a public hearing per the request by the owner of the property, parcel 21-20-360-023 (RCA building) to establish a commercial rehabilitation district. Tonight, we held the public hearing at the beginning of the meeting in order for all residents and taxpayers to be afforded an opportunity to be heard on the subject. In order to establish a district, City Council must determine whether they feel the district meets the requirements of the act. A copy of a sample resolution is attached which covers the establishment of the rehabilitation district for the parcel in question. If City Council votes in favor of establishing the district, the county may still accept or reject the establishment of the district. Once the district is established, the property owner may file an application to the Clerk for a commercial rehabilitation exemption certificate. The Clerk shall provide written notification to all taxing jurisdictions that levy ad valorem property taxes of an additional application public hearing. The City will have 60 days after receipt of the application to either approve or disapprove the application. This will then be sent to the State Tax Commission. He stated he has attached the written request to establish the district for parcel 21-20-360-023. Councilmember Richards stated that property deserves consideration. It has been an eyesore for many years and the new owners goals are very ambitious, but one word of caution is he believes he intends on making it a restaurant and get a liquor license, but he thinks the church across the street from it will block the liquor license from happening.
