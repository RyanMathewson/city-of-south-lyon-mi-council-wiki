## OLD BUSINESS

1. Request for approval to submit resolution approving proposed charter amendment ballot proposition to the County Clerk

Attorney Hamameh stated Council authorized language to be presented to the Attorney General and the Governor's Office for approval before it is placed on the ballot. She stated the Attorney General's office has given tentative approval. She stated she spoke with someone from the Governor's Office and they are expecting the Governor to sign off on this Wednesday. The deadline is Tuesday the 11th, so she is suggesting we move forward with this because it is highly unlikely that the Governor will disagree with the language. Councilmember Dilg asked what will happen if it is rejected after it is submitted. Attorney Hamameh stated the AG's office and the Governor's office was surprised to hear that Oakland County said the deadline to submit or pull is 4:00 p.m. due to the fact there are many communities looking to do ballot propositions that aren't as far ahead as we are.

## NEW BUSINESS

1. Agreement for 390 S Lafayette Street preliminary geotechnical investigation (soil borings) with G2 Consulting Group of Troy, Michigan

Nate Mack, DDA Director stated the City of South Lyon has identified the parcel located at 390 S Lafayette St as a site for potential development in the master plan. He stated part of the development process is determining if whatever the soil is made of, if it can basically handle the weight of a building. He stated the company G2 will be drilling two holes that go 60 feet down and tests the soil at different intervals. He stated G2 was the lowest of the two bids we received. Councilmember Richards stated because it is municipally owned, we have to go through with this, and this is a cheap price, there is a lot of peat down there and the grade of the peat is top grade. He further stated it could cost us $200,000 for our use in the future. It may be to our advantage to get the solid dirt for our own use in the future.
