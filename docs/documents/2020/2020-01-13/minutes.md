Mr. Donohue stated the only item to discuss is the generic procedure of guidelines. It isn't appropriate to discuss any detailed of the proposed request. He stated there would be a public hearing and look at all the criteria to see if that person's request is deserving of the abatement. He stated there is a need for this regardless of the economy.
