# City of South Lyon City Council Budget Workshop April 16, 2025

Mayor Kennedy opened the meeting at 5:30 p.m.

Mayor Kennedy led those present in the Pledge of Allegiance

ROLL CALL

Roll Call: Mayor Kennedy and Councilmembers: Bogert, Dilg, Hansen, Kivell, Mosier and Kurtzweil Also present: City Manager Zelenak, Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan and Clerk/Treasurer Deaton

## NEW BUSINESS

1. Presentation of the 2025-2026 budget

City Manager Zelenak stated we had an average rate increase of 4%. He thanked all the staff for their work on their budget. He then stated we are spending down some fund balance for some capital improvements for this year. He then stated expenditures are similar to last year, with some increases for wages and benefits. We also have money for the city entrance signs and money for the Pontiac Trail Road project.

## ADMINISTRATION

Councilmember Mosier asked about the line item for Community Promotions and why the Volunteer Appreciation Party isn't mentioned in it and if we aren't going to do that anymore and why. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated it is a legal issue. She had more pressure from the auditors and the State of Michigan stating that municipalities cannot fund a volunteer appreciation event using tax payer dollars. Councilmember Mosier asked about donations from our vendors such as Plante Moran and CIB Planning. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated it would have to be 100% funded. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she agrees, it has always been that you can't use tax dollars, and the employee appreciation as far as she knows, there were never city dollars used for it, it was paid for by the vendors. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated it did go over the funds donated by the vendors. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated then don't offer tickets for drinks. She stated it should be looked at because it means a lot to the volunteers and especially when councilmembers are there and not in a formal setting and they can sit and meet with employees and community. It has always been a nice event and you just don't supply gifts or drink tickets. She stated you can always ask the businesses to donate gifts. City Manager Zelenak stated the vendors now donate to the Concerts in the Park.

Further discussion was held regarding the volunteer appreciation event. Mayor Kennedy stated there is $20,000 in the budget for social media for the Pontiac Trail roadwork. He then asked what is the city going to do to let the residents and business owners know about the project and that it is coming. City Manager Zelenak stated we will be using social media, flyers and we'll have informational meetings as well. He further stated we don't have enough information as of yet, but we will let everyone know when we get more information. Councilmember Dilg asked why the engineering services are so much higher. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated it is based on what we've been spending the last few years, and it is for things that HRC is charging us for that we don't necessarily have a cash bond for. There are a lot of smaller projects that occur and for a long time we weren't accounting for it in the right places, so this line item is where we added it to the budget. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked why the wages and salaries jumped up by $17,000. City Manager Zelenak stated that is based upon actual salaries.

and with a 4% increase. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated she looked at other communities and state wages, and 4% was the average. Mayor Kennedy stated the fringe benefits went up too. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated that it is based on what we are expecting as an increase for health insurance. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if we are putting additional money for OPEB. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated we have increased it by 3% increase from the previous year. She further stated we are still buying down the liability and we are at 50%. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she would like to have it paid off and be done with it. Further discussion was held regarding OPEB.

## POLICE DEPARTMENT

Chief Baaki stated we are projecting 4 people to retire in the next year. He further stated we are getting people on board, and the State has allocated money for individuals to go to the Police Academy. He further stated they cost between $7,000 and $9,000. He further stated he raised the line items for training and for uniforms. He further stated for the capital improvement line item, they are getting LED lights in both buildings and for the capital improvement line item, they are asking to buy another vehicle. He then stated they are looking at different camaras to be installed at 11 Mile, 9 Mile, Dixboro and Martindale and the cameras will read plate numbers. He then stated the cost is $3,000 per camera per year. Further discussion was held regarding the cameras. Mayor Kennedy asked if the equipment in the expedition will fit in the Tahoe. Chief Baaki stated they will have to order new equipment, only the lights can be moved to the new vehicle. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked about the increase in the OPEB healthcare. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated that it is for 18 active police employees and there is money built in for the 6 retirees and the stipend they will receive.

## FIRE DEPARTMENT

City Manager Zelenak stated the Fire Department will be adding another shift. They are also working on purchases for the radio system. Chief Thorington stated we currently have firefighters on shift for 4 days, 6 hours a day. He stated he and the Fire Marshall are also working those days. He then stated we have a solid group of firefighters. He then stated it can take up to 3 years to get a new fire engine. He then stated they are looking at purchasing a new vehicle that can be used for victim transport. He stated they will be replacing a 2016 Ford Explorer; it is very beat up and it needs to be replaced. He further stated we will be assigning radios to firefighters on the scene. He stated they will be purchasing more radios in the future. Chief Thorington then stated we will be replacing the garage door openers for $8,000. He then stated they already replaced 3 of them. He further stated the HVAC units need to be replaced because of the age of the units. He further stated they are looking at hiring a company for the hose testing and they will be certified and it will be a better system for testing. He further stated we have some outdated computers that need to be replaced. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if they will have to hire an EMT when they purchase the vehicle for transport to a hospital. Chief Thorington stated everyone at the Fire Hall are certified EMTs. Further discussion was held regarding the Fire Departments budget.

## AMBULANCE

City Manager Zelenak stated we haven't received funds for utilities but this line item covers the insurance for that portion of our Fire Department where the ambulance stays.

##  $ \underline{\text{DPW}} $

Superintendent Archey discussed the budget for DPW. He stated they are going to be purchasing a new leaf truck. He stated they are looking at two different vendors right now. He further stated if they purchase it July 1, they won't have it until next year. They will also be purchasing a new pick-up truck.

##  $ \underline{\text{CEMETERY}} $

City Manager Zelenak stated we are looking at installing signs, foundation for the columbarium and we will be doing some road improvements. He further stated we will also be adding trees to Section 11.

## TRANSPORTATION

City Manager Zelenak stated there are no expenditures in this line item.

## PARKS AND RECREATION

City Manager Zelenak stated we will be doing some improvements at Volunteer Park. We will be adding some playground equipment near the volleyball courts and baseball fields. They are also requesting another investment in McHattie Park to add some additional playground equipment. He then stated they are doing some improvements on the trails, such as picnic benches, recycling, and dog waste stations.

## CABLE COMMISSION

City Manager Zelenak stated we budgeted for the replacement of some equipment and upgrades and salaries for people that record the events.

## CULTURAL ARTS COMMISSION

City Manager Zelenak stated we have expenditures for grants for murals and sculptures and photography and other projects they do within the city. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated they are trying to find someone that would donate their time to replace the frames they put items in. They are in rough shape.

## HISTORICAL

City Manager Zelenak stated we have funds available to add lighting in the village to attract more people to the village during the holidays. He further stated we have some additional ideas to discuss with the historical village. He further stated we have allocated some funds for the barn project for the foundation. He stated we need to add plumbing and electrical for future uses of the barn. He further stated this could be a money maker for the city in the future. We could use it for weddings, receptions, group conferences. He stated for a small dollar amount and fee, he thinks people will use it. He further stated the Historical Commission doesn't like the idea. Further discussion was held on different events that could be held in the barn.

## VETERANS MEMORIAL AND FIRST RESPONDERS' MONUMENT PROJECT

City Manager Zelenak stated we have money allocated for the improvements at the memorial such as a sidewalk. He further stated we also have cost for the material and installation of the first responder's monument. He further stated we have been trying to get this project completed, but we are waiting on the vendor that will be doing the work. Further discussion was held regarding the Veteran's Memorial.

## MAJOR STREETS & LOCAL STREETS

City Manager Zelenak stated we have budgeted money for Eagle Heights subdivision. He stated we will also be updating the faded signs throughout the city. Superintendent Archey stated we will be having a company come in and do some spray patching for us. He stated it is better than cold patching. He further stated we will be doing the parking lots where the Farmers Market is held. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked about the transfer between funds. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated we have always transferred funds from Major Streets to Local Street because we have more local streets than we do Major Streets, and Major Streets is well funded.

MUNICIPAL STREETS

City Manager Zelenak stated we have funds that will be used for our road improvement programs.

PERPETUAL CARE FUND

City Manager Zelenak stated we transfer a percentage for wages of our cemetery workers.

##  $ \underline{\text{DDA}} $

City Manager Zelenak stated they have several projects in the upcoming year such as a parking study, increase social media presence and a TIF development plan as well as working on the new branding for the downtown and replacing signs. DDA Director Mack stated they will continue the façade program and sign program. He then stated we have had 3 or 4 people that are interested in the façade program. He stated they allocated $10,000 per year. He then stated there will be a parking study done to see where we are with parking and looking at the ordinance to see if there is room for improvement. He further stated the Farmers Market is the real strength of the DDA. We will also continue to have all the events we have had in downtown such as Ladies Night and Carnivale.

## DRUG FORFEITURE

City Manager Zelenak stated we don't have any funds set aside for this budget year and we won't have any expenditures.

## CDBG

City Manager Zelenak stated this could be up in the air for the upcoming year, but we do have money allocated for Haven and the Senior Center

## 2003 WW BOND

City Manager Zelenak stated this is the last year for this bond.

## CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS

City Manager Zelenak stated we will be spending funds for the Pontiac Trail Road projects and the Farmers Market parking lot. We will be doing some other parking lots as well.

## WATER AND SEWER

City Manager Zelenak stated we will be replacing the generator at the water treatment plant, as well as a well near the volleyball courts in McHattie Park. We will also be refurbishing an aeration basin and clarifier and UV equipment. They will also be getting a new roof installed. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if the sale of water and wastewater is the same as last year. City Manager Zelenak stated it is not because it will go up based upon the water and sewer rate study that Council approved. She then asked if we are looking at an increase in water rates this year. City Manager Zelenak stated we are increasing the ready to serve charge, as well as the cost of the sale of water. Councilmember Hansen asked about the public utility line item and about grant payback. City Manager Zelenak stated in all of the budgets this year we have funds for updated lighting. He further stated we will go out for bid for the organizations that DTE supplied us. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked about the updates to SCADA. Superintendent Beason stated we do update every year. He stated it pays for updating and if there are any issues at any of our lift stations it pays for that. We see a lot of upgrades going on right now and a lot of our lift stations are outdated so we have 9 lift stations. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked about the line item for security and if it is for updating. Superintendent Beason stated it is for adding new security and we will be adding a lot of cameras. We might get an electrical gate that you will have to use a card to enter. He further stated we have some security but cameras will be beneficial. Further discussion was held regarding security. Superintendent Beason explained some of the updates that are needed in the next budget year. We will have some sandblasting all the piping in the water plant itself, they are deteriorating. He further stated there is a pump can that is leaking and its kind of an emergency situation. He then stated on the wastewater side, we need a lot of things done. It is time to look at upgrading again. Some of the pumps run 24/7. He stated we need a new roof, and we'll be updating some of our clarifiers and some need to be replaced. We are looking at replacement or rebuilding it. He further stated the newer ones are simpler to use. Additional discussion was held regarding the Water and Waste Water budget.
