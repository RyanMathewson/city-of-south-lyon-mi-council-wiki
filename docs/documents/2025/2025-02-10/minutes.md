1. Second reading of Ordinance to amend chapter 90, article III, Section 9-72, fines and costs 02/10/2024

City Manager Zelenak stated at the January 27th council meeting, council approved a proposal to increase the fines for handicapped parking violations from $100 to $125 and all other parking violations from $10 to $20 if paid within seven days. If paid after seven days, fines would increase to $150 for handicapped parking violations and $30 for all other parking violations. These fines were a result in comparing other communities' fines which range from $0 to $75 for other parking violations/snow emergencies and $50 to $180 for handicapped parking violations. The communities included in the research are Wixom, Novi, Milford, and Brighton. He stated we are now asking council to approve the second reading.

## NEW BUSINESS

1. Drilling and installation of new well #8

Water/Sewer Department Superintendent Beason stated the Water Department is seeking approval to hire Peerless Midwest Inc. to provide professional services for the drilling and installation of a new well (#8) to be located approximately 250 ft east of well #7 in McHattie Park. This well will replace the failed #2 well located at the water plant on Dorothy Street. Peerless Midwest currently services and maintains the city wells, low service pumps, high service pumps and drilled the two offset wells #6 and #7. Peerless Midwest has been instrumental in obtaining the test site approval from EGLE and are highly qualified to complete the scope of work. This is a scheduled expenditure for the 2024-2025 fiscal year from line item 592-556-972 with the total not to exceed $320,500. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked why is this a no bid request, it's not unusual but she would like to get facts on why we aren't bidding, is it because there are no other contractors in our area. Superintendent Beason stated there are other contractors, and he has worked with them. Peerless has been our go to for 15 years now. They have a hydrogeologist on site and very familiar with EGLE and our other wells. They are very instrumental and the work they have already done working on the permits from EGLE. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked how may contractors are there in this area. Superintendent Beason stated there are a few. Councilmember Hansen asked about the failing of well #2 and about the work that was put into the location decision. Superintendent Beason stated well #2 was originally drilled in 1954 and it was designed to produce 980 gallons per minute. He further stated they have been watching the well for several years now and we've seen a decline in production. He further stated if there is a small decline, you don't worry about it too much, but over the years it has continued to decline and the run times have increased and with that you see more power being used. He stated that is the indication that the well needs cleaning or an overhaul and we do this with all of the wells. This particular one has been overhauled 3 or 4 times. They did a very aggressive cleaning on this one with mechanical wire brushes, chemicals, and after that is done, the pump and motor are overhauled so the bowls, bearings, and pump head are all overhauled. After the cleaning is done, they take a camera and videotape it looking for any abnormalities in the casing. He further stated they go all
