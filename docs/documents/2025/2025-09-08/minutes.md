##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION-}} $ Downtown

City Manager Zelenak stated they are continuing to work on Lafayette Park, at the corner of McHattie St and Pontiac Trail and the first phase is almost complete. Last week the contractor installed the sod for erosion control and future use of the site until the next phase of the project is completed. We are currently waiting for the drinking fountains and the light poles to be delivered. He suggests people keep an eye on the progress that is being made on the site. It will almost be ready for immediate use. He then stated we are completing the background checks for the DDA Director and we hope to make an announcement as early as the next Council Meeting. He then reminded everyone that the amended DDA TIF plan public hearing will be held at the next City Council Meeting. He then stated he reached out to DTE regarding the electronic chargers, but has yet to receive a response. He then stated the DDA Board will be discussing applications they have received for the board at the next DDA meeting. Then the appointment will be done at the next Council Meeting. Councilmember Bogert asked if there are sprinklers at the park. City Manager Zelenak stated there is water there, but most of the grass that is there now will be removed because it is there now for soil erosion control. There will be turf grass, bushes, trees, shrubs and a lot of brick work will be done on the site. Councilmember Dilg stated the bike racks look great in town and it is a great addition to the downtown. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked for an explanation on the TIF plan. City Manager Zelenak stated every so many years the TIF plan must be updated by law. The first plan was for 25 years, and now developing the new plan it allows the DDA to set forth ideas and plans to look at what the tax incremental financing dollars will actually be collected within the district so they can start to project into the future what improvements they will be making within the district. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if it raises taxes. City Manager Zelenak stated it doesn't raise taxes; it's just a plan to show the improvements they plan on making in the future. Councilmember Kivell stated he was talking with a crossing guard and he was told there was no increase in their pay. City Manager Zelenak stated every year we look at all the rates of pay at the beginning of the year, and he will have to look into this.

### 2. Chevy Tahoe purchase- Police Dept

Chief Unruh stated they are requesting approval to replace the 2019 Ford Explorer which has 106,455 miles on it and has experienced numerous maintenance issues and is showing signs of significant wear. Ongoing repairs have become costly and disruptive to operations, making it no longer reliable for frontline or support use. This replacement will allow for reassignment of existing vehicles within the department to better align with operational needs. Specifically, the 2022 Ford F-150 and is currently serving as the command vehicle, will be reassigned as the utility vehicle, its original intended use. This truck is more practical for utility functions, particularly for the truck bed can be used for hauling equipment to the range and property to the storage unit at the water treatment plant. The newly purchased vehicle, a 2025 Chevy Tahoe will serve as a patrol vehicle. Current patrol vehicle 553 will now be utilized as a command vehicle. She further stated she received 3 bids and Berger Chevrolet was the lowest. Discussion was held regarding the three quotes.

## BUDGET

Councilmember Kurtzweil stated there has been a lot of questions since the house side of the budget has been announced. Her concern is the state revenue sharing that comes into the city. She spoke with Chairwomen of the appropriations committee and she explained what it means. What it means is the House has made a proposal where there is about a 12% cut in state revenue. It isn't in stone; it is only the House's proposal. She then explained that is a claw back from the increase from the previous year, it was a one-time grant. She further stated other than that, we will be status quo, but what the house is also proposing that additional funding for roads which will be substantial. She further stated there will be a public safety trust fund and it will be targeted to Police Departments. She then stated there may be other opportunities to get other funds beyond the state funding. She then stated Senator Runestads office sent her the Senate Fiscal Agency out of his side what the state funding would be, and we are showing a decrease in the House side by 3% but the Senate wants to restore it. We will see what happens on September 30th, nothing is in stone. She stated she expressed her concern in any decrease in funding.
