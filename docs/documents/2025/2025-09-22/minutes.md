## Denise Semion

25142 Jefferson Court, South Lyon

I've been member of the South Lyon Parks and Recreation Commission since about 2014.

I'm speaking tonight as a resident and not representing the Commission.

My remarks are limited to the use of the Huron Valley Trail.

I frequently walk or bicycle the trail. Several years ago, I mentioned to Paul that the trail users have changed. It used to be that cyclists practiced safety rules and trail etiquette, including riding at a safe speed, keeping your cycle under control, and giving an audible warning when passing others.

That has changed over the years with the popularity of e-bikes and I'll share a story as an example of the unsafe riders on the trail.

I was biking at about eight to 10 miles an hour. An older man, riding an e-bike zipped by me at a fast pace without any warning. He was pulling a trailer with a large dog in it. I estimate that the man, the e-bike, trailer and dog combined must have weighed at least 300 pounds.

His wife passed me a while later and I mentioned to her that a friendly audible warning is good way to practice trail etiquette and safety for others and that he was traveling very fast. She said he likes to go fast and doesn't believe in warning anyone.

If I had been preparing to go around a walker he could have easily run into me. I hate to think of getting hit by a fast moving 300-pound vehicle at 15 to 20 miles an hour.

This is just one example of the new riders on the trail.

I support your efforts and encourage you to adopt a speed limit. The trails at Kensington, Hudson Mills and Stony Creek Metroparks all have a 10 mile an hour speed limit.

As a Commissioner, I've suggested for the past several years getting signage installed along the trail posting a speed limit and to give an audible warning when passing.

I give my strong support for a speed limit on the trail and throughout town.
