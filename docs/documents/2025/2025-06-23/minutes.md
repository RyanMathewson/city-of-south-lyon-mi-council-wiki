## PROCLAMATION- Police Chief Baaki

Mayor Kennedy read the attached proclamation for Police Chief Baaki's service to the City of South Lyon for over 30 years. Chief Baaki thanked everyone for the proclamation. He stated it has been a long journey. He then thanked his family, his wife Stacy who supported him all these years. He then thanked Chris Sovik for being a great mentor to him over the years. He said he was able to give him a great base to begin as Chief. He then stated everyone at the department is dedicated that are here to do the job and they want to do it right, it is not about the money, it is about putting on the uniform and serving our citizens. They have a great relationship with the community and all the festivals and things everyone is there to help out. ## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. McHattie Park electronic sign

City Manager Zelenak stated the sign in McHattie Park is outdated and needs to be replaced. The current sign is over 15 years old. When changing the message on the sign, you must be within so many feet of it, using a laptop and software that is outdated and no longer supported. The new sign will have a new double-sided illuminated light box as well as a new double-sided LED messaging portion. The new LED portion will feature the capability of changing the message from our office and will have updated fonts, options and LED messaging capabilities. It will be the same size as the current sign and will sit within the pillars that are currently there as described by our zoning ordinance. The original sign was purchased from Image 360 which is the middle bid and offers everything we are requesting and is a local company located in Southfield, Michigan. We have received three bids ranging from $18,994 to $28,693. The lowest bidder, Signs Plus is located in Sarasota, Florida. They are unfortunately, out of state and would need to hire contractors that would install the sign on their behalf. This would make it very difficult if there were problems with the sign regarding installation and maintenance. He further stated he is recommending Image 360 for this project. Councilmember Hansen asked if this is a similar sign as what we are considering for an upgrade in Paul Baker Park. City Manager Zelenak stated that would be a totally different size and a different bid. Councilmember Bogert stated one nice aspect of this vendor is the sign resolution is very high and we should have some good graphics. Councilmember Kivell asked if we will be able to change the sign from the office. City Manager Zelenak stated yes, this is one of the advantages of having a new sign.

### 2. Extension of Assessing Services

City Manager Zelenak stated Oakland County Commissioners recently voted in an approximate 80% increase to our assessing contract. This increase was set to begin July 1st. ## PROCLAMATION

WHERAS, Douglas Baaki, a veteran Police Officer with over 30 years of service to the City of South Lyon; and

WHERAS, the South Lyon City Council along with the entire community, would like to honor the contributions that Chief Baaki has made during his law enforcement career; and

WHERAS, Chief Baaki earned a Bachelor of Arts Degree in Criminal Justice Administration from Columbia Southern University in 2016; and

WHERAS, Chief Baaki started his law enforcement career when he joined the South Lyon Police Department as a part-time officer in 1990, becoming a full-time Officer in 1993. During his first six years of service, Chief Baaki took on a variety of roles including road patrol, field training officer, detective, and data master administrator. Chief Baaki was promoted to Sergeant in September 1999, to Lieutenant in February, 2019, and became our Chief of Police in 2022; and

WHERAS, Chief Baaki has attended dozens of advance training classes including New Chief's and Executive School, Supervisor, Leadership, and Liability, Performance and Accountability, and other various instructor schools. Chief Baaki has been involved with the Lakes Area Citizen's Police Academy since 1999 where he instructed classes regarding police response to resistance and patrol operations for citizens attending the academy. Chief Baaki is a member of the Michigan Association of Chiefs of Police, Oakland County Association of Chiefs of Police, and International Association of Chiefs of Police.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Steve Kennedy, Mayor of the City of South Lyon, and on behalf of the South Lyon City Council, hereby commend the outstanding community service performed by Douglas Baaki during his law enforcement career of over 30 years and wish him all the best in retirement.
