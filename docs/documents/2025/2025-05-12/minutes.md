So escheating the state is why we would escheat to the state because it is unclaimed property that we are having a problem identifying who is the owner of this property. She goes on to say that if someone were to escheat to the state and someone does come to you and say that they are owed $10,000. She believes there is a clawback provision where you can claw back that money from the state and pay it or they can claim it within the statute of limitations period of time, but they will have to come forward with documentation. Kurtzweil states that she feels the achievement is probably the way to go. She has been on this for a couple of years now, trying to get this resolved, because she really hates reading that summary and seeing that. It's a public document and everyone reads it.

2. Authorization for the Fire Department to Enter into a Contract for the Purchase of a new Fire Engine.

City Manager Zelenak states the South Lyon Fire Department committee reviewed three major fire truck manufacturers: Sutphen, Spartan, and Pierce. While all offered comparable features, Sutphen stood out as the clear leader in customer service – not only during the purchasing process, but also post-delivery, providing the most responsive support for the maintenance, repairs, and service. Additionally, while Spartan and Pierce have estimated delivery timeframes of approximately five years, Sutphen projects a significantly shorter timeline of two to three years. Zelenak goes on to explain that we are requesting authorization to enter into a contract with Sutphen in order to lock in pricing and initiate the build process. As the build may take up to three years, funding will be allocated from a future fiscal year budget. We are still hoping to receive Federal Grant Funds to pay for at least a portion of the purchase price. Apollo Fire Apparatus Sales and Services is the authorized Sutphen dealer for our region. The cost of a new fire engine and equipment to stock the engine is approximately $1,100,000. Zelenak references materials that have been put in the Councilmembers' packet and states that the Fire Department is requesting authorization to purchase a contract with Apollo Fire Apparatus Sales and Services for an amount not to exceed $1,100,000 for the purchase of a new fire engine and equipment, subject to final approval through the city attorney, from Acct #101-336-978.

City Attorney Hamameh states that City Manager Zelenak revised the suggested motion to approve Apollo and Sutphen as we are not sure the contract indicates Sutphen, so we want some clarity on that, as well as subject to the review of the City Attorney.

## BUDGET

City Manager Zelenak advises Council that in their packet for review is the new Property Assessment Administration Services Contract.
