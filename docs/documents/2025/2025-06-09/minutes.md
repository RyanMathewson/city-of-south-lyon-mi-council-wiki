Female turtles are looking for a place to lay their eggs. If you see a turtle crossing, it's best to let them cross the road on their own. However, if they're in danger and it is safe for you to assist them, you can help them cross the road with these tips in mind, always move them in the direction they were heading, smaller species like painted turtles can simply be picked up and moved, but if larger species like snapping turtles, you can use a snow shovel to move them but be careful because they bite. And remember to only intervene if it's safe to do so. You shouldn't attempt if the roads are busy, if you have to pull over, watch for traffic in both directions.
