# Rules of Conduct for Public Comment at Council Meetings $ ^{*} $

Members of the public may speak at a Council meeting upon recognition by the Mayor. Public comment may only occur during periods designated on the agenda for public comment or a public hearing. A person may speak for up to two (2) minutes during each of the two public comment periods on agenda items or non-agenda items. Waivers of the time requirement may only be granted in the discretion of the Mayor, and waivers to speak at a time other than a designated comment period may only be granted by the Council. Any person wishing to make a presentation longer than two minutes or requiring audio-visual equipment is asked to contact the City Clerk requesting to appear on a future agenda.

A person may only address Council from the podium. Only one person may occupy the podium at a time. All remarks are to be directed to the Mayor and Council. Speakers are not to engage in direct dialog with other meeting attendees.

Any person who violates the Rules of Conduct, disturbs the peace at the meeting, and/or interferes with the meeting may be warned, ordered to be seated, removed, and/or ticketed.

 $ ^{*} $This summarizes Council Resolution 04-18. Complete Rules, including guidelines for considering waivers, are available in the Council Chambers and from the City Clerk.

### 1. 9 Line Project

City Manager Zelenak stated the 9 Line is a community corridor collaboration. The ultimate goal of the ten communities in Oakland County is to create a 30-mile pedestrian and cycling corridor spanning the whole county. He further explained the purpose of the 9 Line Memo of Understanding is to agree to work together with the other communities on creating a uniform look of the trail and promote activities along the 9 Line. The memo of understanding is non-binding between the 5 communities and is for the purpose of planning and development on the Nine Mile Pathway. Simon Rivers from Oakland County Parks introduced himself and stated he is excited to discuss the project and the memo of understanding. He stated this is the second phase of the project and includes, Novi, Northville, Lyon Township and the City of South Lyon, as well as Oakland County. He further stated this is a voluntary agreement to develop and plan a pathway from the west side to the east side of Oakland County. He stated it involves 10 communities and Oakland County. He then stated OHM are providing plan assistance, grant assistance,

She then stated as you move from east to west there are very unique opportunities in each community, and the purpose of the study is to connect the downtown districts, such as Farmington, South Lyon, Northville, Ferndale, Oak Park, Hazel Park and their recreation amenities, and for creating the options for people that live near 9 Mile to bike or walk instead of having to get into a car and drive.
