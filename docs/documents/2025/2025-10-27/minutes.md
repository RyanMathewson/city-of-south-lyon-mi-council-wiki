### 1. Call for CDBG public hearing

City Manager Zelenak explained each year the US Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provides funds to jurisdictions throughout the country for projects that meet their national objectives. We are now requesting to schedule our CDBG public hearing on November 10th at 6:30 p.m. It will be published in the local newspaper which gives the opportunity for hearing public comments relating to allocating those funds in the City of South Lyon.

### 2. 2026 Road improvement project

City Manager Zelenak stated the planned road improvements for 2026 is Oak Creek. That is our next project in our multi-year road improvement program. This project will entail replacement of the entire asphalt roadway. Preliminary cost estimate for the project will be approximately $2.1 million. We will be sending out letters to the residents in the subdivision informing them of the meeting date to discuss the planned improvements in January. At that meeting we will discuss bid dates, and construction dates as well, with a follow-up meeting in April/May with constructions plans and more detailed information. We are currently finishing up the design work in the subdivision. The contractor that bid and did the construction project in Eagle Heights in 2025 will hold their prices for the project next year.

Councilmember Hansen asked about the messaging and the timetable. Mr. Alix of HRC congratulated City Manager Zelenak on his long career as a public servant. He further stated they have been lucky to work with him for over 10 years. He wished him the best in his retirement and thanked him for being a steward for the city and the city has been lucky to have him for the last 7 years and he wished the city luck on finding a new City Manager, but he will be hard to replace. He then stated this depends on how quickly we get the project out for bid. He stated the contractor that was used during the last project has agreed to hold the same bid for this project. We typically see an increase in prices between 3% to 8% per year. If Council decides to make the selection and the contractor holds his price, the city will see between $60,00 and $170,000 in savings instead of opening it up on the streets. He further stated we were very happy with the contractor's performance and the expediency they completed the project. They actually completed it ahead of schedule. He then stated as far as public outreach, we'll have to get the new City Manager in place and run it by him, but we are thinking January or February and the bidding would be at the City Manager's discretion. Construction could begin after frost breaks, and it could be completed by September.

He then stated the waste hauling contracts are due November 3rd. He then stated they are continuing to work on the First Responders Monument and the supports for the roof have been installed. He then stated we are working on the schedule for the road bond and the approval of the bond authorization resolution that will occur sometime in September and determine if there are any changes in our double A+ rating. He further stated the last 7 and a half years have flown by. He then thanked everyone for allowing him to serve the residents of South Lyon. He has worked in municipal government for 40 years. # National Society Daughters of the American Revolution

# PROCLAMATION

WHEREAS, National Family Literary Day, established by the 103 $ ^{rd} $ Congress in 1994, and now marking the 31 $ ^{st} $ anniversary on November 1, 2025, highlights the importance of reading and learning for the entire family and emphasizes the impact that parents have on their child's learning; and

WHEREAS, This day is celebrated across America each year, and focuses on special activities and events that showcase the importance of family literacy programs that empower families and build a nation of readers; and

WHEREAS, literacy programs across the United States will observe National Family Literacy Day by holding read-a-thons, book drives, workshops, and family activities at schools, libraries, and community centers to encourage literacy; and

WHEREAS, as many as one in six adults struggle with reading and writing, and by learning to read, individuals can gain self-respect and confidence and strive toward goals that otherwise would not be achievable; and

WHEREAS, The National Society of the Daughters of the American Revolution is a nonprofit, nonpolitical volunteer women's service organization dedicated to promoting patriotism, preserving American history, and securing America's future through better education for children and adults; and

WHEREAS, education being one of the cornerstones of the National Society of the Daughters of the American Revolution, Grand River Trail Chapter is committed to increasing literacy by promoting and supporting literacy programs.

NOW THEREFORE, I,  $ \underline{\text{Tether in Awaay}} $ by virtue of the authority vested in me by The City of South Lyon, do hereby proclaim November 1 $ ^{st} $ as National Family Literacy Day in the City of South Lyon.

## NATIONAL FAMILY LITERACY DAY

To underscore the importance of literacy, celebrate the joy of reading, encourage residents to promote literacy by reading together as a family, and to extend deep appreciation to our local libraries, educators, and literacy service providers for their tireless efforts to strengthen the literacy of our children and our community.

DONE ON THIS DAY: November 1, 2025 in The City of South Lyon
