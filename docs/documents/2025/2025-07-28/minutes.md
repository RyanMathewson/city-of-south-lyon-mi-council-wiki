## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Fire Department radios

Fire Chief Conrad stated the Fire Department currently has 10 operational radios available for 20 non-officer staff members. This creates a significant communication and safety gap during emergency responses, as not all personnel are equipped with a radio. The lack of adequate communication tools can hinder coordination, delay response efforts, and increase risk on scene. Acquiring 5 additional Motorola Radios will bring us closer to a 1:1 radio-to-person ratio, enhancing operational effectiveness, accountability and safety for all members during incidents. Attorney Hamameh stated it is becoming common, at the bottom of quotes there is a very small line that states "if you accept this quote, you are accepting all terms and conditions on our website". It is the same thing we have seen in the past, and there are some concerning items on their website, and she is asking to review and negotiate the terms.
