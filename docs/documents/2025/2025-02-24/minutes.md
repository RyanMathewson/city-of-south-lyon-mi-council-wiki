##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $- Downtown

DDA Director Mack stated the Farmers Market last week was a little lighter than normal, but that happens this time of year, there were a few last-minute cancellations. He further stated the vendors that were there made a lot of sales. He then stated the outdoor market will have 65-70 vendors and he then reminded everyone that the second Saturday of each month, there will be a food truck rally during the Farmers Market. He then updated everyone on the Burger Battle and said there have been a few responses but our restaurants aren't in the top 3 as of now. He stated they're have been about 250 votes so far. He then stated Main Street America using GIS software, provided us with the number of visitors in 2024, which was 188,807 visitors. He stated that is down 3% from 2023. It is a fun bit of information. He further stated they met with Wade Trim on the DDA's development plan and we're expecting to finish that before the end of the year. Councilmember Bogart asked how do they get the counts. Mr. Mack stated they use the GIS software and they put the districts boundaries in the map and that is how they get the counts.

## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Thorington stated they have had 205 incidences this year to date. He then stated Engine 71 is back in service from having some bodywork done. He then stated we submitted for the second reimbursement for the smoke alarms and there have been changes made, so it may take some time. He further stated he is now part of the Oakland County Management Team and we had a meeting last week that's part of a mutual aide agreement. We had a table top meeting last week and it was based on a flood, but you can apply it to straight line winds or tornado as well. Councilmember Dilg asked about the fire that occurred a couple weeks ago and about the hydrant that wasn't working. Chief Thorington stated it is still part of the investigation. He further stated Oakland County does an investigation on every house fire and it is still ongoing. Councilmember Bogart asked how often are the hydrants flushed and tested. Chief stated they are flushed quarterly, and we go through every year and make sure they are dry and not frozen. Councilmember Kivell stated he was surprised when he went to see what had taken place, and the next hydrant looked like it was a couple football fields away. Chief Thorington stated the distance between hydrants is pretty standard and the fire marshal will get involved during the planning to make sure they are to the right standards. He further stated at every time they go to a fire, they have a tanker with 750 gallons of water that they hit the fire with while they are connecting to the hydrants.

## NEW BUSINESS

1. Purchase of Carmanah Solar LED flashing crosswalk beacon system

City Manager Zelenak stated the DPW is requesting the purchase of the Carmanah Solar LED flashing crosswalk beacon system. The flashing crosswalk will be replacing the non-flashing crosswalk signs at 11 Mile and the Huron Valley Trail. The City of South Lyon is collaborating with Lyon Township on this project. He further stated this is a budgeted item for the current fiscal year and is available in 4-6 weeks from Carrier and Gable, the sole source provider for the Carmanah brand beacon system. This equipment is the exact same equipment that was installed at the other crosswalk locations throughout the city and is interchangeable for parts and materials. Councilmember Bogert stated he is happy to see this crosswalk being installed because he lives in that area and sees people going through the one that's currently there. He then asked how long until it will be installed. City Manager Zelenak stated it will be installed in April.

City Manager Zelenak reminded everyone that the 2025 road paving project is for Eagle Heights Subdivision and we are getting ready to go out for bid. The estimated cost is around $3 million dollars. He then stated over the last few weeks he has been assembling information as it relates to PA 233 renewable energy sources. He then stated we will be having the annual Police and Fire Reports also on an upcoming agenda.
