## Rules of Conduct for Public Comment at Council Meetings*

Members of the public may speak at a Council meeting upon recognition by the Mayor. Public comment may only occur during periods designated on the agenda for public comment or a public hearing. A person may speak for up to two (2) minutes during each of the two public comment periods on agenda items or non-agenda items. Waivers of the time requirement may only be granted in the discretion of the Mayor, and waivers to speak at a time other than a designated comment period may only be granted by the Council. Any person wishing to make a presentation longer than two minutes or requiring audio-visual equipment is asked to contact the City Clerk requesting to appear on a future agenda.

A person may only address Council from the podium. Only one person may occupy the podium at a time. All remarks are to be directed to the Mayor and Council. Speakers are not to engage in direct dialog with other meeting attendees.

Any person who violates the Rules of Conduct, disturbs the peace at the meeting, and/or interferes with the meeting may be warned, ordered to be seated, removed, and/or ticketed.

Water/Sewer Department Superintendent Beason stated the Water Department is seeking approval to hire Peerless Midwest Inc. to provide professional services for the drilling and installation of a new well (#8) to be located approximately 250 ft east of well #7 in McHattie Park. This well will replace the failed #2 well located at the water plant on Dorothy Street. Peerless Midwest currently services and maintains the city wells, low service pumps, high service pumps and drilled the two offset wells #6 and #7. Peerless Midwest has been instrumental in obtaining the test site approval from EGLE and are highly qualified to complete the scope of work. This is a scheduled expenditure for the 2024-2025 fiscal year from line item 592-556-972 with the total not to exceed $320,500. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked why is this a no bid request, it's not unusual but she would like to get facts on why we aren't bidding, is it because there are no other contractors in our area. Superintendent Beason stated there are other contractors, and he has worked with them. Peerless has been our go to for 15 years now. They have a hydrogeologist on site and very familiar with EGLE and our other wells. They are very instrumental and the work they have already done working on the permits from EGLE. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked how may contractors are there in this area. Superintendent Beason stated there are a few. Councilmember Hansen asked about the failing of well #2 and about the work that was put into the location decision. Superintendent Beason stated well #2 was originally drilled in 1954 and it was designed to produce 980 gallons per minute. ## How to Use Demand Response

## As Soon As Possible:

1. Call your transit provider (see map).

2. Provide information to get registered (name, address, wheelchair/walker needs, doctor verification, etc.). Transit provider will need time to process your paperwork.

3. Once registered, you can book a trip!

## At Least 3 Days $ ^{*} $ Before Your Trip:

1. Call your transit provider (see map).

2. Provide the date, time, and location for your trip (and return trip if needed).

3. Receive information on when the transit provider can take you and, if it works for your schedule, book the trip.

4. Prepare your fare (see next slide).

## The Day of Your Trip:

1. Board the bus and give your fare to the driver.

3. Arrive at your destination.

2. Sit back and relax!

4. If you need to make any changes to your trip, call the transit provider.
