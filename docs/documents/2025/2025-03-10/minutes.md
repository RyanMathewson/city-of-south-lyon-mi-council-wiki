## FIRE  $ \underline{\text{CHIEF}} $- 2024 Annual Report

Fire Chief Thorington stated they are at 261 instances for this year so far. He stated Carnivale was a good event and the Fire Department gave out coloring books and we had the trucks there. He stated we are waiting for the reimbursement for the smoke alarms. He further stated we submitted for a 357,000 grant for an ambulance through Senator Peters office. He then thanked Phil Weipert, Mayor Kennedy, Sharon Sour, and Amil Larry from SEMCOG for their letters of support as well as Dave Coulter the Executive for Oakland County. Sergeants Simmons and DeMeyer and aerial pump operator McGillan all went through Fire Infrastructure One Course and completed it last Sunday. Domonic who is known as "Lou" Soave was just promoted to Fire Engineer. Councilmember Bogert asked if there was an update on the recent fire. Chief Thorington stated the last he heard at the time, it was undetermined. Chief Thorington then reviewed the 2024 Annual Report which can be found on their website at  $ \underline{\text{www.southlyonfire.com}} $ Councilmember Kivell stated he likes that there are pictures of all the firefighters in the report. He thinks that all departments should have picture of the employees so people can recognize who may be working in their backyard. Councilmember Dilg asked him to verify the mutual aid numbers. Chief Thorington stated we gave 48 mutual aid and received 8 mutual aids. Councilmember Dilg asked if there is a particular type of incidences that have gone up. Chief Thorington stated across the state medical seems to be the more predominate increase, but fire runs have increased as well. Mayor Kennedy thanked Chief Thorington and his staff for their efforts in getting grants.

##  $ \underline{\text{POLICE CHIEF}} $- 2024 Annual Report

Chief Baaki stated we conducted a couple of interviews for a Police Officer and we are in the process of speaking with one about getting him hired. He then stated we had 24 participants in the Citizen Police Academy. He then reviewed the 2024 Annual Report, which can be found at  $ \underline{\text{www.southlyonmi.org}} $ Councilmember Bogert stated there was a peak in September for arrests, and wondered if it was because of Pumpkinfest. Chief Baaki stated he would have to look into that, but larcenies always go up because of the warmer weather and he then reminded everyone to lock their car doors and keep belongings out of site. Mayor Kennedy stated Chief did a nice job on the report and thanked him for always looking for additional funding.

## MANAGER'S REPORT

City Manager Zelenak stated the 2025 road paving project for Eagle Heights is out for bid. He stated the estimated cost is $3 million dollars. He then stated there will be another meeting with the residents of the subdivision sometime in May after we have the pre-construction meeting with the contractor. He further stated this will be brought before Council on April 14th and the construction is tentatively scheduled to begin after June 1st. City Manager Zelenak stated the 2025-2026 budget meeting is Wednesday April 16th at 5:30 p.m. He then stated we hired a new coordinator for the Senior Center and her name is Susan McCoy, she is replacing Carnie Cavanaugh who left the position in late 2024.

## COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilmember Dilg stated she was very happy to hear the South Lyon High School Girls Basketball team won their first district championship since 2008. She stated her daughter played for several years and they struggled a bit and it was really nice to see that after 17 years they pulled it out. She further stated it is great South Lyon Women's Basketball stuck with it and are making those kinds of improvements. She then stated she sat through part of the school board meeting last week, and if anyone gets a chance and listens to their comments, Dr. Theresa Laszlow did an amazing talk on human rights.

Councilmember Kurtzweil thanked the Fire Department and the employees and volunteers, and the Police Department employees and volunteers. She stated they work extremely hard to make this one of the safest cities in the state. She then stated it is good to see the Police Academy is getting full again, and there are people looking to join law enforcement. She further stated she is happy we are past the horrific point in our society where people didn't want to fund Police and people made their lives difficult. She further stated it was a rumor, but its true. St. Joes ran out of fish during their Fish Fry last week, and Lucas ran out as well. She further stated she ended up at Riverbank and she ran into a few employees as well. She then stated she has been assured that St. Joes will be well stocked and ready for Friday, and everyone should get out in the community, support our local businesses, and enjoy a great dinner and a Fish Fry on Friday night.

Councilmember Bogert stated he wanted to wish the best to the South Lyon High Dive Team who are headed to Holland for their competition.

Mayor Kennedy reminded residents about a couple of events that will be taking place in the city. The first is the Kiwanis Annual Easter Egg Scramble that will be held on Saturday, April 19th at 11am at Volunteer Park. There will be four (4) fields set up for the various age groups and the scramble will start at noon. There will also be fire trucks, police cars and EMT vehicles for the kids to explore and of course the Easter Bunny will be there for pictures. The second event he mentioned is the Mostiacolli Dinner that will be held by the South Lyon Historical Society on Wednesday, April 16th. The dinner will take place at the South Lyon VFW Hall from 4pm until 7pm. The cost is $15 for adults $5 for children 10 years of age and under. The funds will be used to help add the Roper Barn to the Historic Village.
