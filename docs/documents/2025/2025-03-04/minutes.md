## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Parks

City Manager Zelenak stated we are always updating and maintaining our parks. We leverage tax dollars when we can get grant funds when available. He further discussed S. Lafayette Park and explained the next investment will be the foundation and public utility work and that phase will be between $125,000 and $150,000. He further explained the first phase will be mainly done before July first and later the other amenities will be added, such as trees and bushes, gazebos, signage, lighting, tables, chairs and the EV charging station. He further stated we received $300,000 from a grant from Oakland County for that park. The parks and recreation committee have been working hard on looking at benches, colors and this park requires the most investment because it is going from the ground up. Discussion was held regarding when the park will be ready to be used, which is later this year. Further discussion was held in regards to putting up signage showing what the park will look like when completed.

He then discussed McHattie Park. He stated the Parks and Recreation Commission would like to replace some of the playground equipment that doesn't get much use. He then discussed a new parking lot that could be put in near Reynolds Sweet Parkway. He stated they are putting in the new well house in the park as well. He then stated they are talking about a barn that could be installed at some point. He spoke with Bob Polasky and they are still raising money for the barn installation. They have a drawing to show what it could look like and they will make a presentation to city council at a later date. His concern is it is just a storage shed, and they don't want water and sewer. Discussion was held in regards to other uses and long-term plans for the barn, for events such as a Christmas market, weddings, receptions, HOA meetings, and Girl Scout Meetings. He stated we need to push them to discuss the long-term use of the property. Further discussion was held about other communities that converted some barns for events, and they are used all the time. Discussion was held in regards to the city paying for the water/sewer lines maybe they would be more interested in using it as an event location. Further discussion was held about putting up Christmas lights in the whole village, as well as planting an evergreen and decorating the entire village with lights, but the Historical Village wasn't interested.

City Manager Zelenak then discussed Volunteer Park. He stated we are getting ready to put in the second pollinator garden and we have the design and cost for putting in the parking segment. He stated the new

### 2. Parking

City Manager Zelenak stated we have 300 parking spaces in downtown and 300 more in adjoining lots. We also have the Whipple St parking lot and it is the least used lot. We have hundreds of spaces that aren't always being used because some of our businesses don't like to park in the Whipple St parking lot instead of downtown so that customers can use the spots downtown. He further stated we can look at improving all improvements, but we have to spread the money across all parks, trails, and parking lots. This year we are looking at improving the Farmers Market parking lots. We are researching to do repairs on the south parking lot, and to improve the north parking lot. He also explained we don't want to do a total reconstruction, then tear it out two years later. Further discussion was held regarding the tripping hazards in the south lot, as well as the drainage issue in those lots. He stated we can probably complete farmers market lot within 2 weeks. He stated that includes working on the water issue. If they have to move the parking lot for one Saturday, they may put it in the Whipple St parking lot. Discussion was held regarding how loud the generators are and City Manager Zelenak stated there would have to be a new electric pole installed so the electrical outlets can be used as opposed to all the generators. He then stated all the parking lots are part of the new signage plan. Discussion was held regarding the billboard that needs to be removed from McHattie Park. City Manager Zelenak stated our attorney will be discussing that because we received a letter from Adam Outdoors stating they don't have to take it down. Further discussion was held regarding the new funding options for park improvements and we may be able to do something like that in the future. City Manager Zelenak stated you still have to be very clear, and it is like a gap fund, the city provides a portion, then the crowdfunding covers the rest.

### 3. Comments / Additional Discussion / Next Steps
