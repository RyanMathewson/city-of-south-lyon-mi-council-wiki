## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Fixed asset capitalization policy

Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated the city currently has an internal fixed asset capitalization policy which has been used for several years of $2,500 and based on the item and if it needed to be capitalized. For years now, Plante Moran has suggested that the city review and update the fixed asset capitalization policy to current standards which is $10,000. It also follows the other communities and GASB. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked if it is currently $2,500, what is the reason to increase to $10,000. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated $10,000 is considered standard practice for communities and GASB and our auditors suggested that we change it and adopt an official policy. She further stated anything under $10,000 is considered minimal and it isn't necessary to keep them on the books. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she agrees, but she doesn't know why it took so long. She further stated $2,500 could be a lawn mower. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated we have things that are not traditionally and other municipalities wouldn't have included because our limit was so low. She further stated with this change; it will keep it more standardized with big ticket items such as a generator. She stated there is no advantage to keeping it at the lower amount, it carries more liability on the books. She further stated in the past, $2,500 items were more common, but things are getting more expensive and we have been doing a lot of capital improvement items. Councilmember Kurtzweil asks where the depreciation schedules come from. Finance and Benefit Tiernan stated they are recommendations from GASB.

# TREE CITY USA

# An Arbor Day Foundation Program

WHEREAS In 1872, the Nebraska Board of Agriculture established a special day to be set aside for the planting of trees, and

WHEREAS this holiday, called Arbor Day, was first observed with the planting of more than a million trees in Nebraska, and

WHEREAS Arbor Day is now observed throughout the nation and the world, and

WHEREAS trees can be a solution to combating climate change by reducing the erosion of our precious topsoil by wind and water, cutting heating and cooling costs, moderating the temperature, cleaning the air, producing life-giving oxygen, and providing habitat for wildlife, and

WHEREAS trees are a renewable resource giving us paper, wood for our homes, fuel for our fires, and countless other wood products, and

WHEREAS trees in our city increase property values, enhance the economic vitality of business areas, and beautify our community, and

WHEREAS trees – wherever they are planted – are a source of joy and spiritual renewal.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Steve Kennedy, Mayor of the City of South Lyon do hereby proclaim April 25, 2025 as ARBOR DAY in the City of South Lyon, and I urge all citizens to celebrate Arbor Day and to support efforts to protect our trees and woodlands, and

FURTHER, I urge all citizens to plant trees to gladden the heart and promote the well-being of this and future generations.
