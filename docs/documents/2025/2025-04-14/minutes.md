## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. BPPNA soil gas sampling points

City Manager Zelenak stated BP Products of North America, or BPPNA is requesting an access agreement with the city in order to install soil gas sampling points on city-owned located at 138 S Lafayette. There are already existing sampling points on this property for sampling ground water which consist of 2-inch diameter PVC pipes that extend a few feet below the water table. This new request and access agreement will allow them to sample soil gas. The new sampling points will consist of ¼-inch diameter tubing with fine mesh screens about the size of a thumb. According to BPPNA reps, this is a relatively new additional requirement imposed by EGLE for site investigations. Councilmember Mosier asked how many will they be putting in the parking lot. Douglas Repen of Parsons Corp stated he is here to represent BP. He then stated there will be two locations and at each location there will be one at one depth, and one at a second depth. Councilmember Mosier then asked how long it will take to put in. Mr. Repen stated a half day. Councilmember Mosier stated she was wondering because that is the location of the Farmers Market and she didn't know if there would be any interruptions. Councilmember Kivell asked if when they go in to get the measurements, is it just a metal cap that you pull up to access it. Mr. Repen stated it will be a round 7-inch cover on the ground surface, and inside that two 8-inch diameter tubes that they will connect to and it trickles the soil gas in.

### 2. Stormwater management operations and management agreement

City Manager Zelenak stated the purpose of this agreement is to provide the means and assurance that maintenance of stormwater management and facilities on the development property shall be undertaken by the owner in accordance with the approved plan for the system. a maintenance agreement requires the property owner to follow a plan for routine, emergency and long-term maintenance of all stormwater facilities. The maintenance agreement allows the city to periodically inspect the stormwater facilities to ensure they are operating as intended. If the property owner fails to maintain the storm water facilities, the city may do so and charge the cost of doing so to the property owner. The maintenance agreement shall be binding on all subsequent owners of land served by the stormwater management and facilities, and shall be recorded in the office of the Oakland County Register of Deeds. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked why we need this agreement and why now. City Manager Zelenak stated its for a new development and anything we have to do with stormwater and to ensure it operates properly. We have other agreements in place as well. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she doesn't remember that, but who is responsible for recording that. City Attorney Hamameh stated the agreement requires the property owner to record it with the county. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she would have put it on the city to record the document, not the property owner.

### 4. Appointment of City Representative to Huron River Watershed Council Board of Directors

City Manager Zelenak stated the Huron River Watershed Council (HRWC) is a council of local governmental units that have jurisdiction over property in the watershed. The membership represents the 7 counties and 68 townships, villages, and cities located wholly or partially within the watershed. He further stated the HRWC provides water resource information and research services to member governments. This information includes information regarding water quality issues, land use, groundwater, hydrology, lake management, wetland protection, stormwater management and other related topics. Councilmember Hansen stated he is happy to represent the city in this compacity and to report back to the city any information he gets at the quarterly meetings. He further stated he has full confidence in Suzann Martin as the alternate, and he has gotten to know her really well while serving on the nonprofit they both created and also on the Annual Creek Cleanup.
