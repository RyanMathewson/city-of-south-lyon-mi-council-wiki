## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Call for CDBG public hearing

City Manager Zelenak explained each year the US Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provides funds to jurisdictions throughout the country for projects that meet their national objectives. We receive funds for HUD via Oakland County. This coming year we are applying for funding of $30,000. The 2026 CDBG application deadline for receiving CDBG funds is Friday, December 5, 2025. We are now requesting to schedule our CDBG public hearing on November 10th at 6:30 p.m. It will be published in the local newspaper which gives the opportunity for hearing public comments relating to allocating those funds in the City of South Lyon.

### 2. 2026 Road improvement project

City Manager Zelenak stated the planned road improvements for 2026 is Oak Creek. That is our next project in our multi-year road improvement program. This project will entail replacement of the entire asphalt roadway. Preliminary cost estimate for the project will be approximately $2.1 million. We will be sending out letters to the residents in the subdivision informing them of the meeting date to discuss the planned improvements in January. At that meeting we will discuss bid dates, and construction dates as well, with a follow-up meeting in April/May with constructions plans and more detailed information. We are currently finishing up the design work in the subdivision. The contractor that bid and did the construction project in Eagle Heights in 2025 will hold their prices for the project next year.

Councilmember Hansen asked about the messaging and the timetable. Mr. Alix of HRC congratulated City Manager Zelenak on his long career as a public servant. He further stated they have been lucky to work with him for over 10 years. He wished him the best in his retirement and thanked him for being a steward for the city and the city has been lucky to have him for the last 7 years and he wished the city luck on finding a new City Manager, but he will be hard to replace. He then stated this depends on how quickly we get the project out for bid. He stated the contractor that was used during the last project has agreed to hold the same bid for this project. We typically see an increase in prices between 3% to 8% per year. If Council decides to make the selection and the contractor holds his price, the city will see between $60,00 and $170,000 in savings instead of opening it up on the streets. He further stated we were very happy with the contractor's performance and the expediency they completed the project. They actually completed it ahead of schedule. He then stated as far as public outreach, we'll have to get the new City Manager in place and run it by him, but we are thinking January or February and the bidding would be at the City Manager's discretion. Construction could begin after frost breaks, and it could be completed by September.

## MANAGER'S REPORT

City Manager Zelenak stated the 2026 Pontiac Trail Road project will begin in spring, and they are currently getting easements, and permits are being worked on by various agencies. The total cost to the city will be $1.3 million dollars. He stated we received notice from Oakland County that they will be making adjustments to the Transit contract and we should have a draft soon. He then stated the waste hauling contracts are due November 3rd. He then stated they are continuing to work on the First Responders Monument and the supports for the roof have been installed. He then stated an e-bike ordinance was discussed at a previous meeting and we have made all the changes that were discussed, and City Council will need to decide if they want to establish a resident committee to review and or make comments on the ordinance before bringing it up for first reading. He then stated the Flock Safety will be on the agenda at the second meeting in November regarding an agreement with road commission. He then stated as discussed earlier, Oak Creek will be the next road improvement project, and the contractor that did the road project in 2025 stated they will hold their prices for next year, but Council will need to award the bid at a later date. He then stated we are working on the schedule for the road bond and the approval of the bond authorization resolution that will occur sometime in September and determine if there are any changes in our double A+ rating. He further stated the bond issuance will be 9.5 million dollars and the closing of the bond should take place around April 1st. He then stated staff and consultants are working together to ensure upcoming projects continue to move forward. He has been meeting with staff to make sure the budget process continues to move forward and the new Manager will have the budget process in motion. He further stated the last 7 and a half years have flown by. He then thanked everyone for allowing him to serve the residents of South Lyon. He has worked in municipal government for 40 years. He started in engineering, planning, assessing, building and ultimately the City Manager role for over the last 25 years. He then thanked the staff for accepting and working with him over the last 7 years. We may not have had a Hallmark movie made here in the city, or held the Olympics, but we have accomplished a lot over the last 7+ years. Including the hiring and placement of excellent staff, and management team. The millions of dollars in grants we received, improvements to our parks and infrastructure, the passing of the road bond, the improvements of adding community events, the upcoming $11 million dollar Pontiac Trail project and infrastructure project, and working with staff to create the first multi-year capital improvement plan that put us in excellent financial position helping us to retain our double A+ bond rating. All these years in different types of meetings, he has stood in front of this flag and said the Pledge of Allegiance, hundreds of times, just as tonight, brought him back to his childhood when they said it every day in school. He further stated over the years he feels lucky to have the support of his daughters, and sometimes with his crazy work schedule where he sometimes had to figure out a way to attend meetings and still try to find time to attend his daughters' sporting events, or helping with their homework. He further stated he hopes now he can support them and help them with their careers more.

# CDBG Public Hearing

MEETING DATE: November 10, 2025

PERSON PLACING ITEM ON AGENDA: City Administration

AGENDA TOPIC: CDBG Public Hearing

EXPLANATION OF TOPIC: Each year the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provides funds to jurisdictions throughout our country for projects that meet their national objectives. Here in South Lyon we receive funds from HUD via Oakland County. This coming year our tentative allocation will be $30,000. Last year the City designated the funds to be used for the Senior Center and Haven. The 2026 CDBG application for receiving CDBG Funds is due Friday, December 5, 2025. On October 11, 2025, the City published notice of the CDBG Public Hearing, which tonight gives the opportunity for hearing public comments relating to allocating those funds in South Lyon.

MATERIALS ATTACHED AS SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS: 2026 CDBG Public Hearing Notice

REQUIREMENTS OF TONIGHT'S PUBLIC HEARING:

Open the Public Hearing

Hear any Public Comments

Close Public Hearing.

At the November 24, 2025 City Council meeting we will present and vote on the 2026 CDBG Allocation.

## City of South Lyon Notice of Public Hearing Community Development Block Grant Funds

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the City of South Lyon will hold a public hearing on the use of Community Development Block Grant Funds. The public hearing will be held on Monday November 10, 2025 at 6:30 pm at the South Lyon City Hall 335 S Warren Street, South Lyon, Michigan 48178 for the purpose of hearing public comments on the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Program application to fund eligible projects. All interested citizens are requested to attend the Hearing. Comments will also be received in writing or in person at City Hall until Monday November 3, 2025 at 5:00 pm. Arrangements to reasonably accommodate special needs, including handicap accessibility or interpreter, will be made upon receiving 72-hour advance notice. Contact Lisa Deaton City Clerk/Treasurer at (248) 437-1735 at City Hall for special services.
