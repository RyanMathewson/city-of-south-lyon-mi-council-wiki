## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Conrad stated they have had 1,238 calls as of today. We have had 47 calls in the last 2 weeks which included 31 EMS calls, 8 service calls, 3 hazardous waste calls, one good intent, 3 false alarms and one fire call. She then stated she wanted to let everyone know at the last meeting, she stated the EMS fire truck will be ready in 2027, but it is actually going to be in early 2028. Councilmember Bogert asked if there are still any smoke and carbon monoxide detectors available. Chief Conrad stated they do have some available, and one of our inspectors has been diligently handling the scheduling of the installations.

Chief Unruh stated the 2025 Tahoe has been outfitted and is on the road. She then stated we had Officers attend the Downtown Trick or Treat and this was her first year. She then stated Chief Unruh runs a very efficient department. She then stated a car in front of her hit a small deer on Dixboro, so she stopped and the deer and it was a horrible scene. Officer Stevens came and handled the situation and saw her in the street and he said he was just on his way to her house to give her the council packet. She then thanked Officer Stevens because it was a tough scene to see and he handled it really well. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she is very proud to have Chief Unruh here in the city and she thanked her for coming to South Lyon with her credentials, experience and character.

##  $ \underline{\text{CDBG-}} $ Public Hearing

Mayor Kennedy stated each year the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provides funds to jurisdictions throughout our country for projects that meet their national objectives. Here in South Lyon, we receive funds from HUD via Oakland County. This upcoming year our tentative allocation will be $30,000. Last year the city designated the funds to be used for the Senior Center and Haven. The 2026 CDBG application for receiving funds is due Friday December 5th. On October 11th the city published notice of the CDBG public hearing which tonight gives the opportunity for hearing public comments relating to allocating of the funds in South Lyon.

Mayor Kennedy opened the public hearing at 6:47 p.m.

Judy Keeling of 6180 Arlington Circle stated she can always speak for the Senior Center and she stated it is a great organization and that they can always use more programs to serve the seniors better.

Mayor Kennedy closed the public hearing at 6:48 p.m.

##  $ \underline{\text{MANAGER'S REPORT}} $

Clerk Deaton stated Oak Creek has been selected as the subdivision that will be paved in 2026. We have met with the contractor that did the work in 2025, and they have agreed to hold prices for quantities for next year's work. Council will still need to award the bid at a later date. A meeting with the residents of the subdivision will be held in February, with a follow-up meeting held after the contractor has been selected. We are working on a schedule of the bond, including the approval of a bond authorizing resolution for December 2025 and to determine if there are any changes to our AA+ bond rating. The bond issuance will be approximate $9.5 million, and the closing of the bond should take place around April 1st. Clerk Deaton then reported that the columbarium was delivered last Thursday. Staff is working on the plan to allow sales of the niches in the columbarium and the new Section 11 in the cemetery. The beginning of the year after all of the details of the engraving and other aspects of the burials and purchases are finalized. DPW still needs to put in the sidewalk around and leading up to the columbarium. Additionally, we have begun the planting of the new trees in Section 11.
