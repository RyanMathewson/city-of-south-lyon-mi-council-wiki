### 4. 2015 City Council Meeting Schedule

City Manager Ladner stated the State requires Council to set the dates they will meet during the first month of the year. She stated we normally meet the second and fourth Monday's of the month, but there is a conflict with Memorial Day in May being the fourth Monday of the month, also Council does not normally meet the fourth Monday in December. Councilman Kivell stated historically we have not had a second meeting in December. City Manager Ladner stated the calendar on our website is already up to date with any dates City Hall is closed for Holidays. Mayor Wallace stated we could reschedule the second meeting in May to Tuesday the  $ 26^{th} $.

City Manager Ladner stated we received a thank you note for Officer's Sederlund and Barbour for helping one of our community members and their spouse going above and beyond.
