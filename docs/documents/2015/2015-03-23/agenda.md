# **Regular City Council Meeting**
## **March 23, 2015** **Agenda**

**7:30 p.m.** **Call to Order**
**Pledge of Allegiance**
**Roll Call**
**Approval of Minutes: March 9, 2015**
**Approval of Bills: None**
**Approval of Agenda**
**Public Comment**

**I. Old Business**


1) Second Reading: Amendment to the City of South Lyon Ordinance Adding Regulations
Concerning the Medical Use of Marihuana and Related Land Uses and Activities

**II. New Business**

1) Consider recommendation to be the Lead Applicant on the joint Safe Routes to School Grant
being prepared to improve safety in the 9 Mile and Pontiac Trail area

2) Consider resolution of support for the state ballot issue Proposal 1
3) Consider allowing the City Manager to submit an application to hire a 12 week summer
intern subsidized by the MGMLA Fellowship program.
4) Budget Amendments

**III. Manager’s Report**
**IV. Council Comments**
**V. Executive Session Pursuant to Sections 8 (e) and (h) of the Open Meetings Act**
**V. Adjournment**
