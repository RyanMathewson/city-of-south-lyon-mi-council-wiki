## OLD BUSINESS

### 1. Second Reading: Amendment to the City of South Lyon Ordinance adding regulations concerning the Medical Use of Marihuana and related land uses and activities

Attorney Wilhelm stated at the last meeting there was a comment made regarding a section in the ordinance regarding the inspection of the facilities by City officials. He has done additional research and the Medical Marihuana Act states having a registered care giver card does not give probable cause for inspection and that section in the ordinance should be removed. He stated there may be another way for the City to do inspections to ensure the business is staying in compliance, possibly by some way of the special land use issue to ensure the location is in compliance. Councilman Kramer stated he is concerned regarding how we can ensure they are in compliance with the act, more than the law enforcement part of it. He stated he would like some way for the City to ensure compliance, and right now our Ordinance is silent regarding that. There should be some avenue for us to ensure they are in compliance. Councilman Wedell stated he is concerned with this as well. Some communities are dealing with issues of non-compliance regarding the number of plants allowed, and he would like to see something that will ensure they are in compliance. Councilman Kivel stated the fact they put these metrics in the act, why would the numbers be there if we do not have the ability to ensure they do not have more plants than allowed? We should be able to validate that they are not exceeding the law. Councilman Kivel stated it would be helpful to know what the repercussions are if they are not in compliance, and if they could lose their license. Attorney Wilhelm stated if they violate, we could revoke the special land use. He further stated depending on the violation, it could also be a law enforcement issue. Councilman Kramer stated he would like to see language in the ordinance regulating the zoning use, or land use, in some way to ensure they are in compliance.

1. Consider recommendation to be the lead applicant on the Joint Safe Routes to School Grant being prepared to improve safety in the 9 Mile and Pontiac Trail area

City Manager Ladner stated she is requesting Council to approve the City to be the lead applicant for the Safe Route to School Grant. She is happy to be a part of this.
