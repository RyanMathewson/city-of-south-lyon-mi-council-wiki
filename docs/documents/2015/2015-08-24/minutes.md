### 2. Consider adopting renewal of Huron Valley Ambulance contract

Chief Kennedy stated the HVA tri party contract between the City, Lyon Township and Huron Valley Ambulance came up for renewal earlier this year. Lyon Township desired to increase their transport frequency and ability. Later this year, Lyon Townships Fire Department will receive a new ambulance the Township has purchased. Lyon Township sent notice they wanted to terminate the contract, which opened the contract for negotiations, and through several meetings, we were able to come up with an agreement for all three parties which allows Lyon Township to do more of their transporting than in previous contracts. He stated his prospective is the City is very happy with HVA; they have been an outstanding community partner. He further stated if Lyon Township decided to terminate the tri party contract, we may have had to look at other options; there is a mutual benefit of the City and the Township using the same ambulance service provider. Councilman Wedell asked if there is a change in cost to the City. Chief Kennedy stated there is not. Councilman Kramer asked what if Lyon Township decides to provide their own ambulance services to their residents in the future, where would that leave us. Chief Kennedy stated he doesn't foresee that in the near future, but there are a lot of issues that would need to be worked out. If that did happen we could still stay with HVA, they cover Green Oak Township and Washtenaw County as well, he had confirmation early on from Dale Berry if Lyon Township did cancel the contract, HVA would remain with the City. Chief Kennedy stated he is happy with this contract. Councilman Kivell asked why Lyon Township doesn't do the major emergency events, is it a training issue, or time intensity issue. Chief Kennedy stated it is a training level issue. Right now the City and Lyon Township both operate on basic life support, but we cannot do cardiac monitoring, or advanced airways, so for someone that is acutely ill, they need the services HVA can supply. Dale Berry of HVA stated they are a non-profit nationally accredited agency. He stated they are based in Ann Arbor and serve 144 municipalities. He stated he was a City Police Officer for South Lyon in 1976-1981. He further stated he was the ambulance Chief when the City owned their own ambulance. Mr.

### 3. Consider adopting use of Unsafe structure placards

Chief Kennedy stated earlier this summer the Fire Department responded to a call and from the outside of the house, everything looked fine, but once inside they found a very hazardous hoarding situation. He stated they were able to get the homeowner some much needed help. Had our crew entered this house to respond to a fire in the middle of the night, they would have had no way of knowing the dangerous situation inside. He has spoken with other Fire Chiefs across the State regarding this issue and he is asking Council to allow the use of unsafe structure placards to be placed on buildings that have a hazardous situation. This is a literal red flag to our staff that there is an unsafe issue inside. Chief Kennedy stated this is permitted with the current code. He further stated this is a good idea, but if Council is not supportive, he will not move forward. Chief Kennedy stated in speaking with other Fire Chiefs that currently use these placards, it has caused some of the local businesses to clean up the buildings; therefore it has helped in that way as well. Councilman Ryzyi stated he thinks the City has been lacking with not insisting businesses keep their buildings up. He further stated we need to protect our Firefighters we need to do this, and if it prompts the business owners to clean up their building, that is good too. Councilman Kramer asked why we need to add a scarlet letter by the Fire Department, why can't we force the business owners to tear the building down. He further stated he believes we should do as Brighton has done in the past and take the building owners to court and force them to clean it up or tear it down. He believes this is a good first step, but we should be able to do more. City Manager Ladner stated we are currently looking at Brighton's Ordinance and we are looking at adopting the International Property Maintenance Code. Councilman Wedell stated he is supportive of this, but will we go back again to check the property to see if the building has been improved. Chief Kennedy stated they would check the building annually, but he is also hoping to be in contact with the business/property owners more than annually. City Attorney Wilhelm stated he believes the placards will go hand in hand with Building Code and property maintenance code enforcement, which will offer the potential for additional legal action for the City to force a property owner to make their buildings safe. Council agreed to allow Chief Kennedy to use hazardous placards when necessary.

### 9. Consider demolition bids for 390 S Lafayette

City Manager Ladner stated we received 2 bids for demolition; we were not able to get a bid package out for rehabilitation. She further stated she believes we need more time to get additional complete bids. She feels Council should reject the bids and we will advertise once again with additional time to get more bids. City Manager Ladner stated she received a letter from the code enforcement officer stating the building is rotting out with a horrible stench. Councilman Kivell stated just because it is dirty doesn’t mean it cannot be refurbished. He further stated it is too short of a timeline to gather good information. He stated good decisions are made by actually thinking things through and seeing the possibilities rather than rushing and acting radically.

Councilman Wedell stated he wants to disclose he knows Mr. Hoffman who is one of the bidders. Mr. Hoffman worked as a County Commissioner when he worked for the Board of Commissioners, but that will not forfeit him from the discussion or vote. He further stated the two bids appear to be based on two entirely different bases. One was based on a tour and one was with remediation of asbestos and lead paint, the other one just stated there would be additional fees. He stated he agrees with the City Manager that we reject the two bids that were received and re-bid the demolition project and require they either tour the building or include any remediation with the bid.

Councilmember Kopkowski stated she is all for getting rid of blight, but she would like to see all aspects of all information. She stated she is not against tearing it down, but we need to look at what will happen after it is torn down. There is a change in zoning ordinance that needs to be taken into consideration. We need more information then we should make a decision. Councilman Ryzyi stated he agrees that we need to give more time for bids to come in, but he does want this item on the next Council Agenda.
