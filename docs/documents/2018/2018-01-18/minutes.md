## NEW BUSINESS

1. Interview candidates to fill the vacancy on City Council created by resignation of Joseph Ryzyi

Mayor Pelchat explained the framework of how he would like this process to go. He would like the candidates to draw numbers from a hat which will decide what order the interviews will be done. He further explained he would ask that each candidate in fairness to exit the room while the other candidates are being interviewed. He stated no one has to leave the room due to this being an open meeting, but in fairness he would like them to do so. Mayor Pelchat then stated each candidate will have 3 minutes for an opening statement, then Council will have 10 minutes to ask questions. He further stated each Councilmember can ask each candidate 2 questions, then each candidate will have 2 minutes for a closing statement.

Councilmember Kurtzweil stated we have a difference of opinion. The Open Meetings Act states a person shall not be excluded from a public meeting except for a breach of the peace at the meeting. No one should feel under any obligation to leave the room when someone is speaking at the microphone. She then reminded the public we had a Mayor's forum and each had a microphone, they were being filmed and they were asked questions, and they did so in the public view, no one was asked to leave the room.

One way this could have been handled would have been to have candidates come in to be interviewed at different times. She will not participate in any meeting where anyone is excluded from the meeting. Mayor Pelchat stated no one has to leave if they don't want to. Councilmember Kivell stated the distinction between us scheduling individual interviews and doing this with the request of leaving the room doesn't seem like such a stretch. Everyone is here, this doesn't extend or shorten the term we will conduct the interviews, just because it is an open meeting, doesn't mean the validity of the request should be recognized, and the request is just until after the candidates interview is concluded. The objective is so no one parrots what someone else says, it gives you the opportunity to create a more substantive answer than you might have if you're on the spot answering the questions from the body.

Councilmember Kurtzweil stated there is no reason to ask anyone to give up their right to attend an open meeting. There is no validity to do so. We are dealing with seasoned adults that can sit here and listen to other people's answers. She spoke with individuals today that were shocked this was even going to be asked. There is no reason to violate the open meetings and no one should feel pressured to do so.

Councilmember Parisien asked Attorney Johnson because the Open Meetings act uses the word shall, would this be a violation by asking the applicants to do this. She said but if we are asking people to leave and it is their choice, given that they have the right to be here, but if they choose to leave, is that a violation of the OMA. Attorney Johnson stated no, it is not a violation.
