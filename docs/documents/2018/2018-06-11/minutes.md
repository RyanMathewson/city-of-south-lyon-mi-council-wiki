##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $- Downtown

Bob Donohue the Downtown Development Director stated he wanted to thank the DPW crew who put up the farmers market fence and shed. He stated it looks great and it was already used for the first time last week. He further stated there was a lot of buzz downtown today with the last section of the Lake Street Tavern being done. The brick crews are on site, and they have to do all new brick, it is more expensive than originally thought, but the owner is following through with it. Mr. Donohue stated they started stripping off the roof at 115 and 117 Lafayette and the 115 Lafayette building is in better condition than they thought. He further stated the new owner of the A&E Jewelers building came in and filled out the paperwork, he will be doing a Victorian paint scheme on that building as well. He stated at the end of summer we will have a big media event, and it is a new day in downtown South Lyon and people are starting to realize that. Just a generic update, we are making good progress with 4 of the 5 building owners. We are we are moving forward.

He stated his last item is he was at a workshop in Monroe last week, and what was interesting was the capital improvement plan is a subject of how it is a signal to developers that the downtown is part of that plan.

Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she has been pushing for a capital improvement plan for years. She stated it is important because the developers look at the capital improvement plan because if the City isn't putting money into the downtown they will think why they should. She further stated the capital improvement plan is critical to provide insurance to investors and developers that they have a partner in the City, which hasn't happened in the past. She further stated we have talked about it, but we still don't have one in place. She further stated if you are going to get serious money because they want to know that the City is coming to the table and has a plan and a vision of where they want this to go, and she supports this program 100%. Mr. Donohue stated the developers are well aware of things that need to be fixed downtown. He has had developers ask about the condition of the parking lots, and light poles that need to be painted. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated if she is going to spend that kind of money in downtown, she would want to know that 5 years from now, she is dealing with a responsible City. She further stated we can't even keep up Paul Baker park and that is a park that is at an entrance to the City. She further stated that is why the developers are looking at the City to see if they can manage the assets they have. She stated they want to look to see what has been budgeted for financial planning for the downtown.

##  $ \underline{\text{New business}} $

1. Consider award of bid recommendation letter from Dixon Engineering for water tower repainting project

Ron Beason, the Chief Operator of the Water and Wastewater plant. He stated this started in 2016 and the idea was for Dixon Engineering to give us a summary of what needed to be done with the inside and outside of the water tower. In the summary they included a 50-page summary and a cost summary which at that time was $231,000. He further stated the total was $271,000 with the engineering and contingency plan. At that time, we generated a line in the budget, along with $117,000 for professional services, but that was put in the wastewater budget, it should have been in the water budget. If you look in the previous years, it was there. He doesn't know how that mistake happened. He stated we missed the timeline for this to be done in this spring, there are very few companies that do that and there is a very small window to have this done. He further stated we knew we missed the window, so we used that money for the VFD's. He further stated he had discussed this with Council before. That will keep the water pressure going while the water tower is down. Mr. Beason stated it took 30-40 days to paint the tower. He stated the plan this time is for 50 days. There were 7 bids and there were 2 bids Dixon Engineering preferred we not accept the bids, some reasons were quality of workmanship and some was the time frames. He stated we have to stay within that timeline. There is no room for error, if we lose one or two of the pumps, the City would be without water. He further stated they separated the line items so we can delete anything we don't think we need. One of the items is a mixer to agitate the water that keeps the water from freezing. We don't have that problem here. He further stated you normally see those with communities with little use. We don't have that problem, we don't have a sediment problem, therefore we don't need the mixer. By eliminating that Horizon Brothers would still be the lowest bidder, but Dixon asked us not to use them. He stated the second would be LC United. He further stated we currently have $20,000 in professional services, and $175,000 for the painting inside and out in the budget, so we will have to ask to use some of the enterprise money for this at this time. Councilmember Kennedy stated the useful life of the coating is 15 years or so, and we are currently at 22 years. Mr. Beason stated it was painted in 1999. Councilmember Kennedy stated the need to move quickly to secure our place in line to have this done during the small window. Mr. Beason stated we are already a little late, some bids were very high. The high bidders were probably booked and didn't need the work.

Councilmember Richards asked if the DEQ have any objections about using a contractor. Mr. Beason stated no. Councilmember Richards stated we could do it ourselves. Mr. Beason stated we don't have the experience or equipment. Councilmember Richards then asked if any of these companies would split the work to save money. Mr. Beason stated it is inspected by Dixon Engineering. Councilmember Richards stated he understands we need this but isn't there a way to save $40,000. Mr. Beason stated there is not. He stated these companies have the equipment, they have the expertise, and they are bonded, we are fully covered.

Councilmember Parisien asked if we could apply for grants for this. Mr. Beason stated he doesn't think so, this is a maintenance issue and it is time sensitive, but he can ask Dixon Engineering.

Councilmember Kurtzweil stated you have $178,500 in the budget, but you are short $63,000, and you have money in the enterprise fund. She stated the enterprise funds are there for that purpose. She stated
