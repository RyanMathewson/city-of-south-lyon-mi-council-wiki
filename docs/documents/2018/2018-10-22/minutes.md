He stated we don't want to delay the land use application only to receive a recommendation from the Board of Ethics, most of the fact are clear. There is a procedure in the Charter to be followed. The Ethics Ordinance provides for roles or interests in a Councilmember that are incompatible of those duties that may impair their vote.
