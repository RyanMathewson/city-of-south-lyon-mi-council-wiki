## OLD BUSINESS

### 1. Withdrawal Letter from Tom Turner, Spurt Industries, LLC

Mayor Doyle stated that we received a letter from Spurt Industries withdrawing their proposal. Council will need to accept that letter.

Ms. Erica Wilson of 952 S. Parkwood and Parks and Recreation Commissioner stated that the Commission held a meeting in January where the proposal was put forth. During that meeting they voiced their concerns, and concerns of residents were also voiced. She stated that she is disappointed with the chain of communications. During the January 26 $ ^{th} $ Council meetings, the concerns were brought up again by her during public comment, but no mention by the City Manager. She further discussed the leaf collection that the City dumps at the park as well as other items such as appliances, etc. She stated she would like that dumping stopped.

Mr. T.J. Connelly of 22286 Brookfield Drive stated that residents need to pay more attention to what is on Council's agenda. He discussed public perception of civil service and elected officials. He stated that a large percentage may see the Council as trying to push something through. While Council would say that is not true, one could suspect that Council, with the exception of Council Member Kopkowski, tried to get this done quickly and under the radar by not giving necessary time to review the contract and not notifying our border communities that this could affect them. He stated that Council acted lawfully, but did they act ethically. He thanked Council for the retraction and Mr. Turner's letter of withdraw.

Ms. Maggie Kurthzweil of 939 West Hills Drive stated that she was before Council in January to ask Council to take their time in making this decision. She stated that there is a level of distrust with some of the members of Council. The community was outraged and not one member of Council gave it some thought. She further discussed the impact on the community and property values. That is not the kind of decision maker she wants representing her. She further stated that she would hope that we all have learned a lesson. This was a bad decision, with the exception of Council Member Kopkowski, and she would hope that there is a process put in place when decisions are made where property values are being affected. The outrage is appropriate, and maybe in time it will go away.

Mayor Doyle thanked everyone for being present. It was a show of support and the community coming together. He further stated that Council has listened.
