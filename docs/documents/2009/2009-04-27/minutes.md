## PUBLIC COMMENT:

Linda Depoorter of 646 Maple Drive stated she did a fire hydrant survey of surrounding cities regarding fire hydrants on personal property. Brighton, Novi, Northville and Plymouth all inspect hydrants on private property at no charge. They also do all the maintenance at no charge, with Plymouth being the exception. If there is a more expensive fix that needs to be done, Plymouth will charge for it. She further stated at one time there was a house bill being discussed regarding local governments inspecting all fire hydrants once a year, but as of right now that bill has not been passed. She further stated condo owners don't get all the services other tax payers do, yet they are charged the same taxes. She asked the people in the audience to raise their hands that are here in support of this issue. Many people in the audience raised their hands.

Jerry Chandry of 22195 #1 Quail Run Circle stated the condo owners do all their own roads, snow plowing and now their own hydrants yet they still pay the same taxes as other people. He feels that condo owners are paying more than their fair share and it seems they should get a reduction in their taxes.

Mayor Doyle stated as of right now there is a number of hydrants on private property. The City Manager and the Chief of Fire are currently checking into the cost and the manpower for checking all the hydrants, then it will be brought back to council for further discussion.

## OLD BUISINESS:

### 1. Resolution for DWRF

Discussion was held regarding the process of obtaining the low interest loan. Jesse VanDeCreek stated that this resolution is the next step of the process. He further stated this does not obligate the City in any way, it just approves Hubbel Roth & Clark to apply for the loan/grant for the City. We did have a public hearing on March 30th, they have not received any public comment as of now. He stated if approved by the state, the next process would be to go out for bids.

## CM 4-3-09 RESOLUTION FOR DWRF

## Motion by Morelli, supported by Maida

WHEREAS, the City of South Lyon recognizes the need to make improvements to its existing water treatment and distribution system; and

WHEREAS, the City authorized Hubbell, Roth & Clark, Inc., Consulting Engineers, to prepare a Project Plan, which recommends the upgrade of the existing 4" and 6" water mains located throughout the City; perform necessary maintenance on the Elevated and Ground Storage Water Tanks; upgrade the existing Backup Power System at the Water Treatment Facility; remove and replacement of the antiquated Induced Draft Aerator at the Water Treatment Facility; install an additional Low Service Pump at the Water Treatment Facility and investigate the potential of a New Raw Water Well Site; and

WHEREAS, said Project Plan was presented at a Public Hearing held on March 30, 2009 and all public comments have been considered and addressed;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the City of South Lyon formally adopts said Project Plan and agrees to implement the selected alternative (Alternative No. 3).

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City Manager, a position currently held by David M. Murphy, is designated as the authorized representative for all activities associated with the project referenced above, including the submittal of said Project Plan as the first step in applying to the State of Michigan for a Drinking Water Revolving Fund Loan to assist in the implementation of the selected alternative.

## VOTE: ___

## MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY

## COUNCIL COMMENTS

We are only liable if someone trips and falls because the sidewalk was broken or not level. We are only liable for travel on the right-of-ways, not conduct on the right-of-ways. If someone gets drunk and falls, or gets in to a fight, it will be the restaurant's liability.

Councilman Wedell stated he had heard there was a ticket issued to Primal. Attorney Lee stated we requested to authorize a warrant for both a civil infraction and misdemeanor. The decibel levels were 78. The police will issue the tickets this week. He further stated there were no complaints on this past Friday night, but there was a couple on Saturday, and the police officer went out, but didn't think it was as loud as the previous weekend.
