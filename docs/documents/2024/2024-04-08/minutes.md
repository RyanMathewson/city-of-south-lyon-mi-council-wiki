## PUBLIC COMMENT- No public comment was made

## DISCUSSION- Downtown

City Manager Zelenak stated there are 71 vendors scheduled for the May 4th opening summer Farmer's Market. He then stated Director Mack met with the Chamber to discuss a dog walk event they will be hosting in South Lyon. He then stated the Ladies Night will be on Friday May 10th and the DDA will be partnering with Active Faith for a food drive that night. He further stated Director Mack submitted items to secure funds allocated for Lafayette Park in the amount of $301,000. Councilmember Dilg asked about the capacity for vendors the Farmers Market. City Manager Zelenak stated he believes it is 70-75. Councilmember Dilg then asked if there is still going to be music and having roads closed. City Manager Zelenak stated Director Mack is working on that and it should be the same as it has been in the past.

## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Fire Chief Thorington stated they have had 321 instances as of today. He then stated they are caught up on the installation of smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, other than waiting for people to call back to schedule. He then thanked the South Lyon Police Department for all their help, they are way more involved in patient care than many other departments. They are doing an excellent job and he appreciates their help on calls. He then stated Alex Good is a long-time firefighter and he has taken a 3 month leave of absence for personal reasons, and he is one of our top shift fillers and he will be missed, and we hope he'll be back in 3 months. Chief Thorington stated they now have their new pagers, and Novi is working to accommodate them. Councilmember Dilg asked how many smoke and carbon monoxide detectors have they installed. Chief Thorington stated 75-100. Councilmember Dilg stated they should share that on social media and that is great. Councilmember Bogert asked about the open house. Chief Thorington stated it is going great and Fire Marshall Moynihan is doing a great job. The only thing different will be we won't have live fire sprinkler demo because that is being used elsewhere that week.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Police Chief Baaki thanked Chief Thorington and said the fire department is always doing a great job. He then stated they are having their drug take back program on the 27th between 10-2. They are mainly looking for narcotics, but if anyone has any prescription drugs they aren't using and want to get rid of, they will take them too. Then take they will go to the DEA on Monday. He stated Officers participated in the Easter Egg Scramble on the 30th and they had a great time. They had a good turnout. Chief Baaki then stated they now have the final date for the claim for the Emergency Declaration that was made for the storm and its for about $39,000. Councilmember Bogert asked if the drug take back will be at the police station. Chief Baaki stated it would, and they will have an officer there and there will be a flyer posted out front. He stated they will always take them, but this is for a public service announcement and it is done throughout the state and we just want to get the drugs off the streets. Councilmember Kivell stated he seen when the police were out while they were doing the advertisement. Chief Baaki stated that was a PSA for motorcycles and they were filming something for don't drink and ride. They were portraying someone going through a stop sign.

Linda Benson 438 Washington Street and Housing Commission President, thanked the South Lyon Police Department for noticing one of the security cameras at the apartments was not working and they were able to get it working again and keeping our children safe.

## MANAGER'S REPORT

City Manager Zelenak stated Washington Manor will be on an upcoming agenda. He then stated at the next agenda we will have the first reading for the ordinance amendment to change the meeting time from 7:30 pm to 6:30 pm. He stated the change will go into effect in July. He further stated construction is continuing on 318 W Lake Street. He then stated the road bids for Eagle Pointe and Brookfield Subdivisions are due on April 16th and the project will be on the agenda for the next Council meeting. City Manager Zelenak stated that staff have been working with Patriot Concrete for the removal and replacement of a new concrete wall along the railway that is north of 10 Mile Road. He then stated we are moving forward with the Pontiac Trail paving project that is an 8-million-dollar project. It includes

some storm sewer improvements as well. Mayor Kennedy stated he wanted to discuss a couple of things he and the City Manager have been discussing over the last few weeks. He stated the first is recognizing Dayna Johnston and her invaluable contributions and volunteerism to the community. He said they discussed a Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year award, which is something where someone would be recognized based on whatever the criteria are, and selected by an established group of individuals. They would be recognized for their efforts in the form of a plaque and there would be a board kept at City Hall listing all the names of the people that received that recognition over the years to come. He then stated he is looking for Council's opinion if we should move forward with this. It was the consensus of Council to move forward with the award. Mayor Kennedy then stated he and the City Manager have also been discussing the removal of the billboard at the edge of McHattie Park. It is up for renewal in 2025 which will then renew for 10 years. He stated the goal would be to consider having it removed to move forward with the further design of McHattie Park and putting the entrance and parking lot there as has been discussed in the past. He stated he is looking for Council's opinion if they are interested in asking the City Manager and City Attorney to move forward with cancelling the contract. It was the consensus of Council to move forward with having the billboard removed to move forward with the plan for McHattie Park. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated her friends at Oak Creek are asking about the Andover Bridge. City Manager Zelenak stated he is waiting for a delivery date. City Manager Zelenak stated the plans are available at city hall as well as in the council packet that is online from the meeting where Council approved it. Councilmember Kivell stated he doesn't understand why the city is paying for the 37 feet of tube for tying into the sewer. City Manager Zelenak stated that was our portion of the sewer that collapsed and we have made similar repairs around town. Councilmember Kivell stated he is delighted there is something happening at Lake Street Tavern, but he didn't see any permits. City Manager Zelenak stated he isn't aware of any activity that would require permits, but he will check into it. Councilmember Dilg stated she is pleased the wall by Patriot Concrete will be repaired, it seems more and more dangerous every time she walks by it and its scary when kids are climbing on it.
