# City of South Lyon City Council Budget Meeting April 4, 2024

Mayor Kennedy opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m.

Mayor Kennedy led those present in the Pledge of Allegiance

## ROLL CALL

Roll Call: Mayor Kennedy and Council Members: Dilg, Hansen, Kivell, Mosier and Kurtzweil. Also present: City Manager Zelenak, Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan, DDA/Economic Development Director Mack, DPW Superintendent Archey, Fire Chief Thorington, Police Chief Baaki, Water & Sewer Department Superintendent Beason. Council Member Bogart arrived at 6:52 p.m.

## NEW BUSINESS

1. Presentation of the draft 2024-2025 Budget

City Manager Zelenak state that the budget was prepared and was based upon City Council recommendations, staff inputs, and review of needs of departments and the community. City Manager Zelenak gave an overview of the 2024-2025 Budget. Please see attached document. City Manager Zelenak stated that the Budget is scheduled for adoption at the May 28, 2024 City Council meeting.

### 2. Comments/Additional Discussion/Next Steps

Mayor Kennedy inquired about the Administrative Department budget is estimating a $15,000 increase to GL Account line item #101-222-817 Planning Consultant. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated that the requested increase is due to increased activity and subsequent fees paid to CIB Planning, City Planning Consultant, for potential and ongoing development projects in the city. The increase has been trending for the past few years resulting in the need for a Budget Amendment at fiscal year-end.

Council Member Dilg asked why the Election Worker GL Account line item #101-222-818 was not increased from Fiscal Year 2023-2024. Finance & Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated that City Clerk/Treasurer Deaton has made several election equipment purchases in current budget year that will reduce the need for items in the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 budget. Funds in this line will be going towards election inspector pay for training and working the City's elections in August and November 2024.

Council Member Kurtzweil stated that the City's budget includes a millage rate increase from the current budget year for General Operations. City Manager Zelenak explained that the budget numbers are reflective of expected revenues that are received based upon taxable values provided by Oakland County Equalization and the proposed maximum allowable millage. City Manager Zelenak stated it is necessary to be able to fund the vast services and projects in the upcoming year.

Council Member Kurtzweil asked what the proposed wage increase is for employees. City Manager Zelenak stated that non-contract employees are budgeted for a 4% increase while Union employee increase will be based on current contract. Three City Unions have contracts expiring on June 30, 2024 so those rates are yet to be determined; a 4% increase is currently estimated in the budget.

Council Member Hansen inquired about funds listed under Beautification GL Account line item #101-222-971.100. Council Member Hansen stated he would like to see lighting of current signs and a possible "Thank You for Visiting" sign when existing the city limits. He discussed similar signs in Dexter.

Police Chief Baaki discussed a needs in addition to the Police Department budget that is not currently listed in the draft documents. A mandatory update to the Police Vehicle computers must be completed by October 2024. This will involve the replacement of (6) vehicle computers and mounting equipment for approximately $19,000 to GL Account line item #101-301-820. Council Member Kurtzweil recommended that the city look at replacing a couple of the units this current Fiscal Year to lessen the burden of the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 budget. Chief Baaki stated he will look into ordering one or two units now.

Council Member Kurtzweil held a discussion about increasing the funds budgeted to renovate the DPW Complex Kitchen and Bathrooms. Superintendent Archey stated that $35,000 is budgeted for those repairs and to replace kitchen appliances under the DPW Department budget GL Account line item #101-441-930 Repairs and Maintenance. Council Member Kurtzweil exclaimed that she feels $45,000 is needed to completely redo the facilities to the standards the employees deserve. Superintendent Archey stated he has spoken with the DPW staff to get their input on what repairs/updates they would like done. He stated that the employees are really interested in getting updated appliances to improve the kitchen. Superintendent Archey also gave a brief update on the status of this current budget year's repairs and maintenance of the DPW complex.

Council Member Hansen asked if Council would consider adding membership to the Huron River Watershed Council to the DPW budget GL Account line item #101-441-830. Benefits of membership include governmental advice, grants, outreach and education. Membership also provides the city with board membership. The cost is approximately 11 cents per capita; $1,300 per year.

Council Member Dilg inquired about the plans for a pollinator garden budgeted for Volunteer Park in GL Account line item #101-751-978 Capital Improvements. City Manager Zelenak stated he budgeted for grading and the planting of clover and/or wildflowers. City Manager Zelenak further stated that additional gardens or improvements such as a parking lot and irrigation system would need significant more funds and planning for future budget years. Council Member Kivell requested that the pollinator garden and improvements to surrounding areas such as a parking lot needs to coincide with the Master Plan too.

Council held a lengthy discussion with DDA/Economic Development Director Mack about the budgeting for the Social Media Marketing Consultant under GL Account line item #248-801 Professional Services Contractual. Council Member Hansen suggested that the Social Media Marketing Consultant would be a great asset to the all the South Lyon Businesses and promotions of the city. City Manager Zelenak stated that if Council was interesting in expanding the role of the Social Media Consultant services to outside of the DDA, that it would need to be added to the Administrative Department budget. Council Member Hansen suggested a possible part-time employee could provide Social Media Marketing to both the DDA and the city. He recommended that a marketing framework be put in place prior to the Pontiac Trail Road Project to get people engaged in the community and want to return despite construction. Council Member Kivell has concerned if the city would see the payback on this investment. City Manager Zelenak stated the return of investment may not be tangible in numbers of visitors or dollar amounts. Mayor Kennedy reminded Council that SEMCOG created a promotional video and it could be brought back in circulation on the cable channel and in social media.

Mayor Kennedy asked if there will be increase in bike racks to the Downtown next year. DDA/Economic Development Director Mack stated that item is an upcoming agenda topic for DDA Board.

Council Member Kurtzweil inquired why the DDA budget is projecting a deficit in Fiscal Years 2025 and 2026. DDA/Economic Development Director Mack explained that the State of Michigan and Accounting Standards require the DDA to keep Fund Balance amounts below a certain threshold. Since the current DDA Fund Balance is over 100%, the DDA Board is strategically spending down the funds with Capital Improvements. Council Member Kurzweil suggested the DDA consider using those funds to improve the downtown lighting.

### 3. Public Comment

There was no public comment.

### 4. Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned by 7:56 p.m. with a motion by Council Member Kurtzweil and seconded by Council Member Hansen.

Respectfully submitted.
