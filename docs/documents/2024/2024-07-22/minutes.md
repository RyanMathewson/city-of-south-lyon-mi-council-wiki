## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Lieutenant Sederlund stated they participated in the Touch a Truck in Lyon Township on the 17th and Chief Baaki said it was well attended even though it was cut short due to the weather. He further stated the Pride Event was on the 14th and a great turnout and there were no issues reported. He then stated they are beginning to conduct interviews for the administrative assistant position on Thursday of this week and they are hoping to have the position filled by the end of the month. He then stated the drone came in and they are setting up training for two operators. Second reading of ordinance to amend the official zoning map of the City of South Lyon by conditionally rezoning portions of the total 34.17+/- acre parcel on the east side of Dixboro, south of Ten Mile/Lake Street, parcel #21-30-126-003, zoned I-1, light industrial, as follows: 24.46+/- acres of RM-3, multi-family residential and 1.63+/- acres R-3, single family residential. Planner Smith stated at the May 13 $ ^{th} $ Council Meeting, they approved the first reading of the ordinance amendment and the preliminary site plan. She then explained some elements of the plans are now shifted slightly to the west to accommodate the new footpath. Bob Langen of Elevate Land Holdings of 128 N Center Street in Northville, stated the benefit of moving the pathway to the east side of the sub will keep a road crossing avoidable. 1. Award sidewalk replacement project to Luigi Ferdinandi and Son Cement Co DPW Superintendent Archey stated the sidewalk replacement project is being set up to replace any sidewalk that has been heaved or misaligned due to the root system of our city trees. Luigi Ferdinandi and Son Cement Company was awarded the contract last year and did a wonderful job. The contractor will be directed in which areas to address. The plan is to start in a subdivision and complete that area and move to the next. The contractor will be removing and replacing 300 flags of misaligned sidewalks that are creating trip hazards. They will also be removing and disposing of the tree roots that are causing the problem. The contractor is also performing all restoration. He further stated this project is in the current budget. Councilmember Kivell asked how much sidewalk needs to be replaced. Superintendent Archey stated last year they did 300, he is hoping 300 will get the 4 subdivisions done, there is a lot in Hidden Creek. He further stated his goal is to hit the 4 areas, and he is hoping to finish them all within 2-3 years. Further discussion was held regarding the replacement of sidewalks.

City Manager Zelenak stated they have put down the base coat on half of the road in the Eagle Point subdivision, and now they will start the milling of the other side of the road, then the whole top coat for the entire sub. He then stated they have started to remove some of the concrete sections in the Brookfield subdivision, and it continues to move forward. He then stated the 2024 concerts in the park have been very successful and two Friday's ago we had the event with the food trucks, and we had over 300 people for the event. He stated we had a South Lyon talent as the warm up band for Phoenix Theory and it was a good crowd and everyone enjoyed the evening. He further stated we had over 300 people at the last concert in the park as well. Councilmember Dilg asked for an update on the bike racks. City Manager Zelenak stated Nate has been working with Superb Fabricating but he doesn't know if they've been completed. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated it was a rough week last week. She said her phone was blowing up because many people's garbage wasn't picked up, and some other people's yard waste wasn't picked up. She further stated people were waiting for hours on the phone to get through. She then stated one of the employees told a resident they don't have maps of the city and she doesn't believe that. She further stated they picked up for one resident and they were rude. City Manager Zelenak stated a lot of the municipalities had problems. He then stated a lot of subdivisions contract with GFL and are now with Priority are not necessarily in the city. The first week of July wasn't good, they came back to locations several times. The second week was worse, but this past week was much better and this week so far has been better. We have been in contact with RRASOC and other jurisdictions, and we are now seeing more equipment on the road every week, and we will continue to meet to ensure the residents garbage and yard waste is picked up in a timely manner. Councilmember Kurtzweil asked what is their reason for having so many problems. City Manager Zelenak stated they didn't have enough trucks, and they didn't have enough people. They were picking up garbage on the 4th of July and all through that week, they had delays, but as long as we see they are responsive to us and things will get better and better. He further stated we have a grading system, which A would be no calls, and this past week would be a B grade. Mayor Kennedy asked if residents should call city hall if their garbage isn't picked up. City Manager Zelenak stated they should contact city hall and Mary has all of the contact information, and she has been documenting everything.
