## PROCLAMATION

Mayor Kennedy read the attached proclamation for Mark Savedes of Alekos.

## PUBLIC COMMENT

Ralph Dunigan of 216 E Lake stated he doesn't understand the consent agreement. He stated when the cruise first started, they were asked and they said they would come every year to ask if they wanted it. They said they would try it the first year, the following year when they came, they said they didn't want this, and they said it is too late the city has already approved it. It's been approved each year since, without asking them. The Police have a form on their website for block parties, and every person on the block has to sign it or it isn't allowed. That is basically a block party because you're closing the street, and without asking them. He again stated that for a block party, everyone has to agree before the road can be closed, but the city is basically having a block party 5-6 times a year without asking us and assuming that they are ok with it. He further stated they don't want it, there have been numerous problems, and cars have been hit and there is no place to park. He wakes up at 3 in the morning and he isn't allowed to park in his driveway until 9:30 at night. He said he can't park at 9:30 at night and get up at 3:00 in the morning and drive a truck all day. He then stated his wife gets home at 6:00 and in her high heels, purse and brief case, she has to park a block away and walk home. He doesn't think anyone else would do that, and he is very upset that it was approved, and no one asked them. He stated he has spoken with 3 or 4 councilmembers telling them they didn't want this last year.

Stacy Kissel of 228 E Lake Street a few houses down from the previous speaker and she is here to support her neighbor. She stated they were also asked one time at the beginning and they were supportive of it, but over the years, it has grown cumbersome. It is very large, and it is one thing to live close to town, but it is another thing to have strangers camping out in chairs on your front lawn. She stated she is also concerned that now that Lake Street Tavern is closed, which was their headquarters, she doesn't know how that will spill out into the town. She further stated that is 2 of them on a block with 4 houses that aren't in support of the events. She isn't asking to not hold the events, but look at where it is mapped out. The way it is currently set up, it is very loud, they can't have their windows open, there is a DJ playing the same music every time just blaring. She stated it is an annoyance more than anything. She further stated she isn't asking for it to be shut down, she wants everyone to have a good time, but the homeowners would like to be considered.

Jennifer Dunigan of 216 E Lake Street stated she wanted to make sure everyone received the letter she sent asking Council to vote no on the event. She stated it is a page and half letter stating why they are asking for Council to not shut down Motorfest, or car cruise, but to vote no on the closing of the street. She further stated she is sorry if they didn't read the letter, but she would hope they would listen to their constituents.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Baaki stated he was happy to help with the Creek Clean Up yesterday, there were a lot of people there. He and the Fire Chief were working behind Kroger. He then stated he has been working on their frequencies with Livingston County dispatch so they will be able to communicate with Green Oak Township and Livingston County. Councilmember Bogert asked Chief Baaki to speak about the speed limit signs. Chief Baaki stated they have a two total, and one is near the railroad tracks on Pontiac Trail. He further stated it was on Hagadorn for a couple of weeks, and it counted 5,000 cars, with an average speed of 19 mph. Chief Baaki stated it is harmless, seniors go out and try to spray each other with water guns. Usually it is harmless, we don't get many complaints about it. Mayor Kennedy stated he is aware that Chief Baaki met with the City Manager, and the organizers of the cruise in shows and looked at some things to help avoid some of the things in the past happening. # Office of the Mayor

## PROCLAMATION

WHEREAS, Aleko's Restaurant and Catering has been serving delicious food in South Lyon for over 30 years, owner Mark Savedes describes Aleko's as a service-oriented business; and

WHEREAS, Aleko's Restaurant and Catering has been rated one of the best restaurants in the area and has been meeting the needs of food lovers in the South Lyon area; and

WHEREAS, Aleko's Restaurant and Catering has been an important, caring part of the community contributing to many causes and needs without hesitation, volunteering for local and international projects, and surprising local residents with Christmas gifts or just paying bills; and

WHEREAS, the personal motto of Mark Savedes is “To whom much is given, much is expected” and prefers to live for a greater purpose; and

NOW THEREFORE, I, Stephen B. Kennedy, Mayor of the City of South Lyon on behalf of the City Council and the entire community offer congratulations to Aleko's Restaurant and Catering as they celebrate over 30 years in business and wish them many more years of success.
