# Rules of Conduct for Public Comment at Council Meetings $ ^{*} $

Members of the public may speak at a Council meeting upon recognition by the Mayor. Public comment may only occur during periods designated on the agenda for public comment or a public hearing. A person may speak for up to two (2) minutes during each of the two public comment periods on agenda items or non-agenda items. Waivers of the time requirement may only be granted in the discretion of the Mayor, and waivers to speak at a time other than a designated comment period may only be granted by the Council. Any person wishing to make a presentation longer than two minutes or requiring audio-visual equipment is asked to contact the City Clerk requesting to appear on a future agenda.

A person may only address Council from the podium. Only one person may occupy the podium at a time. All remarks are to be directed to the Mayor and Council. Speakers are not to engage in direct dialog with other meeting attendees.

Any person who violates the Rules of Conduct, disturbs the peace at the meeting, and/or interferes with the meeting may be warned, ordered to be seated, removed, and/or ticketed.

 $ ^{*} $This summarizes Council Resolution 04-18. Complete Rules, including guidelines for considering waivers, are available in the Council Chambers and from the City Clerk.

# City of South Lyon City Council Budget Meeting April 4, 2024

Mayor Kennedy opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m.

Mayor Kennedy led those present in the Pledge of Allegiance

## ROLL CALL

Roll Call: Mayor Kennedy and Council Members: Dilg, Hansen, Kivell, Mosier and Kurtzweil. Also present: City Manager Zelenak, Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan, DDA/Economic Development Director Mack, DPW Superintendent Archey, Fire Chief Thorington, Police Chief Baaki, Water & Sewer Department Superintendent Beason. Council Member Bogart arrived at 6:52 p.m.

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Presentation of the draft 2024-2025 Budget

City Manager Zelenak state that the budget was prepared and was based upon City Council recommendations, staff inputs, and review of needs of departments and the community. City Manager Zelenak gave an overview of the 2024-2025 Budget. Please see attached document. City Manager Zelenak stated that the Budget is scheduled for adoption at the May 28, 2024 City Council meeting.

### 2. Comments/Additional Discussion/Next Steps

Mayor Kennedy inquired about the Administrative Department budget is estimating a $15,000 increase to GL Account line item #101-222-817 Planning Consultant. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated that the requested increase is due to increased activity and subsequent fees paid to CIB Planning, City Planning Consultant, for potential and ongoing development projects in the city. The increase has been trending for the past few years resulting in the need for a Budget Amendment at fiscal year-end.

Council Member Dilg asked why the Election Worker GL Account line item #101-222-818 was not increased from Fiscal Year 2023-2024. Finance & Benefit Administrator Tiernan stated that City Clerk/Treasurer Deaton has made several election equipment purchases in current budget year that will reduce the need for items in the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 budget. Funds in this line will be going towards election inspector pay for training and working the City's elections in August and November 2024.

Council Member Kurtzweil stated that the City's budget includes a millage rate increase from the current budget year for General Operations. City Manager Zelenak explained that the budget numbers are reflective of expected revenues that are received based upon taxable values provided by Oakland County Equalization and the proposed maximum allowable millage. City Manager Zelenak stated it is necessary to be able to fund the vast services and projects in the upcoming year.

Council Member Kurtzweil asked what the proposed wage increase is for employees. City Manager Zelenak stated that non-contract employees are budgeted for a 4% increase while Union employee increase will be based on current contract. Three City Unions have contracts expiring on June 30, 2024 so those rates are yet to be determined; a 4% increase is currently estimated in the budget.

Council Member Hansen inquired about funds listed under Beautification GL Account line item #101-222-971.100. Council Member Hansen stated he would like to see lighting of current signs and a possible "Thank You for Visiting" sign when existing the city limits. He discussed similar signs in Dexter.

The applicant held this event for the past two (2) years and because it was very successful, would like to hold the event again this year. Based on the nature of the proposed event, it is considered a temporary use similar to a farmer's market, which is subject to special standards and requires approval by City Council. Since the previous approvals were only for those specific years, they must re-submit the application for the 2024 event.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION: The applicant is proposing to hold the market event at the Biggby Coffee located at 22729 Pontiac Trail, in the open green space between the Biggby Coffee and Lucas Coney Island. The proposed event will have 5-25 booths (roughly  $ 10' \times 10' $ each, two rows of booths total) for K-12 students to showcase their entrepreneurial work. A sketch plan of the proposed temporary use has been provided by the applicant. As noted on the plan, the green space area will be used for the temporary use. Parking will use spaces in the Biggby parking lot, with the school parking lot to the west indicated as overflow parking, as needed. The owner of the property has provided their consent and support for the use. The Police Department and Fire Department have reviewed and approved the proposal. The proposed temporary use meets the applicable ordinance standards. The applicant has provided a certificate of liability insurance. Proposed signs will be applied for separately under a sign permit.
