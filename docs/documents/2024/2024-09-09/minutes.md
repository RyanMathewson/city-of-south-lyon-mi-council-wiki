### 1. Award bid for city landscaping maintenance contract

City Manager Zelenak stated we have been looking for a contractor to do landscaping and maintenance at several of our city-owned properties. He further stated the list includes new plantings at entrances to the city and at some of our parks. He further stated he has been in contact with no less than 6 different landscape companies. Some of which followed up and met at the locations. Others did not respond after several attempts via email and phone calls. Lightening Landscaping located in South Lyon has been excellent to work with. He stated they met him several times at our locations. He further stated they offered suggestions on landscaping material, improvements and working to develop a contract for installation and maintenance. He then stated he has been soliciting bids since 2023, he has been contacted the 7 companies which some are within the city and some outside the city. He stated he has provided them information about the project and the locations and providing them ideas and photos of the locations. He further stated after dozens of emails to the different companies, he is now recommending Lightning Landscaping to perform the work. He stated other companies that requested information were Pure Earth, Integrity Landscaping, Lucia Landscaping, South Lyon Lawn Care, and Munro Landscaping. He further stated he requested information on plantings, maintenance schedules and other information relating to the bid. Some provided the information and others didn't supply the data. He further stated he met with 3 of the companies at different locations, but only one provided estimates, sketches, and listing of plant materials. He then stated Lightning Landscaping provided a full year's maintenance agreement and they are willing to work with the city and the care to provide an opportunity for just a few locations the first year, then follow up with future recommendations for plantings and maintenance based upon our needs. He further stated he requested bid information, including sketches and maintenance. He stated he did not share Lightning Landscape information with other bidders. They took the time to design each of the beds, and an estimate on each of the locations. He said it would be unfair to give their bid information to other companies so they can undercut the bid. He further stated he had to set the limits on the number of contacts made with other potential bidders, if the companies refuse to follow up or not provide bid information as requested, that is not who we want to hire to do our landscaping if they don't want to follow up with the contacts, we don't want to follow up with them and hire them to perform the maintenance. City Manager Zelenak then stated he has been working with them for a couple of months, and they have been following up on calls and requests. He then stated a Councilmember made the suggestion that we possibly lower the number of locations for the first year, and Lightning Landscaping is ok with that.

### 2. Property Maintenance Ordinance- First Reading

City Manager Zelenak stated this has been discussed briefly at a prior council meeting while discussing No Mow May. It pertains to the property maintenance ordinance. He stated specifically tall grass and noxious weeds. Councilmember Dilg stated she doesn't like the language that states the City and other public entities are exempt. City Manager Zelenak stated this is for residential and commercial properties, not the city schools. We wouldn't issue a ticket to ourselves. Attorney Kolb stated the zoning ordinance doesn't apply to the city. She stated it would be very unusual for a jurisdiction to comply with the property maintenance code. Councilmember Dilg stated it is a bad look to have that language. She further stated while we are enforcing our ordinance on business owners and residents, there have been time that the city's property hasn't looked good and was overgrown. She then asked if we are missing a step by issuing notice in the paper, and then we send someone on their property without notice. City Manager Zelenak stated our actions in putting this together isn't to raise revenue, we are trying to prevent people from having long grass. Councilmember Dilg asked how someone would know if someone was authorized to be on their property. City Manager Zelenak stated they would be given a notice ahead of time. Councilmember Kivell stated we need to be responsible for our own properties. Councilmember Hanson stated having the language stating city doesn't have to comply is something like, do as I say, not as I do. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated she has a problem with the language that talks about being over 8 inches. City Manager Zelenak stated the next sentence speaks about not including trees, shrubs or cultivated plantings and gardens. Attorney Kolb stated that is not new language, that was in the original ordinance. Councilmember Kurtzweil stated milkweed is no longer defined as a noxious weed. City Manager Zelenak stated he doesn't want to list everything that this could include, but it only includes some. Attorney Kolb stated the property maintenance code currently stated the penalty is a misdemeanor. She further stated when it was adopted in 2014 there was not a specific penalty named, so it defaults. She further stated we can also add the language that the penalty will be a civil infraction. City Manager Zelenak stated most communities have the penalty as a civil infraction.
