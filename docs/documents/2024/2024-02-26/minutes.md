## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Thorington stated they are currently at 204 instances. There was a structural fire in town yesterday and we had two on shift, and two on site having coffee, so we had a truck leave with 4 firefighters and it was a quick knockdown. It was extending outside of the house up into the eves and they knocked it down quick and saved the house. Our cadet Soave recently turned 18 and has his Fire 1 & 2 through the Howell Fire Academy and he's currently in the EMT class and should be completed by June and then he'll be a full-fledged Firefighter. He then pointed out some highlights of the Fire Departments annual report. He stated Micah DeMeyer was the 2023 Firefighter of the year. 2023 was the busiest year for the Fire Department history. He then stated between 2018 and 2023 was the most significant increase. 2020 was an anomaly due to COVID. All the departments around South Lyon went down in 2020. There are multiple theories on why the rise across the state and the US. Everything from increase in population, increased elderly population, more people coming through the community, and a different mindset on when to call 911, and its hard-to-get doctors' appointments, so if people are feeling sick, they call 911. It can be construed by some as abusing the system, or not the best of the use for it, but he feels if anyone has a need to call them, they are like problem solves and they will show up and help them or at least steer them in the right direction. He would choose to have them call and err on the side of safety. He further stated their EMS calls were 64% and the balance was fire rescue and other service calls, which is pretty standard across the industry. We had 47 mutual aids given and 5 received. He stated it looks like an off number between the given and the received, but we give aid to multiple communities and only receive from one. He further stated they feel the mutual aid is important because they have automatic aid with Green Oak Township, on both sides of us so anytime we're dispatched to a structure fire they automatically start moving in our direction, but we can cancel at any time. It is really the only way for us to get the minimum recommended NFPA number on scene which is 16, without that we would have a hard time getting to that number and its great training for our people who can't get the hands on experience of being in fires, which we can replicate the best we can on our training facility with fires, but it's not the same as live structure fires with the chaos that that ensues around you in an uncontrolled environment. Councilmember Bogert stated he wants to acknowledge Chief Thorington and his staff for a well put together annual report.

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Purchase of new Boerger PL300 rotary lobe pump

Water/Sewer Department Superintendent Beason stated they are requesting the purchase of a new Boerger PL 300 rotary lobe pump to replace a failing piston-type sludge transfer pump. He stated the new rotary lobe design is easier to maintain and allows for better performance and efficiency. Recently the wastewater department has replaced 3 older piston pumps with the Boerger lobe type and has found these pumps to be very reliable with less maintenance. He further stated this is a scheduled purchase for this year's budget and can be purchased out of capital improvements line item 592-557-972 in the amount of $14,982.00 Councilmember Kurtzweil asked why this isn't going out for bid. Superintendent Beason stated this is the only company that will provide this. They are the representative for Michigan for that pump. He stated it is the type of lobe that we need for our facility.
