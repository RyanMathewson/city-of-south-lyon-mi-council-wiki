 $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $ - Downtown

DDA Director Mack stated the Farmers Market is going well, even though there were some cancellations due to weather, and even if they cancel, their spots are spoken for. The vendors said the holiday weekend went well. He then stated the DDA Board will be meeting next week and the results of the Transformation Strategy session will be presented by the facilitator, and it will then be shared with City Council after the board approves it. He further stated the DDA Board will be working on their mission/vision statement for Oakland County Main Street program. He further stated they are planning the Holiday Spectacular and they are looking at having the carriage rides there. Councilmember Bogert asked about the controversy about the South Lyon Pride Event. Councilmember Mosier asked what is the process for the liquor license for vendors such as the Twisted Cork and the Witches Hat. Mr. Mack stated that is a different process, it is for  $ \frac{1}{2} $ a season vendor and it offers 5 days of liquor license and Diana gives them a spot and their location would remain their spot, they wouldn't be moved to another. ## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Thorington stated there have been 587 instances to date. He said they have 3 openings in the Fire Department. He further stated they are waiting on parts for Engine 7-1.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Baaki stated they will be ordering the new car computers that are required for the new software updates implemented by CLEMIS. He stated they have ordered their drone and are preparing officers for training on its use, and it's a very capable drone. He further stated Lisa Werner left us and they are advertising to hire a new Administrative Assistant. He then stated that pending background checks, they will be hiring 2 new cadets. Councilmember Dilg asked about the drone. Chief Baaki stated the drone can help look for people. It is infrared, it has spot lights and he is impressed. Councilmember Dilg then asked about the license plate readers that Lyon Township have. Chief Baaki stated he can contact them; he thinks the cameras cost $3,000 per camera per year.

## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Award of bid 2024 roadway preventative maintenance program

City Manager Zelenak stated as part of our efforts to make further improvements to our city road system, we went out for bid for crack sealing segments of our local roads. This contract is to conduct overband crank fill and asphalt mastic repair. He further stated we received 2 bids.

## COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilmember Kurtzweil thanked the Cultural Arts Commission for putting together the tribute to Dayna Johnson on the boards throughout the city. She then stated everyone should take some time and visit this exhibit of her life. And a PDF of the pictures will be delivered to Craig and Doug Bakki. Again, thank you to the Cultural Arts Commission for this project of tribute. She then reminded everyone to attend our concerts in the park, they are great events and she encourages all her colleagues on Council to host one of them.

Councilmember Dilg stated she agrees with Councilmember Kurtzweil, she also has enjoyed seeing the images of Dayna Johnston downtown and she thanked the Cultural Arts Commission for honoring her. She then stated it looks like a great weekend ahead with the concert in the park, the Farmers Market and the Pride in the Park event.

Councilmember Bogert reminded everyone that South Lyon Pride event is this Sunday between 12-4pm and the young entrepreneur event is Saturday between 10-3pm.

Councilmember Hansen stated the clover is coming up very nicely in his backyard.

Mayor Kennedy reminded everyone about this Friday's Concert in the Park that will be held in McHattie Park on the ball fields. It starts at 6pm with our guest performer Leah Brooke and then followed by the Phoenix Theory, a great band that has previously performed at the Christmas in July event last year at Heinanen Engineering. There will be several food trucks at the event so you can grab something to eat while you enjoy the music. And remember we're also having a food drive for Active Faith, so please bring along some non-perishable food items to donate. He then stated on Friday, July 19th there will be the first Movie in the Park in McHattie Park. There will be bounce houses, games and crafts for the kids and Snowy Owl will be there with their shaved ice delicacies. The movie will be the Lorax. Pre-movie entertainment starts at 6pm and the movie begins at dusk. He then stated he wanted to recognize and thank the McCauley Family for taking the time and effort to perform some repairs and repaint our Little Free Library in McHattie Park. The Little Free Library was originally built in August of 2016 by the Friends of the Salem-South Lyon District Library and has been providing a variety of free reading material for young and old alike since then. The Friends of the Salem-South Lyon District Library include about two dozen volunteers and stewards who are part of an extraordinary effort to keep that library maintained and stocked. Last week, one family of those volunteers, the McCauleys, retiled the roof and repainted our Free Little Library. He then thanked Marcus, Emmy, Clara and Nora McCauley. And thank you also to all of the volunteers who pitch in to help take care of this jewel in our community throughout the year.
