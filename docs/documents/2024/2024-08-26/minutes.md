## NEW BUSINESS

Installation of 750 kw standby generator at Wastewater Treatment Plant

Water/Sewer Superintendent Beason stated they have allocated funds for this fiscal year's budget for the purchase and installation of a 750kW standby generator. He further stated the generator has been ordered and is expected to be delivered between late September and early October. HRC has provided the installation specifications and submitted them for bidding. The completion date is October 10th. Three contractors responded, with bids ranging from $193,010.00 to $499,536.00 After reviewing we are recommending O'Donnell Electric. Councilmember Bogert asked how the generator is fueled and what is the run time. Superintendent Beason stated it is fueled by diesel and it holds over 16,00 gallons and will last a 24-hour period. He further stated they also have diesel on standby. Councilmember Hansen stated he was taken aback by the difference in costs between the bids. Superintendent Beason stated they are all electrical contractors, so they will be sub-contracting the jobs out, and as long as they stay with the specs, he isn't concerned. Councilmember Hansen asked if we have worked with the contractor in the past. Superintendent Beason stated he has not, but HRC has. Councilmember Kivell asked if everything is going to work out fine and there's no concern having to augment the well. Superintendent Beason stated we also have a standby tank and generator. It is enclosed in a double wall tank and will sit on top of the tank. Councilmember Kivell asked if he is relying on the fact, it is in a double wall unit it won't leak. Superintendent Beason stated all the underground tanks have been taken out and all are above ground. Councilmember Kivell asked if anyone uses natural gas. Superintendent Beason stated they have not, and the diesel engines last a lot longer.
