DDA Director Mack stated he is happy to announce the Doctors Physical Therapy opened last week, and the Wellness Drip is a medical spa and will be opening this week. ## NEW BUSINESS

1. Purchase of two (2) Kaeser rotary blowers for the grit chamber at the Wastewater Treatment Plant

Water/Sewer Superintendent Beason stated the Water/Wastewater Department is seeking approval to purchase two Kaeser Rotary Blowers, specifically the Grit Chamber Model BB52C complete with outdoor-rated sound enclosures. He stated the existing blowers installed in 2003 are deteriorating due to exposure to corrosive gases from the grit chamber. He further stated to mitigate this issue, the new blowers will be relocated to minimize exposure to the gases. He then stated the purchase is included in this year's budget under line item 592-557-972 in the amount of $44,476.00.

### 3. Priority Waste status

City Manager Zelenak stated the City of South Lyon's waste hauling contract was assigned to Priority Waste on June 30, 2024. He stated since that time their performance for regular pickup of garbage, recycling and yard waste has been inconsistent. He stated tonight we have representatives of Priority Waste in attendance to address our concerns and update us regarding the transition in taking over waste collection for our community. Andrea Cruz of 5880 Hollow Corners Road, Dryden Michigan stated she is the Chief Sales Officer and Sam Caramagno of 17441 Merriman Road introduced themselves. Mayor Kennedy stated it's been about 12 weeks since Priority assumed the contract for the city and over 75 other communities, and he knows in that 12 weeks, his yard waste was only picked up on the right day two times. He further stated he would like to know the path to get us back to the service we had with GFL Ms. Cruz thanked everyone for their patience, and she stated the transition has not been ideal for everyone. She stated they weren't anticipating the fleets they purchased and the condition they were in. She further stated they have done a tremendous amount of work in the last couple months with outside repair companies, and hiring additional mechanics to do everything they can possibly do to get the fleet back in shape, in addition to renting trucks from all across the Midwest to try to get them caught up. She then stated she is happy to say today, they started with the actual number of trucks they should have. The repairs are working and she commends her maintenance staff and they have dedicated people, and they have been working 7 days a week since July 1st, and it has not been ideal for anyone. She further stated when they talked about the transition, they thought it would take about 90 days, and we are about there. She then stated the trucks will have cameras within the next week, so the city will get their own logistic driver which will be able to see the cameras with GPS.
