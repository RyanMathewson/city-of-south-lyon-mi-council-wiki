## NEW BUSINESS

### 1. Nyhart GASB 74/75 actuarial valuation

City Manager Zelenak stated each year we receive our GASB Actuarial Valuation. The report represents the funded status for our post-employment benefit plans, other than pension plans. Finance and Benefit Administrator Tiernan explained the Nyhart report is based on information through June 30, 2024 and the MERS reports was based on information through December 31, 2024. She stated the interest rates are more favorable in this report. This actuarial has a discount rate of 7% and this is a bit lower than the previous year which was at 7.35%. She stated the assumed interest rate is the same as last year at 3%,

<a id="p4"></a>
*[Page 4 of the original](https://www.southlyonmi.org/12-9-24%20CM%20Approved.pdf?t=202501141312220#page=4)* (read by OCR; check the original)

which is the same as last year. She further stated we are using the same mortality tables, which is the same mortality tables that MERS use for the pensions. They are the most valid rates. She then stated it was started in fiscal year 2017, and we started our trust in 2021. She stated we paid as we went; we weren't paying down the liability. We only paid the actual costs for the retirees. She further stated we started funding our liabilities in 2021. She then explained in 2020, the city established the OPEB trust through the MERS retiree health funding vehicle. She stated the State of Michigan requires all municipalities that all full-time employees hired as of July 1, 2018 be included in OPEB trust. It requires an actuary be done annually to determine the actual costs of the employees eligible to receive benefits. She further stated the normal cost is basically the minimum that must be funded to the trust each year. She then stated the city decided in 2021 to budget and pay over the normal cost to include an ADC which is actuarily determined contribution. She then stated the ADC is a target contribution which if paid ongoing annually will provide sufficient resources to fund the future for the services to be earned, and liabilities attributed to the past services. An example is this report shows our normal cost is $6,240.00. She further stated the city budgeted for that and paid $73,600.00 so now the city is paying down on the liabilities. For the current fiscal year, it is budgeted to be $78,400 and that was paid to the trust this year. By doing this, we are decreasing our overall liability. She then stated as of June 30, 2023, our liability was at $686,141.00 and was funded at 42%. She then stated as of June 30, 2024 the city's liability is $784,474.00 with a funded ratio of 51%. She stated the liabilities have gone up, but we have increased our funded portion. The reason the liabilities have gone up is the total amount of active participants in the trust and the number of retiree participants. We currently have 47 active employees that qualify, and we have 5 total retirees. She further stated it has also gone up for the different stipends depending on the different union contracts. She further stated employees with 1-9 years of service are our largest group of employees. The next group with the highest numbers is the 20-29 years, which means they could be retiring in the next few years, so we will be making additional payments. She then discussed a report that has to be given to the State of Michigan, and they would like to see 35% funded, and we are at 51%. Further discussion was held in regards to funding the liability.
