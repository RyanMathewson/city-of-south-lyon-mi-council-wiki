### 1. CDBG- Call for public Hearing

City Manager Zelenak stated each year HUD provides funds to jurisdictions throughout the country for projects that meet their national objectives. In South Lyon we receive funds via Oakland County. He further stated he is asking Council to schedule the CDBG public hearing for Monday, November 11th at 6:30p.m. This will be published in a local newspaper which give the opportunity for hearing public comments relating to the allocation of the funds.

# South Lyon Police Department "Safeguarding Our Community"

To: Ofc. Angela Bay

From: Chief Douglas Baaki

Date: 10/28/24

Sgt. John Tomanek nominated you for a Department Citation for your actions surrounding the fire at the Corner Social, incident number 24-8277.

On the early morning of 10/21/24 around 1:24am you detected an odor of smoke in the area of Lafayette and Lake St. You then quickly located a fire on the rooftop of the Corner Social. You had dispatchers call out the South Lyon Fire Department, Sgt. Tomanek arrived as well to assist. While awaiting their response, you and Sgt. Tomanek attempted to put the fire out with the extinguishers from your patrol cars, however they were ineffective. You and Sgt. Tomanek began trying to evacuate any residents in danger from the fire and were able to locate one resident in a nearby apartment who was quickly evacuated without injury. Once the South Lyon Fire Department arrived, they were able to extinguish the fire which was spreading quickly. The fire would have most certainly been more serious causing further property damage and potential harm to citizens had you not been on patrol in the area and acted quickly in response to the fire.

The South Lyon Police Awards Committee has unanimously voted to award you a Departmental Citation for your “Outstanding actions involving risk to your safety and of a nature which brings unusual credit to the Department and its members”.
