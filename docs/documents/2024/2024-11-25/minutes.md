## DISCUSSION- Downtown

DDA Director Mack stated this weekend is the first indoor Farmers Market at the

VFW on Saturday from 10am – 2pm, and they have 30 vendors. He then reminded everyone that this weekend is Shop Small Business Saturday. He further stated that it is a very important day for our small businesses, not only in the downtown, but throughout the city and throughout the country. He then stated that for every $100 spent at a local business, $68.00 of it stays in the community, as opposed to $43.00 when you spend money at a chain store. Shopping small businesses keeps our community vibrant and keeps money in our community. Mr. Mack then stated that Waycaster Tiki will be opening on December 3rd at 228 S Lafayette which is good for them. He then stated the garland and lights are up downtown, and the Holiday Spectacular is happening on December 7th, from 3pm to 6pm and there will be ice sculptures, characters that will be walking around town, and the parade starts at 6pm. Mayor Kennedy asked if they are still looking for volunteers, and groups that want to participate in the parade. Mr. Mack stated they are looking for participants.

## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Thorington stated they are currently at 1,090 instances and it has been a busy month, they are catching up to last years pace. He stated they had their burn building training but without live fire, they used the fake smoke machine, so they could get the cadets and kids under 18 years old in. If it's a live fire, they can support, but they can't go inside. He then stated they were approved by FEMA for an addendum to their grant for the smoke alarms. Colonial Acres has a lot of old hard wired smoke detectors. He stated the previous grant only covered the battery smoke detectors, but now they have approved 100 wired, battery back up smoke detectors, as well as carbon monoxide detectors. They are out for bid right now, with bids coming in early January. He further stated there is also assistance to firefighters grant right now that is due December 20th, and they are going to put in for some gear and we'll look at it a little more closely to see what the priorities are. Councilmember Hansen asked Chief Thorington about the FEMA training he attended. Chief Thorington stated it was training for larger type of events, such as the straight-line winds that occurred here a couple years ago, such as the paperwork to fill out, managing a larger incident and who to talk to at FEMA.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Baaki stated we have 7 officers that will be taking the drone training next month, and we have the defensive tactics training, which we have to do one session every year to keep up certifications. He further stated they will be going through a legal update next month. He then stated on the hiring front, they are having oral interviews tomorrow with a candidate, one finishing up the background check, and hoping to get that person in by the end of next month. He further stated they will be sending one of the cadets to the Oakland Police Academy.

# Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year Award

Earlier this year we lost a dear member of our community, Dayna Johnston. Through the years Dayna, was instrumental in so many of the activities and events that took place throughout South Lyon. She touched so many lives in our community, and so many people were affected by her actions, while never fully realizing who was responsible. She was always there and ready to help with whatever needed to be done to ensure that an event was successful and that everyone enjoyed it. Whether it was organizing the Memorial Day and Holiday Spectacular parades; doing facepainting and making balloon animals at Depot Day and the Farmers Market; or brightening spirits with her murals on the store fronts throughout the town, Dayna gave so much of her time for the benefit and enjoyment of others.

Today, I have the honor of introducing a new tradition – one that will recognize the incredible contributions of our volunteers. Starting this year, we will present the Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year Award. This award will recognize a volunteer whose dedication has made a lasting impact on our City, someone whose selfless actions have inspired others and strengthened our community, just as Dayna did.

Here tonight to help us commemorate this recognition are several members of Dayna's family including her husband, Craig Johnston; her son David Johnston and his wife Jen Johnston; her granddaughter Charlie Johnston and grandson Hunter Johnston; Her daughter Kellie Fehrenbaker and her husband, Stephen; Dayna's grandson Jaxon Fehrenbaker; her sister Carol Diglio and brother-in-law Clem Diglio; her brother, and our Police Chief, Doug Baaki and his wife Staci Baaki and their daughter, Dayna's niece, Emma Baaki. And last, but certainly not least, Dayna's mother, Sandra Mullen, who made all of this possible.

## We thank each of you for sharing Dayna with us over all of those years!

The first recipient of this award is someone whose contributions have truly set them apart. This individual has dedicated countless hours to numerous initiatives over the years, and demonstrated an unwavering commitment to making our community a better place for all of us. Their efforts have touched many lives, and we are honored to celebrate this individual today. This year, and each year to come, the recipient will receive a plaque commemorating their contributions. In addition, their name will be engraved on a plate and will be added to the commemorative board which will be placed in the City Hall offices. The City Manager has the board for display.

So, thank you, to all of our volunteers, past and present, for the incredible work you do. And now, I am honored to recognize the very first recipient of the Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year Award—someone whose dedication stands as an example for us all. Please join with me in congratulating Suzann Martin as this year's award recipient!

# 2024 Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year Award

The City of South Lyon hereby recognizes Suzann C. Martin as the inaugural recipient of the Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year Award.

Suzann's long-term dedication and commitment to the City of South Lyon is reflected in the variety of committees of which she is a member. Suzann has been a conscientious advocate of protecting our environment through her countless hours serving on the Wellhead Protection and Storm Water Management Committee, the Watershed & Stormwater Committee of the Michigan Environment Association, the Water Environment Federation, the Michigan Water Environment Association, the South Lyon Area Historical Commission, the Environmental Advisory Board to U.S. Congresswoman Elissa Slotkin, and the South Lyon Water Guardians.

Suzann embodies the spirit of commitment to volunteerism here in the City of South Lyon that makes her the inaugural recipient of the 2024 Dayna Johnston Volunteer of the Year.

Suzann, we thank you for all you have done for the community of South Lyon.
