##  $ \underline{\text{DISCUSSION}} $- Downtown

## FIRE CHIEF REPORT

Fire Marshall Moynihan stated they have had 291 runs for the year. He said they have a long waiting list for the fire and carbon monoxide detectors and they are waiting for the state. He stated they have installed 40 of the combined smoke/carbon monoxide detectors, and 28 battery only detectors. He then stated they are currently planning their yearly open house, and it will be on the 18th of May from 11-3. He further stated they won't have the sprinkler truck this year, but they are looking at other activities for the kids, such as a scavenger hunt, and they will be doing a can drive for Active Faith.

## POLICE CHIEF REPORT

Chief Baaki stated they just received a check for 1,000 as part of a leadership grant through MMRMA. He stated he will be trying to get another grant check for just over 900.00 He then stated they just hosted the Citizens Police Academy, and they talked about use of force and control operations. He then said he and the City Manager and Benefit and Finance Administrator went to MSP North on Thursday last week to discuss the disaster relief funding we are supposed to get. We have to get the paperwork by the  $ 11^{th} $ of next month to ensure we get any funds we are able to. He then stated FEMA was here for 2 weeks, and there was a lot of online stuff, as well as people coming in from other jurisdictions as well. He hopes everyone was able to use that resource. Councilmember Bogert stated he attended the Citizens Police Academy and he really enjoyed it. He stated it was interesting to understand the process of elevation, and Chief Baaki and Officer Walton did a really good job. He then stated he was speaking with another officer and he said we needed another Officer on staff. Chief Baaki stated they currently have one in the academy, but they do need one more. It is challenging, everyone is competing for the best people. Mayor Kennedy stated he has spoken with people that attended the Citizen Police Academy and they said it was really great.

2. Purchase of new 14K standby generator for the water tower

Water/Sewer Department Superintendent Beason stated they are requesting to purchase a new 14KW stand-by on-site natural gas generator for the water tower located on Mill Street. He further stated the water tower does not have an onsite generator and in emergency power outages department personnel must haul a portable generator to the site and manually connect to provide temporary power. He further stated an on-site standby generator equipped with an ATS will allow a continuous uninterruptible power supply for lighting, heating, and PLC at the water tower. Councilmember Hansen asked if the generator will be within the fenced area. Mr. Beason stated it would be. Councilmember Hansen then asked if there was a reason PM Technologies bid came in so low. Mr. Beason stated they also bid on the big generator and each company came and looked at it, and they all had their own ideas of how they would add the lines for the hook up and run the electric. Councilmember Bogert asked if there is no generator at there and there is a power outage, is there still water there. Mr. Beason stated there is. There is a battery back-up, but once it is drained, we don't have our PLC and for security reasons we shouldn't talk about all that is there.

Further discussion was held regarding the generator.

## MANAGER'S REPORT

City Manager Zelenak stated we closed on the property at 318 W Lake St last week, and the purchaser has already submitted their plans to the building department for the construction of the new home. He stated the bid information has gone out for the road improvement program at the Eagle Pointe and Brookfield Subdivision and they are due on April 16th. The project will be on the April 22nd agenda and the expected start date will be after schools are out for summer. He then reminded everyone city hall will be closed on Friday March 29th because it's Good Friday. He then stated RRASOC are working on a pilot program for food scrap recycling to be added to the yard waste. It is being tested in Wixom right now, and he will inform Council if it is expanded. Councilmember Dilg stated she would be interested in seeing if there is a way we can have a rule that if a local company comes in within the certain percentage of the lowest bid, they get the opportunity to match so we could give some consideration to those local companies. City Manager Zelenak stated that would have to be a policy, but you would have to be very careful to ask someone to match, because then you would have to go to the other companies, and if they go even lower, you want to consider that as well. You would also have to define what a local business is, city, township, Oakland County. And there would have to be a justifiable reason regarding a particular company, if they are familiar with the plant, so there are other factors, but to spend more money on something, there has to be more reasons to do so. Councilmember Dilg stated it is something interesting to think about and she has seen it before in policies with municipalities that says if a local company comes within a certain percentage, they get the opportunity to match the low bid. Councilmember Bogert stated there was a post today that the swings were missing from McHattie Park.
