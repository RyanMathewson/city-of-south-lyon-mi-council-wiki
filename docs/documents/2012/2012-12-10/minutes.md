## NEW BUSINESS:

1. Community Center Committee

Councilman Kramer and Steve Fletcher from Lyon Township, presented a power point slide presentation regarding the community survey results. Councilman Kramer stated he is very encouraged after reading all the results to the survey. Over 80% in all three communities want a community recreation center. He further stated swimming and workout programs were the most requested. Councilman Kramer stated he has spoken with Salem Township as well, and they have shown some interest. He further stated he is asking for Council to approve $10,000.00 to be used for moving ahead with this project. Councilman Kramer stated Lyon Township has already agreed to spend $10,000.00 for moving forward as well. Some discussion was held regarding how the money would be disbursed.

## CM 12-3-12 MOTION TO APPROVE COMMUNITY CENTER MONEY

Motion by Wedell, supported by Kivell

Motion to approve $10,000.00 to be used by the Community Center Committee to explore the possibility of building a community wide recreational center and to have the City Manager bring a budget amendment to the next Council Meeting to properly account for this budget change, with the understanding the $10,000.00 will be paid back to the City if a mileage is passed at a later date.

#### 4. First reading for Amendment to Code of Ordinances; Ch. 90, Section 90-38

Chief Collins stated that in 2010 the state law changed with respect to drunk driving, at that time they changed the super drunk law. Anyone arrested with a blood alcohol level of .17 or higher, is qualified for increased penalties. In February 2012, the state law changed, and is now allowing the local municipalities to adopt a section of the state law that would allow local prosecution of “super drunks”. That will save us time and effort of using the County prosecutor for a complaint and warrant, and we could use our own City attorney at the local level. He further stated, an added benefit would be the increased fines, and revenues that will stay with the local court, and return to the local municipalities.

### CM 12-5-12 MOTION TO ACCEPT FIRST READING AMENDEMENT TO CODE OF ORDINANCES; Ch. 90, section 90-38

Motion by Kivell, supported by Wedell

To approve the first reading of the amendment to Chapter 90, Article II of the South Lyon City Code to amend section 90-38, "Penalties".
