## PUBLIC COMMENT:

Nancy Kern of 450 W. Liberty stated that although she is for tax abatements, more jobs and boosting the economy, she is not in favor of a tax abatement for Michigan Seamless Tube. She is against it and against the management of the facility. She further discussed her objections and the needed clean-up of toxic chemicals at their site including the contamination investigation. She discussed the last addition to the facility and funding assistance through the Oakland County Economic Development Corporation. She discussed the increased noise at the facility. She stated that she is concerned about the quality of the environment not only for herself but the community and neighboring communities.

### 2. Purchase of Toolcat

Superintendent Renwick stated that during budget talks he had discussed the purchase of a Utility Work Machine. At this time he is requesting to move forward with the purchase in the amount of $66,835.00 which includes accessories. This is available through the state bid. However, we can get a better deal by going through the Bobcat dealer. He discussed the machine as well as the accessories.

## CM 7-4-11 – PURCHASE OF TOOLCAT

Motion by Kivell, supported by Wedell

To approve the purchase of a 5600 Turbo Toolcat Work Machine with noted accessories at the delivered cost of $66,835.00

Councilman Wedell asked if this is replacing equipment. Superintendent Renwick stated that we do have a smaller machine that we use for cleaning sidewalks, etc., and we will continue to use it but it is 11 years old. This new equipment will be better suited for working on the trail, etc.

Councilman Wedell asked if when this 11 year old equipment can no longer be repaired will we also be looking at replacing it. Superintendent Renwick stated that we will probably go a different route, but we are trying to make it last as long as we can. He further discussed the various uses of the equipment.

## MANAGER'S REPORT:

City Manager Murphy asked Superintendent Martin to give an update on the new well. Superintendent Martin stated that the well is on line and we are just waiting for the security fence, which will be finished by the end of the week. This job was done without any cost over-run. Discussion was held on permits for the well. Superintendent Martin stated that we hand delivered the permit information, but kept waiting for a response. They indicated that they were overworked and short of staff. We played by all the rules and should have been done a couple of months ago.

## COUNCIL COMMENTS:

Councilman Kivell stated that he had hoped that we would have better luck with movies in the park. We have only had 2 of the 5 events. Weather is always a threat. He further stated that although he likes the idea of entertaining children, these movies cannot start until late. He would like to poll the other communities that we went in with to see what types of movies they have been presenting. He further discussed working with the Recreation Department. Councilman Morelli stated that he would like to keep it for the kids and possibly look at other scheduling alternatives. Discussion was held on adult language, etc. being a concern.

Mayor Wallace stated that he would like to commend the City Clerk for the planting around City Hall. City Clerk/Treasurer Zemke stated that she would also like to commend Maggie Kurtzweil for the work she had done as well.
