## OLD BUSINESS:

### 1. Second Reading: Amendment to Fire Prevention and Protection Ordinance

Chief Kennedy stated that we have amended the language since the last meeting after feedback from Council and the public. We are currently under the 2006 International Fire Code. We were able to determine that the 2009 Fire Code addresses the portable units and we were able to pull language from that code for one and two family dwellings. The other concern was the nuisance language, which we have removed but added objectionable or offensive because of smoke conditions. Outdoor wood furnaces language has also been changed to leave that within the confines of the building department.

Councilman Wedell stated that he was pleased with the changes and appreciates the diligence

## NEW BUSINESS:

### 2. GASB 54 Implementation

City Manager stated that at the last meeting Mr. Les Pulver was present to explain the changes to the GASB 54. This will make changes to our Capital Improvement and our Equipment Fund as well as the Land Acquisition Fund changing them from a Special Revenue Fund to a Capital Project Fund. It includes giving the City Manager the authority to assign fund balance. It is not giving the manager authority to spend money but simply assign money.

## CM 6-5-11 – GASB 54 IMPLEMENTATION

Motion by Morelli, supported by Wedell

To make the following changes:

Capital Improvement Fund – Change from a Special Revenue fund to a Capital Project fund. Commit the current fund balance of $2,119,249 and future changes to fund balance in this fund for future infrastructure improvements.

Equipment Fund – Change from a Special Revenue fund to a Capital Project fund. Commit the fund balance of $70,419 and future changes to fund balance in this fund for future equipment purchases.

Land Acquisition Fund – Change to Capital Project Fund. Commit the fund balance of $137,681 and future changes to fund balance in this fund for the purchase land.

Grant the City Manager the authority to assign fund balances that are not previously restricted or committed.

MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY

that this be the first reading of the rezoning request of Oakland 40 from IRO Industrial Research Office District to R-2 Single Family.

### 11. Discussion – South Lyon Woods Lawsuit

Attorney Lee stated that we have prevailed on all counts. The Court has denied the Plantiff's motion for summary disposition and approved our motion for summary disposition. As a result, there will be no refund of any kind. This will put an end to the litigation unless they file an appeal. He further stated that our utility ordinance says that we only go back three months when there is an error in the meter, whether it is the City's loss or the customer's loss. It has worked very well and makes sure that if people feel that there is an error, then they get it resolved so that we do not have things over our head for many years.

The tragic thing to their detriment is that the City agreed that they overpaid by $63,000. They claimed they over paid $107,000. The issue was that there was an overpayment beyond the three months. We agreed that we owe them $11,000 and we will be cutting a check back for that amount only after the 21 day appeal deadline.

Councilman Wedell asked if we would be reimbursed legal fees. Attorney Lee stated that we are not entitled to legal fees.
