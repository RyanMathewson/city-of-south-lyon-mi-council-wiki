## NEW BUSINESS:

### 1. Proposed Park Use Policy

Attorney Lee stated recently there was a decision made by the United States Supreme Court finding that a city ordinance banning hand guns from a city is unconstitutional. We have a statement in our park policy which states it is in violation of park policy to carry a weapon in our park. He stated he has spoken with Chief Collins about this and it would be nice if we leave it as is and hope for compliance, but it might cause certain second amendment advocates to come to our park and end up with lots of people demonstrating in our parks openly carrying guns and rifles. Councilman Wedell stated he would like to follow up on this issue, so we can be within State Law. Discussion was held regarding the different laws regarding weapons and concealed weapons. Attorney Lee suggested we could remove paragraph 3 in the park use policy or we could change the language. It was the concensus of Council that Attorney Lee change the language and discuss it again at the next meeting.

### 2. Sale of Bikes

Renwick stated we have been collecting many bikes that have not been claimed by the owners. He further stated we advertised for bids, and received two. He further stated he would like Council to accept the high bid by Clyde Hatfield for $653.00. Attorney Lee stated we have had these bikes unclaimed for at least 6 months.

## CM 7-4-10 MOTION TO APPROVE HIGH BID FOR BIKES

## Motion by Morelli, supported by Kivell

## MANAGER'S REPORT:

City Manager Murphy stated he would like to mention the signs the road commission has put at different roads of the county. The signs say Jeff Potter Highway. That is a very nice thing.

City Manager Murphy stated ARH Building returned the contract for the Bodnar building for some changes. He further stated the attorneys are working on that.

City Manager Murphy stated he has some new information on the DWRF. He has received information that everyone will receive 15% forgiveness and some will receive up to 40% based on how many points. If Council would like, he will gather the numbers for them to see. It was the consensus of Council for City Manager Murphy to gather the numbers for them to review at a later date.

## COUNCIL COMMENTS:

Councilman Selden stated channel 19 is down and was down all weekend. Mayor Wallace stated it was probably caused by the power outage. If he would have known, he would have taken care of it.

Councilman Kivell asked if the two properties that were going before the Boundary Commission is a still an ongoing issue. City Manager Murphy stated Lyon Township gave them the zoning they were wanting, and as far as he knows, it is no longer an issue.

Councilman Kivell stated he had a wonderful time the Downtown Saturday night. It was a great success again.

Councilman Kramer stated he was unable to attend, but his neighbors had a great time at the Downtown Saturday night, and would like them to happen more often. He has heard nothing but great things about the event.

Councilman Wedell stated the Board of Commissioners used their own money for the signs and no tax payer money was used. He further stated what a nice thing. After they are up for a while, the signs will be presented to his family.

Mayor Wallace stated that recently there have been many deaths of young people from suspected heroin overdoses. He would like young people to reconsider your drug use and seek help. Dying is a no return option and grieves all of your loved ones.
