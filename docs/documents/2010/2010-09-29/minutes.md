Attorney Lee stated it is his suggestion to Council to hold off on anything to do with the medical Marijuana issue. He further stated we currently have a moratorium and he suggests to leave it as it is, and after that is up, to re-issue another moratorium for another 180 days to see what is happening at that time with the other cities. Attorney Lee stated he will keep the Council updated on any new information regarding this issue. Some discussion was held regarding the current medical marijuana law.

### 2. Blight

Commissioner Weipert stated the planning commission feels there is a need for a registry of all the vacant homes in the City. Something that is on file so the City could contact the property owners when there is a problem with blight. City Manager Murphy stated there is a way for Julie the City Clerk to find out through the County who is the owner of the property. Councilman Kramer stated the problem is the ownership or the mortgage company changes hands so many times, it would be hard to know who owned it at that time. He further stated it would be hard to enforce it. Councilman Kramer stated it is an enforcement issue. Councilman Morelli stated we already have the ordinances on the books, but we aren't enforcing them. We don't have the manpower to do it at this point in time. Discussion was held regarding gaining access to the inside of the homes. Attorney Lee stated you would have to go through the courts to gain access to the inside. Discussion was held regarding having a registry of foreclosures.

Commissioner Weipert stated Kristen has been doing some research on what other municipalities include in their ordinances. They include some things that we don't, and the planning commission would like to add some to our ordinances. She further stated they are suggesting to Council we may need to add some additional language in the current ordinances. Councilman Kramer stated we have no one to enforce the blight issues right now. We don't have an ordinance enforcement officer. Discussion was held the regarding not having the manpower to do ordinance enforcement or the money to hire a code enforcement officer. Discussion was held regarding having a list of all the blight ordinances throughout the city code onto one list. Discussion was held regarding what is considered blight and what is just considered unsightly.
