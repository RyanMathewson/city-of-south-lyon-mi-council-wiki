## NEW BUSINESS:

### 1. Revision of City Fee Schedule

City Clerk/Treasurer Zemke stated that in January, Council adopted the fee schedule as one comprehensive document. There were a couple items that were somewhat vague. The first is the water shut-off/turn on fee. There has been some question whether each trip should be charged a fee, not simply one fee for turning off then back on. We did neglect to add in the plan review fee, therefore that has to be added back in. Finally, we have arrived with a suggested cost for the removal of snow and ice based on Council's recent adoption of changes to Chapter 82.

Mayor Wallace stated that if the City has to come out and shovel a sidewalk, the cost would be $87.34 per hour.

Councilman Morelli stated that normally when it snows, all of our DPW employees are out working overtime and asked why we did not use an overtime rate. ### 2. Request by the Kiwanis Club to use McHattie Park to sell Christmas Trees

Mr. Rich Sabota of the South Lyon Kiwanis Club stated that they are making their annual request to use the property next to the Depot for their annual tree sale. This sale raises the funds for their annual senior holiday dinner.

Mayor Wallace asked how many seniors are fed at that party. Mr. Sabota stated approximately 700.

Mr. Sabota stated that they recently changed their by-laws to meet twice per month rather than weekly. They are also looking to change their meeting dates from Mondays, which is the same night as Council meetings as well as all of the School Board and other governmental groups.

## CM 11-4-10 – USE OF MCHATTIE PARK

Motion by Selden, supported by Morelli

To allow the use of the Historical Village/Depot Grounds at the south end of McHattie Park by the Kiwanis Club of South Lyon to sell Christmas trees from November 22, 2010 to December 31, 2010

City Manager Murphy stated that he will be off work on Wednesday this week.

## COUNCIL COMMENTS:

Councilman Morelli stated that he would like to thank the Water Department for their help with a recent problem he had at his house. Bob Martin and his staff are extremely knowledgeable.

Councilman Wedell wished everyone a happy Thanksgiving.

Councilman Selden reminded everyone of the Cool Yule on Saturday, December 4th. He also discussed the food drive for Active Faith. He further wished everyone a happy and safe Thanksgiving.

Councilman Kivell reviewed some of the activities that will be taking place around town during the Cool Yule.

Mayor Wallace stated that he would like Council to start thinking about the goals and objectives for the City Manager so that we can put this on a future agenda for discussion.

Mayor Wallace stated that he would once again like us to start discussions with Lyon Township about a study on shared Police and Fire Services.

Mayor Wallace discussed the Veterans' Day event that took place 11:11 a.m. on 11/11. He stated that it was very well attended with over 100 people there.
