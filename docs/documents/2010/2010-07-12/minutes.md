1.  $ 2^{nd} $ Reading: Ordinance to Amend Section 62-64 of City Code of Ordinances.

Mayor Wallace noted that this deals with the proposed park use policy. The ability to take an animal into the park as follows:

a) The animal is tethered by leash and controlled by a person.

b) The person controlling the animal is in possession of items needed for collection, containment and disposal of animal feces.

### 5. ICMA-RC 457 Plan

Mayor Wallace stated that this is an employee plan. City Manager Murphy noted it is pension plan that the employees put money into. The City does not put anything in. This is supplemental to MERS. I was asked to consider letting employees take out loans against their plan. Currently it is not available.

<a id="p5"></a>
*[Page 5 of the original](https://www.southlyonmi.org/document_center/City%20Council/Agenda%20packet%20and%20Minutes/2010/minutes/20100812130439763.pdf?t=202108310733000#page=5)* (read by OCR; check the original)

I was concerned about added administrative work on Lori. It is set-up where the employee applies online and can pay the loan back through their checking account. It is virtually no work for the City to allow this to happen. The employee can receive one loan per year not to exceed five loans out at one time. If the employee defaults on the loan, the IRS is notified and they will be taxed heavily and will also have a penalty for early withdraw. The City would be notified about the default. Attorney Lee stated that the way they handle defaults, is the ICMA would send a 1099 to the defaulting person. Councilman Wedell stated that the 457 program is a pre-taxed deduction that an employee puts into a savings. It is one of the best options the government can give to their employees.

## CM 7-10-10- MOTION TO APPROVE THE RESOLTUION FOR THE ICMA RC LOAN OPTION

## Motion by Selden, supported by Kivell

WHEREAS, the Employer has employees rendering valuable services; and

WHEREAS, the Employer has established a retirement plan (the “Plan”) for such employees which serves the interest of the Employer by enabling it to provide reasonable retirement security for its employees, by providing increased flexibility in its personnel management system, and by assisting in the attraction and retention of competent personnel; and

WHEREAS, the Employer has determined that permitting participants in the retirement plan to take loans from the Plan will serve these objectives:

### 7. On street parking discussion

Mayor Wallace noted that this is regarding the ordinance we have for winter rules. I have to ask Council to consider this – eliminating the rule due to times and changes in society. There are so many people living at home still which makes for more cars. It looks bad when they have to park in their front lawns to get off the road every night. Pitfalls to this are that sometimes there are cars that are parked in the street during a normal snow fall when they are out trying to plow. If a snow emergency was called that means everyone is off the streets and that way they would be cleaned. Overall during a

bigger snow fall, it would be nice to have the streets open to clean them. We could set up some parameters for people to follow such as having the news declare we are under a snow emergency.

Councilman Morelli noted he is against changing the ordinance. He could consider moving the dates in 2 weeks on both sides.

Councilman Kramer would also like to see changes in the ordinance.

Councilman Wedell looked into this topic a year ago. The rule we have now is the best. If changed, it could be a monumental mess with snow piled up. If it is working, don't fix it.

Mayor Wallace noted that it is broke because society has changed.

Council Member Kopkowski noted that it all comes down to enforcement whichever way we go.

## MANAGERS REPORT

Julie is having surgery on her heel on Friday, July 16, 2010. She has to be off her feet for 2 months after the surgery. The "lumber" hardware store is supposed to be open August 1, 2010.

Last meeting it sounded as if I blamed an employee for something and threw her under the bus so to speak. Absolutely not. My intent was to only notify you that I messed up, not the employee. I just wanted to clarify that.

The Engineering RQ – I mentioned I wanted to put that out for bid. I would choose whatever qualified engineering firms that could do the work. Request for their qualifications and then have them put their cost in a sealed envelope and set it aside. Go through and narrow it down to approx. 5 and then interview those. Narrow it down from there to say 3. Once you have those 3, interview them again and after that interview, open the cost sheets and look at them. Rank them accordingly. Then bring the ranking to the Council for the Council's approval. Through the different processes, you can bring up items such as do you write the grants as part of your services for us? Questions like that would all count. I wanted to make clear to you what that process would be like.

I had a Workman Compensation meeting and for the tenth year in a row we are below one. The last 2-3 years we were at about .74. Average is .1. By having a .74 we're saving about $20,000.00 to $25,000.00 on our policy. All the departments and employees are doing a wonderful job at safety.
