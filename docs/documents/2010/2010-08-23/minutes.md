### 5. Temporary suspension of Noise Ordinance

City Manager Murphy stated the Pumpkinfest Committee is requesting a suspension of the noise ordinance for the downtown so the band can play until 11 p.m. Discussion was held regarding this only applying to the downtown where the band will be playing.

## CM 8-8-10 APPROVE SUSPENSIONS OF NOISE ORDINANCE

Motion by Selden, supported by Kramer

WHEREAS, the South Lyon Downtown Development Authority has applied for a special permit from the City of South Lyon to allow a band to play at the Pumpkin Festival; and

WHEREAS, South Lyon City Code 42-62(9) specifically addresses the playing of musical instruments and states as follows:

Each of the following acts is declared unlawful and prohibited, but this enumeration shall not be deemed to be exclusive, namely:

(9) Radio, television and musical instruments. The playing of any radio, television set, phonograph, or any musical instrument in such a manner or with such volume, particularly during the hours between 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. or at any time or place so as to annoy or disturb the quiet, comfort, or repose of persons in any office or in any dwelling, hotel, or other type of residence, or of any persons in the vicinity; and

WHEREAS, South Lyon Downtown Development Authority has requested that the band be permitted to play until 11:00 pm; and

WHEREAS, the Pumpkin festival is a Celebration of the City of South Lyon for its citizens, and the event attracts other visitors to the area, enhances its public image, promotes economic development and fosters a sense of civic pride among its citizens; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of South Lyon considers this request to be in the best interests of the City of South Lyon and in the interests of the citizens' safety, health and welfare for the above-stated reasons;

NOW THEREFORE, the City Council for the City of South Lyon does hereby grant permission to Downtown Development Authority to allow the band Global Village to play until 11:00 pm on Saturday, October 3, 2009 in the areas specifically requested by the Downtown Development Authority.

### 9. Medical Marijuana Moratorium

Pam Wiepert of 135 N. Warren stated in April of this year the City Manager and City Attorney asked the Planning Commission to look at making changes to the Zoning Ordinance to incorporate medical marijuana. At that time, the attorney was not sure if they could ban it all together. Over the last few months, there has been a lot of information in the papers. Communities are dealing with these in different ways. They were either banning the dispensaries or they were incorporating them into the Zoning Ordinance. Our attorney believes in order for us to abide by the law, we cannot ban them, but rather regulate them in our Zoning Ordinance. We have to figure out what business district we want them in. Right now because we do not have any rules, they could put it wherever they want. The Planning Commission is recommending a moratorium on medical marijuana for 90 days so we can study the issue and talk with Council about what their wishes are. She further stated the resolution created by the City Attorney states a 6 month moratorium, and they feel that would be better.

Attorney Lee stated this is a constitutional amendment and we are obligated. We have all taken an oath to uphold the constitution whether we like it or not. We have to follow the laws that are passed. He further stated we can regulate it, but we cannot ban it. Attorney Lee stated the cities that have banned it are already being challenged in the courts and it will likely get costly for them.

Discussion was held regarding there not being any guidelines for the distribution and cultivation of medical marijuana. Councilman Wedell stated he thinks the 6 month moratorium is a perfect amount of time, but if we completed the work before the 6 months is up, can we remove the moratorium. Attorney Lee stated we can lift the moratorium or extend it if needed.

Councilman Selden stated we need more information on who can and who cannot apply to have a marijuana dispensary.

Council Member Kopkowski stated she has done her homework on this. She further stated that there are people growing marijuana in their homes, and we need to grandfather these people in. The law speaks of the caregiver and the patient. It doesn't cover the dispensing. Attorney Lee stated there is the criminal aspect of marijuana use and growing. The people who are growing for medical use and using, are doing so under their constitutional right to not be charged with a crime. We are talking about the City issuing a license to be a dispensary. Attorney Lee stated if we grandfather people in, we are then issuing them a license. They could then do whatever they want, and we could not control it. We are just saying this City administration shall not issue a license or permit for the sale or dispensary of marijuana for the next 6 months. Councilman Kramer stated that is the problem with the law as it stands right now; there are too many different interpretations of the law that was passed by the voters. That is why so many communities are struggling with this issue. He further stated he agrees with the moratorium language and he is not worried about the individual users because they are protected by the administrative rules.

*[Page 6 of the original](https://www.southlyonmi.org/document_center/City%20Council/Agenda%20packet%20and%20Minutes/2010/minutes/08-23-10CityCouncilMinutes.pdf?t=202108310733000#page=6)* (read by OCR; check the original)

Councilman Selden stated he wants to make this very clear that we are talking about medical marijuana and we are talking about people who have permits to use it. We are not loosening up on the illegal use of marijuana. Another problem is that there are clubs forming in other areas. The state legislature has a lot of work to do.

Councilman Kivell asked what the Planning Commission's objective is. Pam Weipert stated the Planning Commission's objective is to regulate where the dispensaries will be located.

Council Member Kopkowski stated you cannot tell people how to do it, but you can tell them where they can do it.

Mayor Wallace stated we need to get the moratorium passed so we can get a sense of direction before something happens and it gets out of control.

Council Member Kopkowski is opposed to the language of the moratorium.

Discussion was held regarding the language of the moratorium.

## CM 8-12-10 APPROVAL OF MEDICAL MARIJUANA MORATORIUM

## Motion by Kramer, supported by Wedell

WHEREAS, the cultivation sale or dispensation of medical marihuana was not envisioned when the current Zoning Ordinance was adopted in 1995 and is not regulated in any way; and

WHEREAS, allowing the cultivation, sale or dispensation of medical marijuana prior to the amendment of the Zoning Ordinance would be contrary to the goals of the City Master Plan; and

WHEREAS, the City desires to ascertain the best and safest path to compliance with the Michigan Medical Marihuana Act, P.A. 2008, Initiated Law, MCL 333.26423(d) in order to protect the public health, safety, and welfare; and

WHEREAS, the City Council determines that it is desirable to immediately forbid the cultivation, sale or dispensation of medical marijuana until an amendment to the Zoning Ordinance (Chapter 102 of the City Code) becomes effective; and

WHEREAS, Chapter 2, § 2.1 (b)(1) of the City Charter authorizes the City Council to enact resolutions immediately necessary for public health, peace, or safety to be adopted at the session at which first introduced, and the City Council determines that the following resolution satisfies that requirement.

RESOLVED, that the following resolution – to impose a moratorium on the issuance of permits or licenses for the cultivation, sale or dispensation of medical marihuana for a period of six (6) months from the effective date of this resolution, or until the effective date of the adoption of proper text amendments to the Zoning Ordinance (Chapter 102 of the City Code) and any necessary licensing requirements, whichever occurs first – is hereby adopted and given immediate effect.
