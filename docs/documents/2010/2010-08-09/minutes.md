### 5. DWRF

City Manager Murphy stated if we become a green project reserve, we can get up to 40% forgiveness on the DWRF loan. The information he has received from Jesse VanDeCreek indicates there would be a minimum of 15%, and we are getting an extra hundred points, but we have to submit a request for them to look at it again. He further stated by the end of August we will be able to see where we place with the other communities that will be receiving this. We are still on the list. Jesse VanDeCreek has indicated that it would take no more than a day for his engineers to pull the information together and could do it by August 11th. The cost would be less than a thousand dollars. City Manager Murphy stated the system is 80 years old and it will need to be replaced sooner or later.

Councilman Wedell stated he feels that is cheap enough for us to keep our name in the queue. He further stated we should not ignore the money and the unheard of interest rate of 2½%. This is not only for now, but for the City long into the future.
