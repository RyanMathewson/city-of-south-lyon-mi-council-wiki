**PUBLIC COMMENT:**

Debra Green of 408 Washington stated she does not have a car, and her only form of transportation is her wheel
chair. She further stated it is hard to get around in the winter time because the sidewalks are not always cleared,
even 24 hours after the snowfall. Ms. Green stated it is very frustrating and difficult to get to the stores for her
groceries.

Lori Green of 404 Washington stated she corresponded with Mayor Wallace regarding the snow on the sidewalks
last year, but it is still a problem. She further stated it is a very dangerous situation because sometimes they even
have to go onto the road to get around the snowy sidewalks.

Councilman Kivell stated it will probably be more of an issue this year because we do not have the Building
Inspector to keep an eye on the situation. It will have to fall on someone else’s shoulders.

Mayor Wallace stated that maybe the City Manager and the DPW could monitor the situation. City Manger
Murphy stated we may need to change the ordinance to allow us to handle the snow after the 24 hours and charge
the property owner. Mayor Wallace stated something must be done.

Councilman Kivell stated we had a court issue with a property owner regarding this issue last year and we lost
because it was demonstrated they were in violation, but the way the language in our ordinance is we lost. We
need to adjust the language in our ordinance so there is metrics assigned to it, rather than something that is
judgment driven.

Mayor Wallace stated as the Mayor, he promises we will do something to take care of this.

Councilmember Kopkowski stated we should look into this at the next meeting.

Jim Herman of 1107 Appaloosa stated he is the South Lyons Football Booster President. They are interested in
being incorporated as a nonprofit organization, and would like Council to approve it. He further stated they do
drawings and other things to raise money for equipment that the schools can’t afford to do. We stated they have
their bylaws and their article of incorporation. Mayor Wallace stated this should be added to the next agenda.

Mr. Herman stated they would also like to change the homecoming parade route, time and the day of the parade.
He stated it will benefit the community because more people will be able to attend. It is hard for everyone to be
here for the parade on a Friday at 4 p.m. It is even difficult for the football team to attend because they have to be
at the school to get ready for the game by 5 p.m. Mr. Herman stated they are hoping to change the time to 6, 6:30,
or 7p.m. and changing the date to Thursday nights. He further stated they are asking the parade route to be
changed to start at Bartlett then go to 10 Mile, and then left onto Pontiac Trail to the first entrance of the high
school with a pep rally to follow, and maybe in the future a bon fire, but the bon fire thing is for another time to
discuss.
Councilman Kivell stated changing the time is a great idea. Changing the time to 6:00 will allow a lot more
people to attend. There should not be too much rush hour traffic at that time. Other communities do this the same
way, and they seem to survive it. The pep rally after the parade will get people wound up for the game the next
night.

Chief Collins stated the ordinance reads they need to submit the application to the Police Department and that has
not been done, so he has not had a chance to look it over. Chief Collins stated it will be cutting it close if they
want to change the route from what it has been the last 12 years. He further stated he has some major concerns

4. RAW SEWAGE PUMP REPLACEMENT

Superintendent Martin stated the pumps are the heartbeat of the plant. He further stated they have been worked
on over and over again. The pumps are 31 years old. We can fix the old pumps for $10,000 a piece or we can
purchase new ones for $23,970.00. Martin stated the old pumps will be sold for scrap metal. They will not be of
any use. He further stated this would be paid for from the budget by moving funds between line items. Martin
stated this would mean 4 out of our 5 pumps will be new. The technology has changed and they should last a
very long time.

CM 9-8-10 MOTION TO APPROVE THE PUMP REPLACEMENT

Motion by Wedell, supported by Selden

Motion to approve the requested purchase of two new raw sewage pumps in the amount of
$23,970

VOTE: MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY
