## NEW BUSINESS:

### 1. Jennifer Foley-9 Mile School Crossing

Jennifer Foley of 61351 Dean Drive stated she is here representing herself along with many other parents that are concerned about the 9 mile school crossing. They are hoping something can be done before someone gets hurt. She further stated they have a petition with over 145 signatures requesting something be done. Ms. Foley stated 9 Mile has changed a lot over the years. We have asked for help for the last 7 years. The yellow flashing lights do not help. She further stated that she herself was almost struck by a car passing another car on the right. Ms Foley stated she spoke with a police officer at the crossing, and he told her he would like to have gotten out and stopped traffic so the kids could cross. The parents are very concerned that one of our children could be hurt or killed.

Councilman Wedell stated before the meeting, Chief Collins handed out a report and also a Traffic Improvement Association Traffic Engineer survey of the same area. Based on that survey, it is recommended that a crossing guard be placed at 9 Mile and Brookdale. He further stated based on that study he would like Council to support the recommendation.

Councilman Kramer stated the Chief did a lot of work getting this traffic engineer and there are also some other suggestions regarding speed limit signs. Chief Collins stated that would clarify the drivers obligations at the crossing. We could purchase signs to put adjacent to the traffic lanes that say Stop for Pedestrians in the Cross Walk. He further stated he has a lot of confidence in the recommendations of the engineer.

Councilmember Kopkowski stated she never wanted the speed limit to change from 25 to 35. She further stated that Ms. Foley stated she had a conversation with a police officer that wished he could help. She asked why he could not. Chief Collins stated he has not spoken with the officer of that conversation, but he was supposed to observe and give tickets to the people flagrantly breaking the traffic laws.

Discussion was held regarding the use of police officers for the cross walks in the morning and afternoon.

Chief Collins stated if a crossing guard is approved tonight, he could have someone there next week.

Cynthia Kivell of 235 W Lake stated she has many years of experience being a crossing guard. She further stated it is the crossing guard's duty to wait for a gap in traffic then stop traffic for the children to cross. With her past experience she stated the crossing guard at Nine Mile will have a tough time finding a gap for the children to cross. She further stated something needs to be done to bring attention to the school crossing.

### 3. Homecoming Parade - South Lyon Football Boosters

Jim Herman of 1107 Appaloosa stated he is the President of the South Lyon Football Boosters and he is asking Council to approve the date and time of the homecoming parade.

Justin Allen of 12004 Pembrooke Circle stated he is the on the South Lyon School Council and they have been working on this and are really excited about it. It will be great for the community.

Councilman Kramer stated that at the previous meeting it was discussed to have the parade on a Thursday, but that is not happening this year. Mr. Allen wanted it on Thursday so it would be closer to the game which is on Friday. They could not move the Panther's games from Thursday to Wednesday, so we are requesting to have it on Wednesday. Councilman Kramer stated he thinks the Student Council, the Boosters and the City should be involved in deciding the route so everyone is on the same page. He further stated the original route submitted was unacceptable to the police and the fire department.

Councilman Kivell stated they may not be legitimate safety concerns because there are solutions for them and if you mitigate the safety concerns then there are no longer safety concerns that disqualify that route for being vital.

Councilmember Kopkowski stated she thinks it is a great idea to have the parade come through town, but she thinks it came together too fast, and there were too many people involved and not enough people sitting down together to work on it. Maybe it will be possible in the future. She further stated there are still a lot of safety concerns.

Devon Petrica of 434 Dorothy street stated they have submitted an application and asked if there was some expression of contempt in regards to what we have decided the route will be. Councilmember Kopkowski

### 5. Sidewalk Snow Removal Discussion

Attorney Lee stated we cannot do the snow ordinance the same as the weed ordinance. There is no ordinance to charge for the removal of snow. We can however amend the ordinance so the City can step in after 24 hours after the snow has fallen, and have the City remove it. We could then issue a ticket and ask the District Court Judge upon a conviction of a civil infraction, to repose the cost as part of the fine. He has adjusted the proposed ordinance to take care of that.

Councilman Kivell stated the fact that the trigger is 3 inches, clearly does not address why this was brought to our attention. If we have over an inch of snow on the sidewalks, it will still be hard for the elderly and the
