### 3. First Reading: Ordinance Amending Section 58-77- Begging

Attorney Wilhelm stated this is a proposed amendment to amend our current Begging Ordinance. The State of Michigan has struck down a Michigan Statute that is similar to our current ordinance. The ACLU has contacted us regarding this ordinance to which we responded that we don't enforce it. The amended ordinance will repeal the existing language and adopt new language that pertains to aggressive begging, such as touching someone, repeatedly asking, threatening, or following someone.
