## NEW BUSINESS

1. Dedication of Colonial Acres utilities, review current status and allow representatives to come before Council to address this topic

Mayor Wallace stated Colonial Acres gave a presentation of their hard work and a packet of information from their attorney and this has been worked on for many years. He further stated he feels this should be reviewed by our engineers and attorney again, and bring this back on the agenda for the next meeting. He has not conversed with Council, but he feels it behooves us to look over all of the paperwork again and if anything is needed it can be resolved in the two weeks before the next meeting. Councilman Ryzyi stated this has been going on for many years, and it is a very important issue to our community and it needs to be addressed. He stated he would like to hear from the representative from Colonial Acres. He further stated this topic has been covered extensively and his preference is in some way this be addressed tonight. Councilman Kivell stated on March 14, 2011 there was a policy made to fulfill this very thing. He stated there are specific rules and benchmarks, and rules that are required for all of the private developments in the City that want to dedicate their utilities to the City. He further stated we just received the required as-builts last week and our attorney and engineers need to review them. Councilman Kivell stated Colonial Acres has been around for 40 years and it makes good sense for the community and the taxpayers for the City to have a good idea of what the condition of the pipes are underground. He stated this has been going on a long time, but not because of the City's resistance. Discussion was held regarding the as-builts. Councilman Kramer stated this has been an on-going issue since he ran for Council. He further stated the easements have now been filed with the County, and the biggest concern is the depth of some of the sewer lines. He is for this 100% and he wants this done, but he would also like to wait the two weeks for our attorney and engineers to review the paperwork that was recently turned over to the City. Councilman Ryzyi stated originally the City thought Colonial Acres

Councilman Wedell stated he is pleased to see the information we now have and it is more information than we have ever had on this issue, but he agrees our attorney and engineers need to assess everything.

Maggie Kurtzweil a business owner at 417 S Lafayette and attorney for Colonial Acres Phase 5 gave a presentation regarding the utilities at Colonial Acres Phase 5. She stated this is not any one person's agenda, this is about good public policy. She stated it is good public policy for local governments to control the utilities. She stated there was clear intent from the beginning for all utilities in Colonial Acres Phase 5 to be dedicated to the City. Colonial Acres Phases 1-3 were dedicated to the City in 1981. The sitting Council at that time understood the importance of public owned utilities. The previous superintendent for the DPW and Water Department Linden Beebe stated it was always the intent for the City to accept the utilities in Colonial Acres. Discussion was held regarding the size of the easements. Ms. Kurtzweil stated there were quick claim deeds filed in 1987 for sewer easements from Colonial Acres to the City. She further stated she was unable to find a resolution that Council accepted the easements, because the minute book was missing. She further stated there was a break down between the City and the developer. A representative from the surveyor firm Colonial Acres used stated they prepared the drawings by the field measurements and some as-built, but they did have pieces of the as-built for Phase 5. Discussion was held regarding the drawings that were eventually found at the City, marked as field measurements. Councilman Ryzyi stated Colonial Acres has been told for 5 years that they need to supply documents and the City already had the drawings, and now we have plenty of documents and would like to move forward with accepting the utilities and easements.
