2) Presentation by Landmarc Development seeking feedback on possible request to rezone West End Park
from I-1 industrial to multi-family residential.
3) Presentation by Parks and Recreation Commission requesting action by the Council for expenditures
related to updating plan for Volunteer Park Development
4) Consider approval of first reading of Dangerous Building Ordinance
5) Consider approval of first reading of Ordinance to adopt the 2012 International Property Maintenance Code
6) Consider Dorothy Street Sanitary Sewer Relocation Proposal for Professional Engineering Services
7) Consider approval of repair for Sanitary Sewer line along Pontiac Trail near the Water’s Edge Complex to
fix sewer line offset and existing sink hole conditions.
8) Consider resolution asking for repeal of the new language in Section 57, subsection (3) of PA 269 of 2016.
9) Approval for City Manager to attend the MLGMA Winter Institute.
