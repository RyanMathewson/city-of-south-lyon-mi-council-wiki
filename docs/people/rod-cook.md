## Notes on the dates

- Rod (Rodney) Cook, long-time manager. Retired effective 2008-03-11 and served as Interim City Manager until David Murphy started 2008-06-18 (minutes 2008-02-25, 2008-05-27); called Retired City Manager Cook on 2008-06-23. Start not found.
- Appointed to a vacant seat on 2011-08-22 (about five meetings, until the Nov 2011 election). His motions appear in the 2011 minutes as Cook.
- Interim agreement approved 2013-11-13 (after Murphy was dismissed about 2013-10-23); thanked and succeeded by Ladner at the 2014-06-09 meeting. The Also-present line still says City Manager Cook on 2014-07-14, which looks like text copied forward.
