# About this wiki

> Unofficial. This page was generated by software from the city's published documents and the meeting video. Transcripts and Markdown conversions are machine-made and can contain errors; the city's own minutes and the video are the record.

## What this is

A public record aid for the City of South Lyon, Michigan council and city staff. It is not run by the city, and it does not cover any township government.

## How it is made

- **Transcripts:** audio from the city's YouTube channel is transcribed by a speech-to-text model, and speakers are separated and named by comparing voices with known officials. Errors in words and in who is speaking are expected.
- **Documents:** agendas, packets and minutes are downloaded from the city's site and converted to Markdown. Scanned pages are read by OCR and marked as such. Each page records the source URL, a SHA-256 of the original file and the retrieval date, and links to the matching page of the original PDF.
- **Officials:** dates in office come from city documents, audits, news reports and archived copies of the city site. Each date lists its source and whether it is documented, inferred or unknown.
- **No text on this site is written by an AI model.** Pages are filled in from the sources by templates.

## Which copy is the record

The city's own minutes and the meeting video. When this wiki and those differ, they are right.

## Corrections

To report a mistake, or to ask for something to be removed (for example a home address or another personal detail), [open an issue](https://github.com/RyanMathewson/city-of-south-lyon-mi-council-wiki/issues). Give the meeting date and, for a transcript, the time shown on the paragraph. Please do not repeat the personal information in the issue, because issues are public; describe where it is instead.
